## The jab, in short

The jab is the straight punch you throw with your front hand. It is the fastest, longest and safest punch you
have, which is why coaches say boxing starts and ends with it. You use it to measure distance, to interrupt the
other person's rhythm, to keep them at the end of your reach, and to open the door for your bigger punches.

## How to throw it

1. Start in your stance: hands up, chin down, elbows in, weight balanced.
2. Fire the front hand straight at the target, no wind-up. The first movement is the fist going forward.
3. Turn the fist palm-down in the last few centimetres and let the shoulder rise to cover your chin.
4. Breathe out sharply on impact. Stay loose on the way out, tighten the fist only when it lands.
5. Snap it back on the same line, straight to your guard. The return is as fast as the punch.
6. Your back hand does not move. It stays glued to your chin the whole time.
7. If the target is out of reach, step in with the front foot as the punch goes out, pushing off the back foot.

## The three mistakes everyone makes

- Pulling the hand back before punching. It tells the other person what is coming. Fix: think "forward first".
- Dropping the hand on the way back. That is when you get hit. Fix: finish every jab in your guard position.
- Reaching with the body. Leaning forward puts your chin in range. Fix: if you cannot reach, step, do not lean.

## Variations you will meet

Double jab (two in a row, the second usually lands), jab to the body (bend the knees, not the back), step jab
(punch and step together to close distance), up-jab (from a low hand against a taller person), and the power jab
(more hip and step behind it, slower, used sparingly).

## How to practise it

- Mirror, three minutes: throw slow jabs and watch for the wind-up and the drop. Speed comes later.
- Heavy bag, three minutes: focus only on the snap back. Punch, guard, punch, guard.
- Double-end bag: timing and accuracy. Hit it, slip its return, hit it again.
- Partner range drill: they hold a glove at their chin height and move; you touch it and return to guard.
- Shadow boxing: one round of jab and step only, forward, back, left, right.

## Check yourself

1. What moves first when you jab, the fist or the shoulder?
2. Where is your back hand while the jab is out?
3. You cannot reach the target. Do you lean or step?

Answers: the fist; on your chin; step.
