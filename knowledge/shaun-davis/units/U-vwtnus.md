## Description

If people only work because you are watching, you are not getting their best. Shaun's aim is a team whose work ethic is the same whether he is in the room or not, because everyone is heading in the same direction. That comes from care and shared goals, not from control.

## Details

- The warning sign: people are nice to your face and do things while you are there, but not when you are gone; when the cat's away the mice will play
- Why it happens: you are directing and calling the shots with no care
- The goal: get the best out of the team whether you are there or not
- How: the work ethic is the same because you are all going in the same direction, each with a vested interest
