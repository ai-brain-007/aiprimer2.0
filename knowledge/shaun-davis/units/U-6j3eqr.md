## Description

Michael's first tenant came from shared housing with autistic children, and her relief is what made him scale hard. Shaun says this purpose is what keeps you going when a deal is not about the numbers. You change your own life and other people's at the same time.

## Details

- First tenant: a mother with autistic children who had been sharing a kitchen in shared accommodation; her relief is what ignited the fire to scale
- Family of six moving into a four-bed: the kids ran in arguing over bedrooms instead of worrying about the next meal
- Michael grew up in temporary accommodation himself after his family was evicted and made homeless when the landlord fell behind on the mortgage; he does not want children to go through it
- Shaun: the purpose gives you momentum and drive to keep helping people
