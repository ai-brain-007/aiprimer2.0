## Description

The author's community grew out of a hard year. His rent-to-rent business started in 2018, tenants stopped paying in 2020, he pivoted into social housing and, in lockdown 2021, polled Instagram about a paid community. Fifteen votes became sixty weekly members, then a partnership with Luigi and a business that has trained hundreds of students.

## Details

- 2018: business started. 2020: tenants not paying during COVID; pivoted into social housing.
- 2021: started an online community during lockdown, when everyone was home and used to Zoom. An Instagram poll for a paid monthly subscription got 15 votes; he had expected three.
- Grew to around 60 active members with weekly Zooms, a WhatsApp community and special guests sharing their story plus a Q&A.
- Nearly closed it when the core social housing business took off; Luigi, a former guest, offered to join after a podcast and the community continued.
- Since then: two sold-out mastermind retreats in Marbella; the hosts say every attendee of the first became financially free; close to 300 people trained and mentored, approaching 500 students on the training platform.
- Every deal in the community is personally checked by the two hosts before it is done.
- The hosts describe it as the largest social housing education company in the UK.

## Notes

Told by the hosts about their own business, so the numbers and the 'largest in the UK' claim are self-reported. The transcript also calls it the High Ticket Academy once.
