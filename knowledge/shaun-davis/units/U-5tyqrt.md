## Description

Contracts Finder is the government website that lists who has won public contracts. Shaun uses it to find the temporary and emergency accommodation companies that hold multi-million pound council contracts, because those companies need houses right now. He calls it the best way to find a deal: you are calling proven buyers, not random companies.

## Details

1. Open Contracts Finder, the government contracts website.
2. Type "temporary accommodation" into the search.
3. Filter to awarded contracts only. Untick every other status; you only want companies that have actually won.
4. Set the location: either a postcode with a 50 mile radius, or all locations. All locations showed around 9,000 awarded contracts; not all are relevant, so sift through them.
5. Open a contract. Ignore the closing date; it is irrelevant and puts a lot of people off. Look at the contract end date instead. An end date in 2027 means the winners are still looking for properties.
6. Scroll to the bottom: award date, contract date, value of contract and the suppliers it was awarded to. Example: a 6.8 million pound contract awarded in 2023 to eight suppliers.
7. Take each supplier's address, put it into Google and find their contact number.
8. Contact them first by phone, introduce yourself and find out what they are after, then follow up by email.
