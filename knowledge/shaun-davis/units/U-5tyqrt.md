## Description

Contracts Finder is the government website that lists public contracts and who won them. Shaun filters it for awarded supported living, temporary and emergency accommodation contracts, opens one and reads off every supplier: each is a provider with fresh funding who needs houses right now. He calls it the best way to find a deal, because you are calling proven buyers, not random companies.

## Details

1. Go to gov.uk/contractsfinder and click Start.
2. Untick 'early engagement' and 'future opportunity' so you only see awarded contracts. Untick every other status; you only want providers that have actually won.
3. Search a keyword: 'supported living', 'temporary accommodation' or 'emergency accommodation'.
4. Set the location by region or postcode (for example a postcode with a 50 mile radius), or leave it as all locations to see the whole country. All locations showed around 9,000 awarded contracts; not all are relevant, so sift through them. Be as specific as you like about the areas you want to work in.
5. Open a contract. Read the description so you understand what the contract is for and what you would be getting into.
6. Ignore the closing date; it is irrelevant and puts a lot of people off. Check the contract end date instead. A date still in the future (for example 2027) means the contract is active and the provider is likely to be scaling.
8. Copy and paste each supplier name into Google, find the company and its contact number, and get through to the person who can partner with you.
9. Contact them first by phone and introduce yourself, then follow up by email. When you contact them, reference the contract you found them through.
10. Find out whether they are looking to scale. If they are, offer your properties as the solution to their problem.

Contracts Finder is the government website that lists who has won public contracts. Shaun uses it to find the temporary and emergency accommodation companies that hold multi-million pound council contracts, because those companies need houses right now. He calls it the best way to find a deal: you are calling proven buyers, not random companies.

1. Open Contracts Finder, the government contracts website.
2. Type "temporary accommodation" into the search.
3. Filter to awarded contracts only. Untick every other status; you only want companies that have actually won.
4. Set the location: either a postcode with a 50 mile radius, or all locations. All locations showed around 9,000 awarded contracts; not all are relevant, so sift through them.
5. Open a contract. Ignore the closing date; it is irrelevant and puts a lot of people off. Look at the contract end date instead. An end date in 2027 means the winners are still looking for properties.
6. Scroll to the bottom: award date, contract date, value of contract and the suppliers it was awarded to. Example: a 6.8 million pound contract awarded in 2023 to eight suppliers.
7. Take each supplier's address, put it into Google and find their contact number.
8. Contact them first by phone, introduce yourself and find out what they are after, then follow up by email.
