## Description

A student had spent a year trying on his own. He was talking to councils but could not close. Within a month of joining the hosts' programme he had a council deal paying £9,400 up front and £600 a month, and three months later he was on four deals with £38,000 in incentives.

## Details

- Before: one year trying alone; good conversations with different councils but he did not know how to close them; no step-by-step guide.
- Within one month of joining: received £9,400 from one deal with a council, making £600 profit a month from it. The hosts then say it is now £900 a month.
- Within three months: on four deals, and paid £38,000 as an incentive.
- The hosts use it to illustrate lack of clarity causing procrastination, the ignorance tax and council incentives.

## Notes

The student's name is garbled in the transcript as "a deal". The monthly profit is first given as 600, then corrected to 900.
