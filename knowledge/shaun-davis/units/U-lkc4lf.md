## Description

Shaun always runs towards a fixed number, currently 1,000 rooms, and decides what comes next only when he gets there. Without an end in sight you run and run, burn out and lose momentum, because you no longer know what you are working towards. Set the big goal first, then plan the next one from there.

## Details

- He has always had a number in mind: 1,000 rooms. When he reaches it he will make an informed decision about what he wants to do next, including any exit.
- Why it matters: if you just keep running with no end, you can burn out and lose the momentum you have built, because there is nothing you are working towards.
- His method: always have big goals you are striving towards. Once you hit one, ask what the next plan is.
- The interviewer expects him to hit 1,000 within the next couple of months rather than years.
