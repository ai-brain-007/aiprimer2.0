## Description

When you meet a council, talk about how you will deliver and stay compliant, not how much money the plan makes. Cover safeguarding, out-of-hours calls and your written procedures and policies. Show them you will not cause them stress.

## Details

- Lead with: delivery, compliance, competence
- Cover: safeguarding; what happens if a tenant calls late at night; what procedures you have in place; what policies ensure the house is maintained to a high standard
- Make clear: you understand the responsibility and accountability, you are competent, you have everything in place and you will do whatever it takes
- Avoid: pitching numbers and acquisition plans; the council does not want a pitch
