## Description

Before this business Shaun got bored and changed jobs every two or three years. Seven years into social housing he is not bored at all, because the work changes and saves lives every day. His lesson: you need an actual mission and a goal to work towards, and purpose is what keeps you going.

## Details

- Pattern before: change organisation every two to three years out of boredom, asking what can I do next.
- Now: seven years in as of August, with more desire and ambition than ever, asking how to go bigger and help more people.
- Why: a huge mission, a huge purpose, making a difference every single day. Lives are being changed and saved.
- What he says he needs: an actual mission, an actual goal to work towards.
