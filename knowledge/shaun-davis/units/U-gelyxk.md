## Description

You take a property and lease it straight to the council, which pays you a guaranteed rent for several years. There is nothing to manage day to day, so you get paid wherever you are. Shaun calls this one of his favourite strategies for the financial security it gives.

## Details

- How it works: get a property and lease it directly to the council.
- Rent: guaranteed every single month, paid directly by the council.
- Term: Shaun says 3 to 5 years at first, and in his recap 3 to 6 years, sometimes even 7 or 10 years.
- Operations: zero. You can be anywhere in the world and still get paid consistently.
- Upfront incentive: depending on the scheme, the council sometimes pays 1, 2 or even 3 months rent upfront, then rents the property from you for the rest of the term.
- It is a government backed scheme, which is where Shaun gets his sense of financial security.
- Also known as back-to-back leasing: you lease the property straight back out to the council, with no operations in between.

## Notes

The transcript renders the abbreviation as 'L'; from context it is the abbreviation of local authority leasing. Lease lengths are given inconsistently in the chunk: 3 to 5 years at first, then 3 to 6 and up to 10.
