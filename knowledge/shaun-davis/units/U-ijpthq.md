## Description

Each housing officer in Shaun's business looks after a patch, a set of houses that are theirs to manage. They handle the maintenance and move tenants in and out of those houses. It gives every house one person who is responsible for it.

## Details

- Every housing officer has a patch: different houses that they manage
- They are responsible for all the maintenance on those houses
- They move people in and out of the properties
- Shaun visits the properties in a patch to look for concerns and speak to the tenants
