## Description

Do not walk in with a pitch. Sit down, learn what is worrying this landlord, then show how your service removes those worries. The author says this is the only way a close becomes frictionless.

## Details

1. Do not pitch aimlessly; the author calls that the fastest way to lose a deal
2. Sit down and understand the problems the landlord faces
3. Typical problems: uncertainty, the Renters' Rights Bill, periodic tenancies, the many rights tenants have in the UK
4. Show that your product and service is the solution: no voids, no daily maintenance, no tenants defaulting, no multiple tenants to manage
5. Only then will the sale and the close become frictionless
- Make sure the landlord has the utmost confidence in you

## Notes

The author also says you can make a landlord become motivated, but he does not explain how in this talk.
