## Description

Keep cash buffers. Cash flow is what keeps a business alive, and the speed at which money comes back to you decides how fast you can take the next deal. Being undercapitalised is one of the two reasons the author says people fail.

## Details

- Hold cash buffers
- Cash flow is the heart of any business
- Velocity of money matters: upfront incentives and deposits let you go again and again
- Being undercapitalised is a main cause of failure
