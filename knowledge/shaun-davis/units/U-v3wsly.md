## Description

A key retractor is a small device that screws into your existing key lock box so the key retracts instead of coming away. It stops contractors, cleaners and staff walking off with the key in their pocket and losing it. Shaun calls it a game changer and now fits one at every property.

## Details

The problem it fixes:
- Contractors, cleaners and even staff members take the key out of the lock box, it goes in their pocket, and it gets lost. Shaun calls this one of the biggest issues in properties and a nightmare.

The fix:
- Fit a key retractor. It screws into the existing lock box, and the key retracts rather than being carried away.
- Shaun now does this at all of his properties.
