## Description

A successful business needs people, so you have to lead. Shaun says you cannot lead well unless you are in the right place yourself: motivated and able to empower your team to do the job properly. The more he works on his own skills and mindset, the better leader he becomes.

## Details

- To run a successful business you need people; you cannot do it yourself
- To lead, you must work on yourself so that you are in the right place and motivated
- Only then can you effectively empower your team to do the job correctly
- His rule: the better he becomes in himself, his skills and his mindset, the better leader he can be
- He agrees with the interviewer's summary: you cannot pour from an empty cup
