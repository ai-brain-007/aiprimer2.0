## Description

Early on, the author did the refurb and bought the white goods himself. Now he negotiates for the landlord to provide them, so the day he gets the keys he can start filling rooms. In return he looks after everything and hands it back in the same condition.

## Details

1. In the negotiation, ask the landlord to include the refurb and the white goods in the package
2. Reason: once you have the property you can start straight away and get tenants in as quickly as possible
3. Your side of the bargain: look after the property and keep everything in working order
4. If the landlord wants the property back, they get all the goods they provided in the same condition

In this property the landlord replaced the worktops, cupboards, cooker and washing machine, painted the whole house, changed the fire alarm and fitted new carpets. Result: keys collected, sofas delivered, tenants moving in the following week.
