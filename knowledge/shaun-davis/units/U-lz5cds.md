## Description

You will take losses in business, and a lot of them. Sean's two-step reset: first look at your own part, because that is the only thing you can change, then ask what the loss is teaching you. The faster you extract the lesson, the faster you bounce back.

## Details

1. Look at your own part first. Do not point the blame at everyone else; that is not being accountable. If you blame the other person you cannot change anything, because you can only change what you do. Find where you went wrong, change it straight away and do better next time. That puts the outcome back in your control.
2. Ask: what is this teaching me? Not: why is this happening to me? Every L carries a lesson.
3. Extract the lesson quickly. The quicker you extract it, the quicker you bounce back.

Underlying idea: perspective is everything. When you change the way you look at things, things begin to change.
