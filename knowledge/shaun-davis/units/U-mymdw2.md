## Description

Shaun's closing point: building something is easy compared with keeping it. Anyone can have one or two lucky years. To build over decades, and leave something that outlives you, you need habits, the strength to stand firm and a bulletproof mentality.

## Details

- Building is one thing; anyone can build, and anyone can have a lucky year or two.
- Building in decades, something that lasts a very long time and may outlive you, is different.
- For that you have to build habits, stand strong and have a bulletproof mentality.
- The seven habits are the ones Shaun credits for getting him there.
