## Description

Shaun did not start by teaching. He learned, built a viable and profitable business, became credible in his niche, and only then built a community and started mentoring. The order matters: credibility comes from results, not from a course.

## Details

The order Shaun followed:
1. Learn and develop yourself first.
2. Build a viable, profitable business.
3. Reach the stage where you have enough experience to be credible in your industry and niche. For Shaun that niche is property and social housing only.
4. Build a community.
5. Start teaching and mentoring people.
