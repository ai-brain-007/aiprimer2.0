## Description

Tips from the hosts for anyone borrowing from or partnering with private investors. Pick people you genuinely like for long-term joint ventures, never borrow more than you can handle, and give yourself a safety buffer on the loan term so a slow project does not turn into a sleepless crisis.

## Details

- Long-term JV: make sure you actually like the person and get along, because you will be tied together for years.
- Do not overstretch on how much you borrow.
- Get the term right. If the project will take 12 or 24 months, do not sign a 6 or 12 month agreement. Having the money feels great on day one, but if the project is not finished when the money is due you are under immense stress.
- Build in a safety buffer: sign a longer term than you expect to need.
- Under-promise, over-deliver: sign 18 months, finish in 12, and tell the investor the good news that you can pay them back six months early. That builds your reputation, instead of approaching month 12 unable to sleep.
