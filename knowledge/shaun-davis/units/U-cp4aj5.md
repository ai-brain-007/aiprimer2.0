## Description

People ask Shaun how he stays so positive. His answer: he cannot afford not to. Negative thinking grows, spreads onto your team and lowers their performance, so as a leader your own energy is a business asset you have to protect.

## Details

- If Shaun starts thinking negatively, negative energy comes into his life and grows day by day.
- Taken a level deeper: a negative leader pushes negativity onto the team, which affects their performance, and soon the whole organisation is full of negativity.
- A team is only as good as its leader. Leaders must make sure their own energy is correct.
- That means working on yourself and filling your own cup so you can keep pouring for others.
