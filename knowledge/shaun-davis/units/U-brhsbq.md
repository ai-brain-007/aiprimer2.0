## Description

What a property needs before a council will place tenants in it as temporary accommodation. The bar is clean and well-maintained, not luxury. Check what the property already has, because many come with beds.

## Details

Minimum most councils expect:
- Beds
- Dining facilities: a dining table and chairs
- A sofa, in the living room or dining area

Standard: clean and well-maintained, not done up like a five-star hotel.

Supply:
- Most properties come unfurnished, but many come furnished or partly furnished.
- Most properties these days come with at least beds in the bedrooms.
