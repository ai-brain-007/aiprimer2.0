## Description

Shaun frames the whole business as problem solving. There is a housing crisis, the contract winners need lots of houses, and you get paid in proportion to how much of that problem you solve. If you keep asking who has a problem you can fix, the money follows.

## Details

- Entrepreneurship is creating value and solving problems.
- If you solve a problem you get money, in proportion to the value you create.
- The problem right now: a massive housing crisis. Providers have won government-backed contracts and need a lot of houses.
- Your job: supply those houses, and you are good to go.
