## Description

Asked what people with a few deals can do to grow, Shaun gives two tips: leverage and being proactive. Each has a concrete action behind it.

## Details

1. Leverage: get yourself a VA, and work with multiple councils rather than one, because things change quickly and you do not want all your eggs in one basket.
2. Be proactive: get from behind the laptop and the phone, get in front of people, visit where you need to, and build relationships. Property is a relationship game.

## Notes

Later in the conversation Shaun adds one more tip specific to temporary accommodation: councils change but the tenants never do, so ask the council where the tenants are going.
