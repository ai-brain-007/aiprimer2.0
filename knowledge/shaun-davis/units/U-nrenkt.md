## Description

Cold calling means selling to someone who does not know you or your product and was not thinking of buying. Shaun calls it the highest level of sales, because you must get the person to like you, listen and buy, all from nothing. Warm inbound leads are far easier since the person is already half interested.

## Details

Why cold calling is the hardest form of selling, in Shaun's words:
1. The person does not know who you are.
2. The person does not know what the product is.
3. You have to convince them to like you personally, listen to you and give you the time of day.
4. You then have to convince them to buy something they were not thinking about buying.

By contrast, with inbound leads the lead is already warm and half interested; your job is just to direct them to the sale and to the right product.

Shaun's experience: working for his father he did nothing but cold calling, going to different shops. He found it really hard but enjoyed it, and says it teaches you a lot about business.
