## Description

Rent to HMO is how Shaun started in property in 2018. You find landlords who struggle to rent rooms because the property is dated, lease it from them for three to five years, refurbish it and rent it out at a higher rate. The gap between the two rents, £700 to £1,000 a month per property, is your profit.

## Details

1. Find landlords who have a problem renting out their rooms, usually because the rooms are dated.
2. Show them the problem: the property is old and no one wants it, so they are struggling to rent it.
3. Lease the property from the landlord on a long-term rent, around 3 to 5 years.
4. Refurbish the property to make it better. Shaun's spend is given in the transcript as £45,000.
5. Because you have added value, put an increase on the rent.
6. Make between £700 and £1,000 a month on the property.

Context: Shaun discovered rent to rent through a course he says was not that good but taught him the fundamentals. He ran the strategy alongside his full-time job at BMW and says it worked very well in 2018.
