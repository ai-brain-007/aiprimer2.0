## Description

When a strategy fails, you change direction instead of quitting. Shaun learned this in COVID, when his serviced accommodation, HMO and student strategies collapsed and he had to choose between going back to a job or finding a new model. Being able to pivot is what keeps you in business.

## Details

- In COVID his existing strategies failed, so he had to adapt, pivot and change.
- The decision in 2020 was binary: scrap the business and go back to the 9-to-5, or come out swinging and find a new strategy.
- He chose to find a new strategy, which turned out to be social housing.
- He calls this a huge entrepreneurial lesson: you have to be able to pivot and adapt to thrive in business.
