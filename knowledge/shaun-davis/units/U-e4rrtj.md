## Description

Finding providers is not hard, but it does not happen by accident. Decide what you are going after, do the work daily and follow through. Sean puts success down to how badly you want it.

## Details

- Move with intention: know what you are looking for (provider first, then the property) and act on it
- It is not hard; it comes down to how bad you actually want it
- The right attitude, willingness to work and ambition are what let you change your life quickly
