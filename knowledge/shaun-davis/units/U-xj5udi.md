## Description

When things go well you learn very little. Shaun had great success before COVID and says he learned nothing from it. In the six months of failing and pivoting to social housing he learned more about himself than in years, and came out a different person.

## Details

- Success teaches little. Failure and pivots teach a lot.
- Before COVID: great success, no learning. During the pivot to social housing: six months with more lessons and growth than in years.
- When you get to the other side of a setback, you are a different person as a result.
