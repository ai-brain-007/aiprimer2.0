## Description

The video is built as four lessons for children: how money works, how money moves, how to create value, and a recap that turns them into rules to remember. Each lesson has a hands-on demonstration behind it.

## Details

1. How money works. Understand it, don't just spend it. Demonstrated with the cup of money, the three ways money comes in, and the seeds picture.
2. How money moves. Watch how money moves and get in the flow of it. Demonstrated with the journey of a £10 note and the three-person £10 game.
3. Value creation. Value perception is key: make people want to give you their money. Demonstrated with Rolex versus Casio and the pink lemonade stand.
4. Takeaway and recap. Don't spend all your seeds, grow them.
