## Description

Get one property closed, then grow quickly. The first deal is a springboard: you gain confidence and know-how, and you have proof of concept to show the next landlord, agent or council.

## Details

- Close your first property
- Snowball effect: more confidence and a better understanding of what you are doing
- Social proof: something tangible to show landlords, agents and councils, showing how you helped the landlord, the tenant and the council
- Use that proof to propel the next deals
