## Description

This is the version of rent-to-rent that really would be illegal, and the one critics picture. If you rent a house as yourself on a normal tenancy and sublet it, you break your contract. The fix is structural: your company signs a commercial lease that allows subletting.

## Details

- The mistake: signing an assured shorthold tenancy with the landlord as an individual and then subletting.
- Consequence: that would be illegal; it is what people mean when they say rent-to-rent is illegal.
- Fix: rent as a company, not as you, under a commercial lease agreement that permits subletting.
- The confusion is a lack of education: critics have read their own residential contract, which says you cannot sublet, and assume every contract says the same.
