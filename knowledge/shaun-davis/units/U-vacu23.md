## Description

Sean sorts his work into high-value tasks that move the business and low-value tasks that just consume time. Admin, research, sending emails and long calls to the council are low value. Your job is to spend your limited hours on the high-value ones and hand off the rest.

## Details

- Low-value tasks Sean names: admin, research, sending emails.
- In social housing specifically: being on the phone to the council, which can take 45 minutes to an hour.
- High-value tasks: the things that move the needle in the business.
- If you have a job or other daily commitments, your time is a limited resource, so the split matters even more.
- How Sean handles it: a virtual assistant takes the low-value tasks so his own time goes on high-value ones.
