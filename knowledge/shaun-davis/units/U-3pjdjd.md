## Description

Even though the nightly rate model has less compliance than other social housing models, you still carry a legal duty of care. Keep spreadsheets and compliance records for every issue so you can show the council what was reported and when.

## Details

- Keep track of your reporting: spreadsheets, compliance records, every issue logged
- Be ready for the council to ask: this was an issue, did you report it?
- You have a duty of care and a legal obligation to do things properly and record them
- This model has probably the least compliance among social housing models; others have higher requirements and more inspections, but your duty remains
- If it is a rent-to-rent, the landlord also wants the property kept to the best standard
