## Description

These two phrases sum up what Shaun has always wanted from work. Location freedom means you can be anywhere. Time freedom means you decide when you work. He wanted to build something from scratch that gave him both, and later added the aim of making money while he sleeps.

## Details

- Location freedom: not tied to a place.
- Time freedom: able to travel and step off the treadmill when you want.
- Shaun says these were the two elements missing from his life when he worked for others, and the reason he pursued building his own business.
- After the 2018 Miami trip he framed the same goal as being in a vehicle that makes money while he is sleeping.
- Caveat from Shaun: everyone dreams of this; the difference is who is willing to put the work in first.
