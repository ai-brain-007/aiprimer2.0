## Description

Being busy is not the same as being productive. Most people spend their time on things that do not move the business forward and call it work. Shaun's test is one question: does this move the needle? If not, it is procrastination in disguise.

## Details

- There is a difference between being productive and being busy. People spend a lot of time doing things that do not move the needle on their business.
- Being busy can be procrastination in disguise.
- As the CEO, work on high value tasks that move the needle, and outsource, delegate and automate the low value tasks (see let go to grow).
- The key is not how many hours you work, it is where you work.
- Hold yourself accountable to your productivity: do not just be busy, be productive.
