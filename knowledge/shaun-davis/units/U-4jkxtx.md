## Description

If you live in London and the property is in Liverpool, you do not travel to inspect it. Companies will do a full detailed inspection report for around £30 to £40. This is leverage applied to property management, and it is what makes far-away areas workable.

## Details

- Cost: about £30 to £40 per inspection
- Output: a full detailed inspection report
- Use case: a property far from where you live, for the regular inspections you must do
- Removes the main reason people stay local
