## Description

Read at least ten pages of a book every day, no exceptions. Ten pages is small enough that you will actually do it, and most days you will find yourself reading more. It is the speaker's daily, non-negotiable way of working on himself mentally.

## Details

- Daily and non-negotiable: read 10 pages of a book minimum.
- Why 10: it is not a lot, so it is nice and easy to keep up, which is what builds the habit.
- What happens next: you often find yourself reading 20, but 10 is the floor.
- Then go to the gym: reading is paired with physical training as the daily work on yourself.
