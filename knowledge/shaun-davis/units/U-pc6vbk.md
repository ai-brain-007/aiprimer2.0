## Description

In 2018 a family cruise from Miami was the first holiday Shaun could really afford. Back at his BMW desk, at 2:23pm on a quiet afternoon, he decided he did not want to do this any more. That moment turned holiday blues into a hard look at what he wanted from life.

## Details

- 2018: big family holiday to Miami and a Caribbean cruise, the first time he had money behind him on a trip.
- On return he sat at his desk at BMW at about 2:23pm, in a very quiet dealership, and told himself he did not want to do this any more.
- He calls it a life moment: he asked what he actually wanted from life.
- The answers: to travel whenever he wants, and to be in a vehicle that makes money while he sleeps.
- It was the trigger for taking personal development seriously and reading quotes, including the one about making money while you sleep.
