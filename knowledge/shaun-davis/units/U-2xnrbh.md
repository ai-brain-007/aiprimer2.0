## Description

Take out a home emergency cover policy on the property and register the tenant as the account holder. When something breaks, the tenant calls the cover company, not you, and they send someone out. The author calls this a clever way to systemise the business.

## Details

1. Take out a home emergency cover service on the property
2. Put the tenant down as the account holder
3. If the tenant has an issue they contact the cover company directly
4. The cover company goes out and resolves it
- Result: fewer calls to you and a more systemised operation
