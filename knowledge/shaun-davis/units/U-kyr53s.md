## Description

The contract you sign with the landlord, usually for three to five years. It gives you permission to sublet, which is what lets you lease the property on to a social housing provider. Without it, subletting is illegal, so the contract is not a formality.

## Details

- Term: usually three to five years.
- It gives you the right to control the asset and the permission to sublet.
- Subletting without this permission is illegal, so the correct contract is essential.
- With the correct contract in place you control the property as if you owned it, and can then lease it to the provider.

The transcript reads "you control the properties if you own it", most likely "as if you own it".
