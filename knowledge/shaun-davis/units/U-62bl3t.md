## Description

Your parents love you, but if they have never run a successful business their business advice will be wrong. Shaun's fix is simple: go to someone who has done exactly what you want to do. Want to be a property investor? Find a property investor, ask how they started and ask them to mentor you.

## Details

- The mistake: mistaking your loved ones for good advisers. If your parents are not entrepreneurs and have no successful business, do not go to them for business advice. You still love them; they will just give you the wrong advice.
- The fix:
  1. Identify someone who is where you want to be and speak to that person.
  2. Go to someone who has a successful business, ideally in your field. If you want to be a property investor, go and speak to a property investor.
  3. Ask them how they started and get them to mentor you.
  4. Find the network: social media, and the many business and property networking events. There is a networking event for your niche.
  5. Surround yourself with that group.
