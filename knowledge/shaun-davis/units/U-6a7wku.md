## Description

Agents often bristle when you approach them because they think you want their business. Show them the opposite: their agreement with the landlord stays as it is, they keep their 10 to 15 percent, and you do the tenant-finding and management. Same pay, much less work.

## Details

1. Reassure the agent you are not taking their business or ruining any existing agreement or contract with their landlord
2. Explain the arrangement: you manage the property and find the tenants; they still get paid their 10 to 15% and pass the rent on to the landlord
3. Frame it as: they get paid the same amount for doing much less work
4. Make their life easier: make decisions quickly and do what you say you will in the time you said
