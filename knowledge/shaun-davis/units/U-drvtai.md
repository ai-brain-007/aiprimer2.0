## Description

Before you chase a property you need one number: what the council pays per month minus what the landlord wants. Shaun does this in seconds on the phone and it decides which listings are worth a call. It also tells you how many properties you need for the income you want.

## Details

1. Take the council's nightly rate and multiply by 30. Three bed in Luton: £65 x 30 = £1,950 a month.
2. Search OpenRent and the agents for three and four beds around your target rent.
3. Profit = council income minus the rent you pay the landlord. £1,950 - £1,500 = £450 a month. A £1,450 property gives about £500.
4. Check who pays the bills first. In Luton the tenant pays utilities and the landlord does repairs, so the margin holds.
5. Set a ceiling and say it to agents: max £1,500 for a three bed.
6. Walk away from thin deals. A four bed at £1,600 gave only £70 profit.
7. Think in scale: £450 on ten properties, and the council needs 300.
