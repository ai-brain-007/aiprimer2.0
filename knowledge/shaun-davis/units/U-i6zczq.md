## Description

Shaun's father wanted him to stay and one day take over the family business. Shaun wanted his own path and the freedom to make his own decisions, so he left in 2014 to sell cars at Ford. He describes it as an itch he had to scratch, with the safety net that he could always come back.

## Details

- His father built a path for him and was gutted when he left; Shaun says he understands this now as a parent himself.
- Shaun's reasoning: in every organisation he felt limits and felt he needed to be the leader and decision maker.
- The easy route would have been to stay under his father's wing in a business that was going somewhere; it was not his vision.
- He had no car sales experience; he simply believed he could do it.
- He agreed with his father that if it did not work out he could come back, then left in 2014 for Ford.
