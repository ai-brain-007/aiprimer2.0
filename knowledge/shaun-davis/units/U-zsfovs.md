## Description

No business model is safe forever. Shaun's serviced accommodation business was wiped out overnight when COVID closed the travel platforms, weeks after he had quit his job. Being able to pivot and adapt fast is, in his view, one of the great entrepreneurial traits.

## Details

- In business, what works today may not work tomorrow. Expect it.
- When the model breaks, do not wait. Ask what you are going to do at this point, make a decision and pivot quickly.
- Being able to pivot and adapt is a great entrepreneurial trait. You need to understand these principles before you need them.
- Shaun's pivot: from serviced accommodation and HMOs to supported accommodation (see the COVID pivot example).
