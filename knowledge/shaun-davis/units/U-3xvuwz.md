## Description

Shaun says the standard private tenancy has changed since the Renters Rights Bill. The assured shorthold tenancy has been replaced by a periodic tenancy, which he refers to as APT. This matters because a periodic tenancy removes the landlord's fixed term.

## Details

- The assured shorthold tenancy, AST, has been changed to what Shaun calls APT, a periodic tenancy, since the Renters Rights Bill came out.
- The Bill brings many changes and more tenant rights. The main one, in his view, is that tenancies are now periodic.
- Shaun does not go into the full detail of the Bill in this video.

## Notes

The transcript renders the new term as 'APT, a short periodic tenancy'. The expansion may be a transcription error; treat the acronym with care.
