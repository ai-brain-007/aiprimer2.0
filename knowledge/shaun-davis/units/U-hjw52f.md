## Description

When you pitch a landlord a model they do not know, words are not enough. Michael shows his processes, his reports and the properties he already runs. Proof calms a landlord who is meeting you for the first time.

## Details

- Do not just describe your process; show it
- Things to show: your inspection routine, the inventory report the landlord signs off, a property you already run and the condition you keep it in
- Most important when the landlord is new to the strategy and to you
