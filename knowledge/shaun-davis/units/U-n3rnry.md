## Description

The council is a business and will lowball you to save money. Know the value of the service you are offering and hold your ground. Make sure enough is left in the budget to cover the landlord's maintenance, because that falls on you.

## Details

- Expect a low first offer; the council is trying to save the most money it can
- Know the service you provide and be clear about it
- Keep both sides in mind: the council and the landlord
- Full maintenance of the property usually sits with your company, so the rate must leave enough budget to fix anything that goes wrong
- The council knows the maintenance is on you and expects you to account for it
