## Description

Shaun treats his hours the way he treats his income: nothing gets spent on things that do not pay. On his one free day, if a task was not high value it simply did not get done by him. When your time is scarce, this rule decides what you touch and what you hand off.

## Details

- Be extremely disciplined with the time you have. Every minute is intentional.
- Ask of every task: is this high value? If not, it does not get done by you.
- Guard your time with the same seriousness you guard your revenue. In Shaun's words, that is the difference.
