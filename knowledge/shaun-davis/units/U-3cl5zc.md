## Description

A landlord does not have to be convinced by your argument first. They have to be convinced that you are convinced. Sean says that when people believe you believe in what you are saying, you come across with conviction, and passion and enthusiasm are what sell.

## Details

- People do not need to believe in what you are saying. People need to believe that you believe in what you are saying. Sean calls it a subtle difference.
- When they believe you believe, you come across with conviction: passionate, enthusiastic.
- Enthusiasm sells, passion sells. Getting that across is very important.
- This is something you have to work on yourself; it cannot be scripted for you.
