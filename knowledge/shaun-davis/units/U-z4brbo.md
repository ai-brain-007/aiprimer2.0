## Description

A prop the author uses with his son: a cup holding a small amount of money. It shows how little is actually there, how spending on things you do not need empties it, and that only three activities can fill it back up.

## Details

How the demonstration runs:
1. Give the child a bit of money in a cup and ask what they think of it. The point lands on its own: it is not a lot.
2. Explain where that money came from: a job, an investment or a business.
3. Ask what happens if it gets spent on things they do not need. The cup gets emptier and only a little is left.
4. Ask how to fill it up again. Only three things refill it: selling your time in a job, investing for passive income, or creating value with a product or service.
5. Close with the seeds picture: eat them (spend) and they are gone; plant them (invest) and they grow.
