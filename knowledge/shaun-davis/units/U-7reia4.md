## Description

The student in the video was a landscaper with no property knowledge. Cancer meant his body could no longer do the old work, so he tried the course, questioned every part of it, and followed it exactly. In under five months he reached 35 properties and about £13,000 a month; three weeks after filming, 46 deals and over £18,000 a month.

## Details

- Background: landscaping; knew nothing about property; a health scare with cancer meant he had to try something else.
- Joined the programme in September; 35 properties in just under five months, with another five agreed on the way to filming.
- Profit: about £13,000 a month; turnover about £154,000.
- Three weeks after filming: 46 deals, over £18,000 a month.
- Team of five. Reinvests everything.
- Questioned every sector of the course at first; now says it is proven to work if you follow the instructions and build good relationships.
- Shaun gives him the academy's crown for the fastest-scaling student.

## Notes

Shaun says the speed came from the right team, mindset, attitude, work ethic and education.
