## Description

Why Shaun now teaches aspiring entrepreneurs. Once you have learned something that changed your life, you are in a position to change other people's lives too. He has already made the mistakes and has a proven formula, so he can fast-track others, and helping people is what fulfils him.

## Details

- When you learn a skill or something that changes your life, you reach a point where you can help change other people's lives.
- He can fast-track others so they do not have to make the mistakes he has already made, using what he calls a proven formula.
- Building a community and helping other people also serves his legacy.
- He feels fulfilled by helping people. Having reached a position many aspire to, he believes he has a lot to give, while saying he still has a long road ahead.
