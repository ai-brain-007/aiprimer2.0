## Description

The housing team leader sits between the directors and the housing officers in Shaun's company and runs the day-to-day operation. If you build a social housing business, this is the role that lets you step back from daily tasks. Tyler holds it at Urban Homes.

## Details

Where the role sits:
- Between the directors and the housing officers

What it covers, all in combination:
- Managing all day-to-day operations
- Assessing a tenant before they move in
- The presentation of the houses
- Maintenance
- Compliance

Tyler's description of the job: new challenges every day, no day the same, and the aim is to keep things operating smoothly. Shaun uses his catch-up with Tyler to hear how the business is performing and what his concerns are.

The transcript says "compliant"; the meaning is compliance.
