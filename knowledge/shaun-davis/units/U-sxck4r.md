## Description

Shaun uses this story to show why nightly rates run high. One council has a domestic violence victim who must leave the area immediately and go far from the abuser. The receiving council needs a place now, so it will pay well. Urgent demand plus scarce supply pushes the rate up.

## Details

- Setup: a borough in London and one in Birmingham, for instance. One council contacts a London borough with a domestic violence case.
- The message: this victim needs to move out of the area immediately and somewhere far away from the abuser, because it is a very severe emergency.
- Effect: the councils become quite desperate and need the property quickly.
- Lesson: when demand is high and supply is low, the rates go higher, so you can negotiate a specific nightly rate with the council.
