## Description

People worry about what happens when the contract on a rented property runs out. Shaun's experience is that if you have done a good job, caused no hassle and paid the rent on time, one of two things happens: the landlord renews, or you get first dibs if the landlord wants to sell. He has just re-signed a house he has held for three years for another five.

## Details

The conditions on your side:
- You have been doing a good job
- You have not been causing any hassle
- You have been paying your rent on time

The two outcomes:
1. The landlord renews the contract
2. If the landlord wants to sell, you get first dibs on buying the property

Example: a property held for 3 years, contracts just signed again for another 5 years. Shaun calls this a game changer.
