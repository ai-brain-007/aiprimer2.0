## Description

Leading with the words rent to rent gets you a no before you have started. The term has a bad name because cowboys took on properties, could not pay the rent and ruined it for everyone. Shaun never says it; he says he works with the council.

## Details

- The mistake: telling an agent you do rent to rent, or opening with temporary accommodation jargon.
- Why it fails: rent to rent has a bad name. Cowboys take on properties, do not do the right job, cannot afford the rent and sabotage it for everyone else. One agent told him he can smell a rent to rent from a mile away.
- The consequence: people conclude that a whole town like Luton does not work and that agents do not do rent to rent, when it is a wording problem.
- The fix: do not use the words. Say "we work with the council", keep it simple, be direct.
