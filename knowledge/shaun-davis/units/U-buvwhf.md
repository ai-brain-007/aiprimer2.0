## Description

Nightly rate income can stop, because the council has not signed a lease with you. So before you take on a nightly rate property, decide what you will do with it if the placements dry up. Shaun highly recommends this for anyone entering the scheme.

## Details

- Applies to: anyone getting involved in the nightly rate scheme.
- Reason: the council is not leasing the property from you, so the income carries a high risk.
- Action: make sure you have an exit strategy in place before you start.
- The chunk does not say what the exit strategy should be.
