## Description

One of Sean's students works directly with the council and takes three and four-bed houses from landlords. The council paid him £9,000 up front and pays about £850 per tenant per month, leaving roughly £750 profit a month on a four-bed. The upfront cash is what funds his next deal.

## Details

- The student works directly with the council, not through a provider
- Upfront payment from the council: £9,000, made up of a cash incentive for landlords to go ahead with the strategy, rent up front and a deposit
- Ongoing income: about £850 per month per tenant
- Profit: roughly £750 per month on a four-bedroom property
- Model: take a lot of three to four-bedroom properties from landlords and hand them to the council
- Why it scales: the upfront incentive gives him the cash flow to move on to the next deal quickly
