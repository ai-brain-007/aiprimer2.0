## Description

Before property, Sean sold cars. Certain customers would only deal with him and would come back another day if he was off, even though the car and the service were the same from any colleague. He uses it to show that people buy from the person they believe in.

## Details

- Sean worked in the motor trade selling cars and says the skills transfer to bringing in business.
- Certain customers would buy specifically from him and refuse another salesperson. If he was off they would come back the next day when he was in.
- They could have gone to someone else and got the exact same product and great customer service.
- The reason they waited: they had confidence in him, they trusted him, they liked him as a person. They believed in him.
- The lesson for landlords: get them to believe in you the same way.
