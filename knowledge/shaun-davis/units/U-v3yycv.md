## Description

You do not have to work where you live. Providers operate in every major UK city, and the business can be systematised so that a student in London runs deals in Liverpool and the reverse. If your local council says no, another area is a phone call away.

## Details

- These strategies are not limited to one area; look across the whole UK.
- Providers operate in Birmingham, London, Manchester and all major cities. Cheaper areas such as the north east suit those looking to buy.
- Students based in London operate in Liverpool and vice versa.
- Systematising the business is simple; the author says it is not hard at all.
- Use a VA to do the research on other areas.
