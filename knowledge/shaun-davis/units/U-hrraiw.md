## Description

Money passes from person to person all day. You can watch it from the outside, or you can stand in the middle so it flows through you. The way in is to have something to sell: a product or a service.

## Details

- Ask: are you watching money move from the outside, or are you in the middle where it passes through you? Are you part of the process?
- The way in is to have a product or a service. Then you become part of the journey of the £10 note rather than a spectator.
- This is lesson two of the four: watch how money moves, get in the flow of money.
