## Description

Shaun's closing message: do whatever it takes to get into bigger rooms. The energy of the people around you rubs off on you even when you do not notice it, so surround yourself with people who feel almost delusional about what they can achieve, and avoid those with limited beliefs who take your power away.

## Details

- Do what you can to get into bigger rooms. Shaun is going to America and then Dubai specifically to expand his network.
- You are who you hang around with. Energy rubs off, even on a subconscious level.
- Around negative people with limited beliefs who do not push themselves, that energy becomes yours.
- Around people who believe they can achieve anything, the conversation changes and you start thinking 'if they can do it, I can too'.
- Choose people who make you feel you can do more and be more; avoid people who want to take your power away.
