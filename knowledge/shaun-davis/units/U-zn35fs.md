## Description

Under the A model you receive roughly the LHA rate, sometimes slightly more, and the tenant pays a top-up from their Universal Credit. The top-up is where your risk sits. It depends on the tenant managing their money and knowing what is due when.

## Details

- You receive usually the LHA rate or a little above it
- The tenant pays a top-up out of their Universal Credit
- Risk: the tenant must understand how to manage their income, what the bills are and the dates they must be paid
- Mitigation: collect by direct debit through a portal that flags failures and cancellations, and ask the council what support exists if the tenant defers

## Notes

The author uses LHA as a known term and does not expand it.
