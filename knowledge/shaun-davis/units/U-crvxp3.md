## Description

The PRS is the ombudsman scheme the author belongs to. Membership shows councils you are a proper business that will stick to its word, and gives tenants, landlords and third parties an adjudicator if something goes wrong. For a new company it buys peace of mind.

## Details

- You need to be part of an ombudsman scheme. The author uses the PRS.
- Other ombudsman services exist.
- What it signals: you are a good business, willing to provide a service and stick to your word.
- What it does: if that does not happen, tenants, landlords and third parties can go to an adjudicator who looks at the situation and solves the problem.
- Why it matters: it gives everyone peace of mind, especially when you are a new company.

The transcript renders 'ombudsman' as 'budsman' and 'unbudsman'. The author does not expand the abbreviation PRS.
