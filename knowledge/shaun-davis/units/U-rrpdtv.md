## Description

A landlord on OpenRent has other viewings booked, so ask what it takes to secure the house today. Offer what a normal tenant would offer, a week's rent as a holding deposit, and ask for the listing to come down. Get the terms written into the contract.

## Details

1. Ask the landlord straight out: "What can we do to secure the property today?"
2. Offer a week's rent as a holding deposit, the same as a tenant would pay through OpenRent.
3. Ask for the bank details and send the money the same day.
4. Ask the landlord to take the property off OpenRent as soon as the payment lands.
5. Put the arrangement in the contract: the holding deposit is deducted from the first month's rent.
6. With that done, collect the keys.
