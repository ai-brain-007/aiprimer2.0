## Description

Asked what he would outsource on day one if he started over, Sean names two things: bookkeeping and accounts, and admin plus lead generation. Both are low-value tasks for the owner and both slowed him down when he tried to do them himself.

## Details

1. Bookkeeping and accounts. Get a bookkeeper or accountant straight away rather than trying to do the books yourself.
2. Admin and lead generation. Hand phone calls, emails, research and chasing landlords and agents to a virtual assistant so your own time goes on high-value tasks.

Both are things Sean wishes he had done from the very start.
