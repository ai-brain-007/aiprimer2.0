## Description

In car sales Shaun had a very low basic wage and earned mostly commission. Because the basic did not even cover his bills, every month he had to deliver results or go unpaid. He credits that pressure with building the drive he later used in business.

## Details

- Structure: very low basic salary plus high commission.
- The basic wage was lower than his monthly bills, so each month he had to hit results to get paid.
- Effect: every single month his back was against the wall, which forced him to deliver.
