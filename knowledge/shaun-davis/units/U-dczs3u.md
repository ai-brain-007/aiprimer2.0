## Description

Run simple online ads aimed at landlords. State plainly the benefits you provide and how you solve their problems. The clearer the problem-solving, the more likely a landlord hands you their property.

## Details

- Keep the ad very simple
- Make the benefits very clear: features and benefits
- Remember you are solving the landlord's problem; showcase how well you solve it
