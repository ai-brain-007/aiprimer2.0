## Description

What you learn in one business or job carries over to the next. Shaun points to his father, who took 20 years of skills from his security business into a food company he acquired. Shaun himself took sales and leadership skills from car dealerships into property.

## Details

- Father's case: resilience, skills and leadership built over 20 years in security were used to grow an acquired Jamaican food company.
- Shaun's case: he helped grow his father's food business with his sales background, then took car sales skills into rent to rent.
- Car sales also taught him that business is cutthroat, another lesson that transferred.
- Shaun says sales in particular teaches a lot about business and gives many transferable skills.
