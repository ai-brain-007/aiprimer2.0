## Description

Sandu's early attempts failed because his councils did not pay enough. With thin margins only a very cheap property makes a profit, and those are rare. Fix the council before you fix the property.

## Details

- Mistake: signing up councils whose rates leave no profit, then searching for properties
- Consequence: you need an unrealistically cheap property, and deals do not stack up
- Fix: find a council with strong temporary or emergency accommodation demand and good rates; then the numbers work on ordinary properties

## Notes

Said by Sandu, the mentee, reflecting on his months before the challenge.
