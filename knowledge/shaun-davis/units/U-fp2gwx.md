## Description

The very first letter Shaun and Ty hand-delivered turned into a deal. He posted it, the landlord called the next day, they sat down over coffee and closed at £950 a month. Shaun thought it was game over and told everyone, but he admits he got lucky and it was never that easy again.

## Details

- Letter posted through the letter box; the landlord rang the following day.
- Meeting at the landlord's house over coffee; deal closed.
- Income from that deal: £950 a month.
- Shaun's reaction: this is too easy; he told family, friends and colleagues and started thinking about handing in his notice.
- His caveat: it was not that easy in the end; he got lucky with that one. Still, handing out letters kept working and the business reached £6,000 a month.
