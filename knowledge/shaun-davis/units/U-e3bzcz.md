## Description

Shaun finds that virtual assistants from countries with a hard economy are very grateful for the job. That gratitude shows up as a different attitude and level of work ethic: hardworking, willing and keen to help you move the needle. He rates attitude in the team as crucial.

## Details

- Observation: overseas VAs are very grateful for the job because the economy and climate in their country is hard.
- Effect: a different attitude and a different level of work ethic.
- Traits he lists: very grateful, very hardworking, great attitude, willing to work, willing to help move the needle.
- Having a team with the right attitude is, in his word, crucial.
