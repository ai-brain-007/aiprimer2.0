## Description

This is what Shaun says when he walks into a lettings agent cold. It is short, plain and improvised around rapport: who you are, that you work with the council, what you need, and whether they do company lets. Sandu's simpler version on day two got two properties lined up.

## Details

Structure, kept loose and friendly:
1. "Hi, lettings please." Shake hands, first names.
2. Who you are, in one line: "We do temporary accommodation with the council" or "We work with Luton Council" or, for a newcomer, "We're property investors, new in the area."
3. What you need: "We're looking for three or four bedroom properties for families. Do you have anything on the books at all?" Timing: as soon as possible.
4. If price comes up: "We're looking at paying max £1,500 for a three-bedroom property."
5. Ask: "Do you guys do company lets as well?"
6. If the landlord's interest comes up: "We'll take it off you on a long-term let. Landlords like it because it's guaranteed rent. We pay the market rent."
7. Ask for a viewing today.

All sales should be improvisation: go in, build rapport, keep it simple, explain what you do and what you want.

Sandu's day-two version: "We are property investors, new in the area, and we're looking for three to four bed houses. As soon as possible." And to a sales desk: "We've been tasked by the council to look for three and four bed properties. Do you have any available right now?"
