## Description

Before taking on A model tenants, ask the council openly what support you get as a provider if a tenant stops paying their top-up. The author says never be afraid to ask. It sets expectations and protects your income.

## Details

Say to the council: "We're more than happy to get involved in this strategy. But if the tenant defers, what help and support is there for me as the provider to get paid?"
