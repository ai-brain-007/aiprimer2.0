## Description

Instead of hunting listings online, go to the area you want to work in and walk into every letting agent. Get to know them. Once the relationship is there, agents send you properties before they ever reach the portals, so you are not competing with everyone else.

## Details

1. Pick the city or area you want to scale into.
2. Rather than searching Rightmove for that area, physically go there.
3. Visit every single agent in the area and start forming a relationship.
4. Build on that relationship over time; aim for long lasting, sustainable relationships.
5. Agents then start sending you off market deals, which are easier to close than listed properties.

Shaun says this has been an absolute game changer for him over 6 and a half years and is one of his best methods to close deals quickly.
