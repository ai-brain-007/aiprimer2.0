## Description

Not every council houses refugees and asylum seekers. Certain councils do, and the list changes over time. If housing refugees is what you care about, research where these tenants are placed and work with those councils directly.

## Details

1. Note that only certain UK councils work specifically with asylum seekers, and this varies and changes from time to time.
2. Research and find out exactly where these tenants are placed.
3. If you are passionate about housing refugees and solving that problem, work with those councils specifically and house refugee tenants.
