## Description

The biggest thing the mentorship taught the student was how to present things. The same request said the right way gets a landlord or council to work with you; said the wrong way, they back off. Saying the right things at the right time builds trust.

## Details

- You can say the same thing in a slightly different way and get an opposite result.
- Right way: they will work with you. Wrong way or wrong context: they back off.
- It gains trust when you say the right things at the right time.
- Applies to landlords, councils and providers, including negotiating rates and deposits.

## Notes

The student says the mentorship gave him confidence in how to deal with situations and talk to people.
