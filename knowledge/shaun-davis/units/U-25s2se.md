## Description

Sean's tie-breaker between business models. If two routes pay the same and one takes less work, take the one with less work. It is why he left serviced accommodation for temporary accommodation.

## Details

- Compare the reward and the work of each option, not just the reward.
- If the reward is the same and the work is lower, choose the lower work. Sean's question: why would you not?
- Applied: serviced accommodation with its guests, cleans and 24/7 calls was not the level of work he wanted long term; temporary accommodation pays consistently with minimal operations.
