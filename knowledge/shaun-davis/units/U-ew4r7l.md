## Description

You lease a property from a landlord and lease it straight on to a social housing provider. The provider runs the house and the tenants; you keep the gap between the two rents. The author calls it a game changer because it involves zero operations.

## Details

- Definition: find providers (like the author) and become the middleman between landlord and provider
- You take a lease from the landlord, then lease the same property on to the provider
- The key benefit: zero operations, so no property, tenant or management worries
- Income: people doing this make between £500 and £1,000 a month
