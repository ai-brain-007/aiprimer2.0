## Description

An entrepreneur on your payroll watches what you do, decides they can do it themselves and leaves. Shaun hires people who want to build within the company: they take risks and act like owners without carrying the financial risk, and they grow inside the business.

## Details

- Do not hire an entrepreneur: they see what you do, think "I can do this myself" and leave.
- Hire an intrapreneur: someone who builds within, takes risks and feels like an entrepreneur without the financial risk, inside the company.
- They still get growth and still need and develop leadership skills, but they grow within the business.
- His own motive for going alone: he wanted to control his own financial future and not have another person responsible for it.

## Notes

The transcript writes both words as "entrepreneur"; from the context ("build within") the second one is "intrapreneur".
