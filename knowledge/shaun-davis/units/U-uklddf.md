## Description

Shaun's starting point is that the person you turn into while building matters more than the assets you end up holding. If the skills and habits are real, you can start again from nothing. The whole challenge in this video is a test of that idea.

## Details

- The tweet that started it: the greatest asset is not what you get, it's who you become.
- The question it raised: could he rebuild everything if he had to start again?
- The test: live with a stranger for one week and change their life through property, in towns he had never worked in, with no contacts, no resources and no network.
