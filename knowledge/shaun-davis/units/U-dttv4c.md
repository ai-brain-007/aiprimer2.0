## Description

This is the remedial list a council surveyor gave on a nearly new house. Most of it is safety and finish. Use it to check a property before you offer on it, so the works are light.

## Details

Safety:
- Window restrictors on every floor above ground level; an unrestricted upstairs window is a major safety risk
- Door stops on every door, so doors do not swing and break the wall
- Fire blanket in the kitchen
- Lock on the bathroom door
- All doors catching and closing properly; a door that does not close will be picked up straight away

Finish:
- Windows dressed with blinds or curtains in every room
- Kitchen pipework and gaps boxed in
- Missing screws in hinges replaced
- Unpainted doors and cupboard doors painted; patched walls sanded and repainted
- No signs of damp; the council is 'all over' damp

Furnishing:
- White goods: fridge, freezer, washing machine, dishwasher (this landlord had already provided them)
- Beds only; this council did not require wardrobes
- Carpet with underlay was a plus

## Notes

Requirements come from one council on one property; other councils may differ. The house had no gas supply.
