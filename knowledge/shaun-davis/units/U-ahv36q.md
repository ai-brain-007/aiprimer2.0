## Description

Unlike serviced accommodation, a temporary accommodation property does not need designer interiors or the latest tech. It has to be fit for purpose and suitable for the council. The tenant furnishes it and makes it their home, which keeps your set-up costs low.

## Details

- No need for the latest tech or top interior design.
- Requirement: fit for purpose and suitable for the council.
- The property becomes someone's home. The tenant is responsible for furnishing it and getting it the way they like it.
- Business effect: upfront set-up costs are substantially lower than serviced accommodation.
