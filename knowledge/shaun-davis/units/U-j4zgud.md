## Description

Before you start a business, check that it fits you. Hardship will come, and by then the early motivation will be gone; discipline only holds if you are acting within your purpose. Shaun does not copy what others do well, because what is written for them may not be written for you.

## Details

Ask yourself, honestly:
1. Does this business align with my purpose?
2. What do I like doing?
3. What are my strengths?
4. What are my goals?
5. What is my purpose, more importantly, and does the business align with it?
6. When I hit hardship, will I have the discipline and the resilience to work through it and come out the other end?
- Why: challenges and obstacles will come, and the motivation you started with will have faded by then. Discipline is very hard to build if you are not acting within your purpose.
- Do not follow people just because they do something well and effortlessly. It is written for them, and it may not be written for you.
