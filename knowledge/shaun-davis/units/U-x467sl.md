## Description

Your tenants are your customers, so ask them in person how they find the service and what you could improve. Shaun says you usually hear the provider's, landlord's or investor's view, and the only way to know what the customer thinks is to speak to them yourself. He spends a day walking his houses doing exactly that.

## Details

- Most of what you hear in the sector comes from the provider, the landlord or the investor. Make time to hear the tenant's point of view.
- Ask about the service, the accommodation, the staff and the house, and what could be done better.
- Shaun frames it as the beauty of business: you need to speak to your actual client, and in social housing the client is the tenant.
- The purpose is constant improvement: striving for perfection and for a better product and service. Speaking to tenants directly is, in his words, the only way to find out how.
- For newcomers to the sector, hearing tenants explains how it all works and what the tenants are like.
