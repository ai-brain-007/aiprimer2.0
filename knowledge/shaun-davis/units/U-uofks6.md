## Description

When Shaun was young and broke he focused only on making money, and made none. The mistake was skipping the fundamentals. Put your energy into building the skill and becoming a true expert, and the money comes as a byproduct.

## Details

- The mistake: being money focused because you have no money, and asking only what you can do to earn money.
- The consequence: he stayed financially broke and could not catch a break, because his mindset and focus were on the wrong things.
- The fix: put your energy into building the skill. Get your 10,000 hours in, hone the process, understand your product and your service, and become a true expert in your field.
- Then everything falls into place. The money and the compensation are a byproduct of the work you put in.
