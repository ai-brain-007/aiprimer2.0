## Description

Asked why Dubai of all places, Luigi says he follows his gut feeling and prays about big decisions, treating the result as being led somewhere. A trip over New Year opened his eyes, he extended it, and ten days in Abu Dhabi confirmed he could live there.

## Details

- Follow your instinct, the good feeling about a place or decision.
- Pray about it a lot and treat the outcome as guidance.
- Test it: he extended his New Year trip, went back after Thailand, spent ten days in Abu Dhabi, and only then decided he could live there.

## Notes

Luigi's personal approach, not Shaun's.
