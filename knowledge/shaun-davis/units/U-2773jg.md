## Description

One of Shaun's students landed a temporary accommodation deal on a council incentive scheme: £900 a month profit plus £9,600 paid up front. The council offered to do the same for his first 15 to 20 properties. It shows how incentives let you grow with very little of your own money.

## Details

- Profit: £900 a month on the property.
- Incentive: £9,600 paid up front by the council.
- The council told him it would do a deal like this for his first 15 or 20 properties.
- He has closed three deals in less than 30 days with the programme.
- He has only been putting in a few hundred pounds per deal, because the incentives cover the rest.
- His targets: 10 properties by next month, 30 by the end of the year.
