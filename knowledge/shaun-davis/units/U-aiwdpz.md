## Description

Shaun's first social housing deal was sourced to someone else. The next three or four he took on himself, did the refurbs and got them running. He became an approved provider with Birmingham City Council at a time when the council was desperate for housing and the process was easy.

## Details

1. Deal one: sourced to a social housing provider for a fee.
2. The next three or four: taken on himself, refurbished himself and boxed off.
3. First own deal: very good condition, no refurb, three months rent free.
4. Second own deal: same three months rent free, needed some work, a couple of months after the first.
5. Became approved provider with Birmingham City Council. Back then it was easy because the council was inundated with homeless people and needed providers; they said come to the office, come and see us.
6. Support and accommodation was the first strategy he did. He says it is much harder now because of red tape.
