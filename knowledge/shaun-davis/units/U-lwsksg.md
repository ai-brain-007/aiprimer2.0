## Description

The most common excuse Shaun hears is "I don't have time". He says it really means you have not yet learned how to use the time you have. You do not need endless free hours to build a rent-to-rent temporary accommodation business. You need a few focused hours and the structure and leverage to make them count.

## Details

- The excuse "I don't have time" really means "I haven't yet learned how to manage my time".
- The idea that you need loads of free time to start in property is a myth. Time has never been the real problem. The real problem is lack of structure and lack of leverage.
- You do not need more hours. You need better use of the hours you already have. Shaun calls this one of the biggest mindset shifts you need to make.
- Instead of more time you need four things:
  1. Clarity
  2. Structure
  3. Leverage
  4. The discipline to stop giving energy to low value tasks that do not move the needle
- If you do that, you can build the business around any job, any lifestyle and any responsibility, no matter how busy you think you are.
