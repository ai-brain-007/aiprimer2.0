## Description

Make the search a daily habit, or hand it to someone on your team. New contracts get awarded all the time, and the first person to call a newly funded provider gets the partnership. A few hours a day of this could mean several deals in a month.

## Details

1. Look at Contracts Finder every day, not once.
2. Outsource or leverage: get someone in your team to run the search for you.
3. When a provider has just closed a contract, contact them immediately and offer to partner.
4. Sean's estimate: spending a few hours a day on this could produce quite a few closed deals within a month.

The search itself is quick: under five minutes found over 40 providers.
