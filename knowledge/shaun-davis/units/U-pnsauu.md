## Description

The more successful you get, the more haters and headaches you attract. Shaun listens to Grant Cardone partly because he keeps reminding people of that. Doing everything by the book does not stop it: haters and difficult employees are just the real world.

## Details

- Success brings haters and hardship; treat both as normal.
- Doing everything right does not exempt you. Shaun still gets haters and employees who cause headaches.
- Shaun's response: document it and keep sharing openly, even though in this generation sharing something means someone gets offended.
