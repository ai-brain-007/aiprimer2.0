## Description

Giving children everything feels like love, but Shaun says the word tells you the result: spoil them and they become spoiled. You skip the lessons and the hardship, so you do more damage than good. Let them enjoy the fruits, but teach the skill set alongside.

## Details

- The mistake: spoiling your kids too much
- The consequence: they become spoiled, you are not teaching the lessons, and you do more damage than good
- Why it backfires: without hardship they end up losing what they are given
- The fix: be conscious about it; show them the lifestyle and let them enjoy the fruits of your labour, but teach them so they build the skill set to steer the ship when you are gone
