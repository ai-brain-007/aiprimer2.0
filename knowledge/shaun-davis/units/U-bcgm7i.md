## Description

Successful people have a rhythm to how they work, move and solve problems. Shaun says it is the biggest thing a newcomer picks up from shadowing him: how quickly you deal with a challenge, how you approach a situation and how you think on your feet.

## Details

- There is a rhythm to success. People who have achieved something great share it.
- You see it in: the way they work, the way they move, how quickly they deal with challenges, how they approach situations, how they solve problems, how they create value, how they think on their feet.
- It is learned by watching and doing alongside someone who has it, which is why day one was shadowing.
