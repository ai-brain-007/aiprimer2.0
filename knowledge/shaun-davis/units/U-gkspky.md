## Description

Picture the outcome you want every day, in detail. Shaun says the mind runs the same process for a vivid visualisation as for reality, so it treats the goal as already real and starts finding ways to get there. It also conditions you to notice opportunities you would otherwise walk past.

## Details

- Practice: visualise your success every single day. Shaun visualised the day he would hand in his notice at work, every day, and thought about it all the time.
- Why he says it works: your mind has the same identical process in visualisation and in actual reality. It does not know the difference, so it thinks the goal is already reality. You are, in effect, tricking your mind into success.
- If you can see it in your mind, you can create the reality. Once you visualise it, you find a way of making it happen, and it becomes a self-fulfilling prophecy.
- Side effect: it conditions your mind for success and to spot opportunities (see the Porsche example).
- He acknowledges it can sound silly to someone who has never done it, and insists on it anyway.
