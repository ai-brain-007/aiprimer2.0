## Description

Ambition and good properties count for nothing if you cannot fill them. Shaun's rule: build the referral pipeline before you build the portfolio, because an unfilled property costs you money every month instead of earning it.

## Details

- Applies most to supported accommodation, where referrals do not arrive on their own.
- Fix: build relationships with councils and homeless shelters first, so there is an influx of referrals before you take on properties.
