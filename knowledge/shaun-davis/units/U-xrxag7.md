## Description

When Shaun comes back to the UK he spends a day on the ground: he catches up with his team leader, then walks a series of houses and talks to the tenants. He checks how the houses are maintained, spots problems and hears how the service feels from inside. It is how an owner who is not there every day keeps a true picture of the business.

## Details

How the day runs:
1. Catch up with the housing team leader: how the business is performing, his views, his concerns.
2. Visit properties in the team's patches and look for any concerns.
3. Speak to tenants in each house: how they find the service, the staff and the house, and whether there are any issues.
4. Where something is wrong, deal with it on the spot. Examples from the day: a house that is too hot, so check for a heating receiver; a smell of weed from a room; a damp repair in progress.
5. Close the day with a verdict on maintenance, tenant feedback and the team.

What Shaun looked at: how well each house is maintained, communal spaces, tenants' rooms, works in progress, and who has been moved in.
