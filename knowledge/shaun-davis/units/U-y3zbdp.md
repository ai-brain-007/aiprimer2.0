## Description

Instead of mapping the whole road, Shaun asks one question: what three things can you do right now that get you ahead? The ABC is the next step in front of you; the XYZ is the far end you cannot see yet. Focus on the three you can act on today.

## Details

1. Stop trying to work out the XYZ, the far-off steps you cannot see yet.
2. Ask: what three things can I do right now that will get me ahead of the game?
3. Do those three. That is the ABC.

## Notes

Shaun calls it one of his favourite quotes; he does not say where it comes from.
