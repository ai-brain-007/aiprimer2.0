## Description

With Airbnb and working-professional HMOs you have to go out and find your tenants, and that never stops. In supported accommodation most tenants come to you through council and homeless shelter referrals. In temporary accommodation there is no tenant marketing at all. Shaun sees this as the main reason to leave Airbnb and HMO behind.

## Details

- Airbnb and working professionals: problem number one is tenant marketing. You have to market to find the tenants.
- Supported accommodation: a little marketing at times, but mostly the tenants come through homeless shelters and the council, who send referrals.
- Temporary accommodation: zero tenant marketing, which is why he calls it his favourite and the most passive strategy.
- He is handing back all his rent-to-rent Airbnbs to get rid of the stress.
