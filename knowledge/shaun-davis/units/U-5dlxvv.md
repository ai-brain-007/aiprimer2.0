## Description

Recessions come round as part of the cycle, so plan to win in every season, not just the good ones. Your job as an entrepreneur is to solve problems and create value; do that well and you can thrive in a downturn. Pulling back out of fear is how people sabotage their own success, because the downturn is the moment with the most room to grow.

## Details

- Covid nearly wiped Shaun out. He adapted by looking at the trend and changing strategy.
- A great entrepreneurial trait: understand that you are there to solve problems and create value. Do that effectively and you thrive.
- Recessions are always coming as part of the cycle. Three responses: some people thrive, some spot the opportunities, some retract and pull back.
- Retracting out of fear sabotages your success, because a recession is the opportunity to grow the most.
