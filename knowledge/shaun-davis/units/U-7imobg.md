## Description

The one condition that makes back-to-back leasing risk-free. Your lease with the landlord must mirror your lease with the provider: same terms, same break clauses. Then if the provider cancels, you can exit the landlord lease too, instead of being stuck paying for an empty property.

## Details

- The structure is a three-way partnership: landlord to you, you to the provider.
- You sign a commercial lease agreement on both sides.
- Whatever you signed with the provider, the contract with the landlord must be congruent: exactly the same terms and the same break clauses.
- Why: if the provider deal falls through, you are not lumbered with a property that becomes bad debt. If they cancel, you can get out of your contract too.
- As long as the contracts align, Shaun says the strategy literally becomes risk-free.
