## Description

Shaun uses Trump's first weeks in office as an example of an entrepreneurial mindset applied to a role: define where you are, where you need to go, and act as if you have not got all day. He is careful to say this is about the mindset, not politics.

## Details

- The pattern: treat the role as a business. 'This is the business, this is where we are now, this is where we need to get to, and we haven't got all day.'
- Result Shaun points to: many decisions made and things boxed off very quickly.
- Both hosts flag that a viewer might read this as being pro-Trump; the point is the business approach, whether you like him or not.

## Notes

Political example; the speakers explicitly distance themselves from endorsing Trump.
