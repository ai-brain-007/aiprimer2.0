## Description

Serviced accommodation starts from zero every month: no bookings until you fill them, plus check-ins, reviews, cleaning, linen, parties and trashed houses. Shaun compares it to his old car-sales job of low basic and high commission. Temporary accommodation gives him the opposite: he knows what is coming in months ahead and can plan.

## Details

- Serviced accommodation workload: guests checking in and out constantly, hospitality reviews, cleaning, changing linen, parties, people trashing the house.
- Income pattern: a blank canvas every month. In March you look at April and May and see no bookings yet.
- Shaun's car-sales comparison: very low basic, high commission, and every month you start from zero again.
- Temporary accommodation: once the property is on board there is little to do, and the income is stable month after month, so you can plan.
- Overall Shaun rates it a lot less risky.
