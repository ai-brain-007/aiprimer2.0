## Description

The way you think drives what you do, the habits you build and the results you get. Shaun says mindset, not money, luck or talent, is what separates people who succeed from people who struggle. His early side hustles failed because he skipped this foundation, and his business took off once he worked on it.

## Details

- The foundation has three parts: your health, your mindset and taking your personal development seriously.
- Before property, every side hustle Shaun tried to turn into a business failed, because he never worked on that foundation.
- As soon as he took personal development seriously his business skyrocketed. He credits it with building a multi seven figure organisation in just under seven years.
- Closing message of the talk: the difference between winners and losers is not money, luck or talent, only mindset. Train your mind to think like a winner, act like a winner and work like a winner, and you become one.
