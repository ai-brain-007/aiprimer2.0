## Description

Shaun's own route into social housing shows why he values stability over quick wins. He tried serviced accommodation, added rent-to-HMO, then lost both overnight when Covid hit. Social housing was the pivot, and it has carried him since 2020.

## Details

1. Started in serviced accommodation. After about six months it was working okay but did not give the stability he wanted.
2. Kept serviced accommodation and added rent-to-HMO on top, building a team on the side. It went well enough for him to leave his job.
3. Covid hit: serviced accommodation was pulled overnight, which taught him it was not a recession-proof strategy. The HMO students ran back to their parents' houses and professionals lost their jobs.
4. He had no choice but to pivot, and moved into social housing in 2020.
5. Social housing has gone extremely well since: he now has 500 rooms and says he has only scratched the surface.
