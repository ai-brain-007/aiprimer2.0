## Description

In social housing, people's lives are at stake. Sean says if money is your main motive you will fail quickly. Put your tenants and your team first, and when it comes to it, pay the team before you pay yourself.

## Details

- In social housing specifically, money at the forefront of your mind means you fail very quickly, because people's lives are at stake.
- The order: the people first, the team first, the tenants first.
- Sean has had to pay himself last and pay the team first many times. He calls that the reality of running a business.
- Why he takes it seriously: people rely on him to pay their mortgages and rent and live their lifestyle. He sees creating reliable, stable careers as part of the job.
