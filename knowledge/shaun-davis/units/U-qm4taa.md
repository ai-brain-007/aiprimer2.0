## Description

Ask yourself what you will do to stand out. Shaun's answer is to be yourself and to care about people for real. Add doing the job properly and you have what he calls the magic formula: people bend over backwards for you.

## Details

Ask: what will I do to be different, to stand out, to be disruptive in the marketplace? The answer is being yourself and having genuine care and interest in people.
What the council remembers about a good provider:
- Gives them good properties
- Does not mess them about
- Turns up on time
- Does the work well
- Looks after the tenants
Do the job and build rapport, and people will recommend you to colleagues and get you in sooner. Make things simple for them too.
