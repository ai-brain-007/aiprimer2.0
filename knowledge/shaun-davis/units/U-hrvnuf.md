## Description

Mattresses in temporary accommodation do not always get respected. A PVC mattress is waterproof and fully wipeable, so you clean it and keep it fresh instead of replacing it.

## Details

- Buy PVC (waterproof) mattresses for every bed, doubles included
- They are fully wipeable, which keeps them clean, fresh and new
- Reason: tenants sometimes do not respect the mattresses
