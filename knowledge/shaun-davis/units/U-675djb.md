## Description

Think of your money as seeds. Eat them all, which means spend, and they are gone. Plant them, which means invest, and they grow into trees that produce more money. It is the author's picture for why you should not spend everything you get.

## Details

- Spend = eat the seeds: the money disappears.
- Invest = plant the seeds: they grow into trees that make more money.
- The closing rule for the kids: don't spend all your seeds, grow them.
