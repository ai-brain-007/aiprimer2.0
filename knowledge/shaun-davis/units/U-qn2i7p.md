## Description

Councils judge you on inspections, maintenance and clear communication; these decide whether you last. Good is not enough, and the author has watched many providers fall away. Put policies, procedures and systems in place from day one.

## Details

- Inspections, maintenance and clear communication mean everything to councils; they give you longevity
- Being good is not good enough; you need to be exceptional
- Have clear systems and processes to deliver an exceptional service
- Have all your policies and procedures in place to run the business effectively and efficiently
- Start as you mean to go on from day one
- Anything less than exceptional maintenance and compliance is not acceptable
