## Description

Serviced accommodation means letting a property for short stays, Airbnb style. It costs the most to set up because you compete on interior design and a unique selling point, and the earning potential is high in busy periods. The author ran it for three years and then stopped.

## Details

- Start-up cost: around £7,000 to £15,000.
- Why the cost is higher: there are lots of very basic Airbnbs. The ones making money are unique, with great interior design. You have to spend to get it up to standard and think about your USP.
- Upside: in a prime location during a special event you can charge three or even four times the normal amount.
- Downside: peaks and troughs. Booked out in summer, horrendous in winter, and you usually hold a rent-to-rent lease with no guarantee of bookings.
- The author's verdict: great if you love hospitality. He left it after COVID for a recession-proof model.
