## Description

When you call a temporary accommodation provider from Contracts Finder, you usually reach voicemail. This is the short message Shaun leaves, and the live version he uses when someone picks up. Say who you are, what you can supply, and make it easy to call back.

## Details

Voicemail version:
"Hi, my name is Sean. I'm a property investor local in the area and just wanted to see what you currently need housing wise as we may be able to work together and supply you with properties. Give me a call back on this number. I'm free all day today. I'm in the area all week. Love the opportunity to have a call with you. Thank you."

Live version:
"We're property investors in the local area and we're looking to work with temporary and emergency accommodation providers. Just wanted to find out if you're currently looking for more properties. We may be able to help you."

After the call:
1. Send them an email straight away so they have two ways back to you.
2. If they are not taking on now, ask for the right person's email and when they will be taking on again.
3. Call a few in an area, then move on; do not get stuck on numbers that are out of service.
