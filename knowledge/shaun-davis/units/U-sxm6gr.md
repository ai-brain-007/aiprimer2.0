## Description

The students who do best are not the ones with the easiest deals. They are the ones who expect problems, embrace them and treat solving them as part of the job. Kicked-in doors, slow councils and bumps in the road are normal; the business must keep sailing regardless.

## Details

- Go in expecting challenges, not an easy ride.
- Embrace the challenges and become a professional problem solver.
- Mindset line: there could be bumps in the road, but we will overcome them regardless, and the ship must continue to sail.
- Sandu is the example: not used to day-to-day problems at first, now a professional problem solver with nine deals.
