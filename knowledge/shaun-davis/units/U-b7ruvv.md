## Description

The simplest temporary accommodation deal. The council places a tenant and pays you a nightly rate you negotiate, and the profit can be £500 to £1,500 a month per property after all costs. Operations are minimal, so the model runs from anywhere; Shaun has mentees doing it from America and Paris.

## Details

- The council places a tenant and pays a nightly rate; you negotiate that rate.
- Profit: roughly £500 to £1,500 per month after all costs.
- Operations are very minimal, which is why it scales nationwide and can be run remotely, even from Dubai.
- Everything can be outsourced; mentees in America and Paris run it.
- Weakness: there is no contract. It is done on a nightly basis and you invoice the council at the end of each month.
