## Description

The author's phrase for running a business: you make a product or a service that people want or need, and they pay you for it. It is the third way money comes in and the one the whole video builds toward.

## Details

- Creating value in the marketplace = creating a product or service that people want or need.
- When you do, people pay you, and money starts to chase you rather than you chasing it.
