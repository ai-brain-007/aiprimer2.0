## Description

Asked what separates him from people who try for years and get nowhere, the student says attitude: you cannot let rejection stop you. Landlords and providers will say no a lot at the start, because so many people claim to do this and they have to weed out who is credible.

## Details

- Expect an awful lot of rejection, especially in the beginning.
- Both landlords and people in the provider sector will say no.
- They will not believe you straight away that you can provide what they want, because so many people are doing various things such as Airbnb.
- Providers also have to weed out who actually has the right quality of property.
- The answer is attitude: keep going.
