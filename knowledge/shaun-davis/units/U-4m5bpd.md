## Description

In Shaun's houses tenants may smoke cigarettes in the garden, but cannabis is not allowed anywhere on the premises, garden included. If someone wants to smoke it, they go to the park across the road. The rule is stated plainly to the tenant and backed by an inspection the next day.

## Details

- Cigarettes: allowed outside in the garden
- Weed: not allowed anywhere on the premises, including the garden
- Alternative offered: the park across the road
- Enforcement: tell the tenant not to do it at all on the premises, and follow up with an inspection the next day
