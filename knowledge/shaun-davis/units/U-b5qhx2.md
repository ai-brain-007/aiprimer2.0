## Description

The author's shortlist of social housing strategies that work best in 2026. Each has a different payer, risk level and workload. He recommends you choose one.

## Details

1. Temporary accommodation on a nightly rate: council-paid, long-term demand, higher income and higher responsibility; you manage the property and the tenant
2. The A model: single occupants in an HMO, paid directly by DWP at around the LHA rate plus a tenant top-up; incentives up front; more tenants to manage and more risk, but tends to make more money
3. Back-to-back leases: you sit between a landlord and a provider and keep the rent difference; virtually no risk, zero operations, easy no-money-down deals; the one he would start with from scratch
