## Description

People often ask whether they must register a community interest company before approaching councils. For the three core strategies you do not. A normal limited company is enough; a CIC is only needed for higher-level social housing such as supported accommodation and supported living.

## Details

- AST model, back-to-back leasing, temporary and emergency accommodation: a limited company is more than sufficient.
- Supported accommodation and supported living: these need a CIC, a community interest company.
- You do not need to register a CIC before approaching councils for the core strategies.
