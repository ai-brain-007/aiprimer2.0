## Description

Shaun's first model, renting to working professionals, failed to give him security. Professionals go missing, get sick, lose their jobs and run into money trouble, and each of those hits your income hard. His fix was to get paid by the government instead.

## Details

- The mistake: expecting professional tenants to give you predictable income
- What goes wrong: professionals go missing, get sick, lose their job, have financial issues
- The consequence: a huge financial impact on the business and no financial security
- His result: the original idea sunk like a stone
- The fix: get paid directly from the government through council contracts, which removes these issues
