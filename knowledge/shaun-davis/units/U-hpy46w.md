## Description

When Shaun started at Ford with no experience, he picked the top salesman who sat in front of him, watched what worked and copied it. Within two months he was top of the leaderboard. He still does this in business and life: find who is doing well, pick the traits you like, copy them.

## Details

1. Identify the person who is performing best around you. At Ford it was the top salesman sitting opposite.
2. Shadow them: watch what they do and what is working well for them.
3. Copy it, as simply as that.
4. In business more broadly: look at who is doing well, ask which character traits you like in that person, and copy those.

Result for Shaun: second on the leaderboard in his second month, then first, with no previous motor trade knowledge or skills.
