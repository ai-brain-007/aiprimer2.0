## Description

Shaun asks you to check your own network with two questions: who can you call, and who can advise you? Business is hard, and you need people who will pick you up on the days everything feels like it is crumbling. If the answer is nobody, that is the gap to fix.

## Details

Ask yourself:
1. In your network, do you have people you can call?
2. Do you have people who can advise you?
3. Do you have people who will pick you up on the days you feel down and everything seems to be crumbling?

If not, change your environment: find positive, growth-minded people who have done what you want to do.
