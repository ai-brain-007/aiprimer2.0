## Description

Housing that a council uses for people and families who cannot get a permanent, stable home. Shaun calls it the buzz word in property right now and the strategy he is using to scale in 2025. You take part by supplying properties the council can place these people in.

## Details

1. Local Authority Leasing: lease the property directly to the council for a guaranteed rent over a multi-year term. Also called back-to-back leasing. Zero operations.
2. Private Sector Leasing: lease to housing associations and council approved providers. Also a back-to-back lease with zero operations.
3. Nightly rate scheme: the council pays you a negotiated nightly rate per placement. Higher reward, higher risk, more operations because you manage the tenant directly.

### Also taught as: Temporary accommodation

- When to use: the landlord likes temporary accommodation but is nervous because their mortgage is high
- Sit down together and crunch the numbers
- Offer: your company takes 100% control of the property, looks after the tenant, much of the maintenance and all the payments
- The landlord gets the hands-off experience with a slightly increased rent
