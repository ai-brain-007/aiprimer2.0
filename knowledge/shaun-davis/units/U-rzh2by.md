## Description

Auction purchases must complete in 30 to 60 days, and if you miss it you lose your 10% deposit. Luigi steers his refurbishment students away unless the funds are already sitting there. The safer route is to buy a lot that failed to sell at auction and negotiate your own timeline, such as three months to complete.

## Details

- The pressure: 30 to 60 days to complete and get the keys. The searches and paperwork are usually done, but the time pressure is severe.
- The cost of missing: they keep your 10% deposit.
- Only consider it if you have the funds readily available, or are certain you can raise the finance in time. Hoping to raise it afterwards is very risky.
- Fix: buy something that did not sell at auction and negotiate terms directly, for example 'I need three months to complete'.
- Luigi's stance to students who insist: if you want to risk it, risk it, but his advice is do not do auctions.

## Notes

Luigi's advice to students on the buy-refurbish-refinance side of the academy.
