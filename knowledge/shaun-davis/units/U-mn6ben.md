## Description

Your business cannot grow bigger than the person running it. So the speaker's message, especially to men, is to put more work into yourself than into your business or your job. Grow mentally, spiritually and physically and you become strong enough to carry a bigger business, and everything else falls into place.

## Details

- The rule: work on yourself more than your business, and more than your job.
- The reason: your business cannot outgrow your personal identity. It cannot outgrow who you are.
- What it means in practice: you constantly work on yourself, whether that is mentally, spiritually or physically. See the card on the three areas for the speaker's own examples.
- The payoff: when you work on yourself in those areas, everything else falls into place, because you become a stronger person in all of them and then you have the resilience to handle business.
- The speaker's own lesson: he used to wish for a big business before he had the mindset to handle the stress that comes with one. Growing as a person is what made bigger problems manageable.

## Notes

Given as the speaker's message to the men watching.
