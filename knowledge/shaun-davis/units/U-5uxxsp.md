## Description

Shaun visualised a specific Porsche daily before he bought it. Suddenly he saw Porsches everywhere, even in his exact colour. They had always been there; his mind had not been conditioned to see them. Opportunities work the same way: visualise them and you start spotting them.

## Details

- He wanted a Porsche for a very long time, thought about it and visualised it on a daily basis, and bought it last year.
- Result: his mind was conditioned to see it. He saw Porsches everywhere, including the very specific colour he wanted.
- The point: the Porsches were always there. His mind had not been conditioned to see them.
- Transfer: once you visualise opportunities and success for yourself, on a subconscious level you condition your mind to spot the opportunities.
