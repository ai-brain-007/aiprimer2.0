## Description

Most people think of legacy as the assets and money you leave behind. Shaun thinks of it as the impact you had on people. No one will remember your business or your money, but they will remember the private conversations, the wisdom, the contact you made for them and the help you gave off camera.

## Details

- The common view: legacy means your assets and your money.
- Shaun's view: when you are gone nobody remembers the business or the money you had. They remember the impact you had.
- Impact looks like: private conversations with people, things done off camera as well as on camera to help someone and change their life, a piece of wisdom, a gem you give them, a contact you make for them.
- He describes himself as literally obsessed with this.
