## Description

Social media is useful if you use it for the right things. Shaun's rule is simple: check who you follow, and if you want to be a property entrepreneur, follow property entrepreneurs. Your feed becomes part of your environment and your source of advice.

## Details

1. Understand who you are currently following
2. Decide what you want to become
3. Follow people who already do that, for example property entrepreneurs if that is your goal
- Reason: social media is great if you use it for the correct things, and it lets you learn from people who have been through it
