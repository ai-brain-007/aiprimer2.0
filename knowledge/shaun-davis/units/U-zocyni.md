## Description

Shaun uses a football team to show why the leader, not the team, decides the results. The same players lose every game under one manager and win under another. The difference is the manager's mindset and game plan, so the lesson is to look at yourself first.

## Details

- Picture a team that is losing every game with the same players on the pitch.
- A new manager arrives with a different mindset, a different way of thinking and a different game plan.
- Same players, different results and a different outcome.
- Shaun's takeaway: if his team is not performing, it is never because of the team; it is because of him and his leadership.
