## Description

Everything that happens in your business is your fault, including what your team does. Blaming someone else hands them the power and leaves you unable to fix the problem. Shaun asks what he could have done differently instead, fixes it fast and keeps moving.

## Details

- Everything that happens in the business is the owner's fault. Do not blame the team or the staff. There is always something you could have done to prevent it.
- Why: when you blame people you give the power to them, and if someone else has the power you have no control to fix the situation.
- What to do instead: put the power back in your hands. Say: this is what happened, this is the situation, what could I have done differently? This lets you fix problems quickly and keep it moving.
- Rule of thumb: you can have results or excuses, never both.
- The final tip of the talk is the same idea: develop a no excuses mentality. Success is your duty. No one else is responsible, and if you fail it is only your fault. No more excuses, only action.
