## Description

A serviced accommodation deal is priced on what you hope to earn. A temporary accommodation deal is priced on rates the council agrees in advance. Sean values knowing exactly what each property pays for the next 12 months, because it lets him budget and plan.

## Details

- Serviced accommodation: when you close a property today you are working on projected figures, not actual figures.
- Temporary accommodation: the council gives the rates in advance, so when Sean closes a property he knows exactly what he will receive for the next 12 months, consistent every single month.
- Why it matters to him: financial security. He wants to budget and plan and know exactly what he is getting for a property.
