## Description

Your staff do not turn up to make you rich. They come for their own goals and motives. Sean says a leader's job is to learn those goals and show each person a clear path to them through the business. Do that and they work harder and stay motivated.

## Details

- Starting point: people do not come to work for you. They come to work for their own goals and motives.
- The leader's job, as Sean sees it: understand each team member's goals, and understand how you can help them achieve those goals through the business.
- The payoff: when they can see a clear path to their goals through the business, they work harder and are much more motivated.
- Sean counts this as one of his biggest successes and something he values a lot.
