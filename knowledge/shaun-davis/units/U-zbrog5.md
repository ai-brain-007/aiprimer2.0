## Description

A landlord Luigi spoke to in Dubai bought about six properties in the Marina, watched them fall massively, and years later they are still below what he paid. Social media sells Dubai as doubling your money in a year; the reality is that timing matters, and losses can take much longer to recover than the UK dip Luigi's father lived through.

## Details

- About six properties bought in Dubai Marina, probably near a peak. Prices went down massively and have not fully recovered years later.
- Contrast: Luigi's father bought in the UK, sat in negative equity for a while, and was back up after about three years.
- Both hosts hear horror stories about the 2008 crash.
- Warning: agents on social media portray Dubai as a dream where you double your money in a year. Some developments may deliver that, but the picture is painted far rosier than the reality.

## Notes

Told by Luigi from a conversation with one landlord.
