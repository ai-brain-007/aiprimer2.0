## Description

Pick one social housing strategy and stick to it instead of trying them all. The author credits his seven and a half years of results to tunnel vision on one model. Spreading yourself thin kills results.

## Details

- Social housing is a minefield of strategies and newcomers get confused
- Choose one specific strategy and niche down
- Do not spread yourself too thin or try every strategy under the sun
- Stay tunnel-visioned on your results and goals; that is how you stay in the game year after year and grow
