## Description

Shaun offers his own start as proof that you do not need a head start. He began with nothing, nobody gave him money or worked for free, and the help he did get he paid for. He refuses a victim mentality and says anyone can do it with the will to work, ambition, and the humility to keep learning.

## Details

- Started off with nothing, zero handouts.
- Any help he had was paid for. Nobody handed him a pot of money or worked for free.
- Zero victim mentality.
- What you need: the willingness to work, ambition, and readiness to remain a student.
- If you are where he was, use him as a case study.
