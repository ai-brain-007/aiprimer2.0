## Description

Because business runs in decades, Shaun wants you to plan at that scale. Write down exactly what you want over the next ten years and the level of action it will take. It turns long-term thinking into something concrete you can check against.

## Details

1. Plan for the next decade, not the next year.
2. Set out exactly what you want.
3. Set out the level of action you will need to take to get there.

## Notes

Shaun does not give a format for the plan; the steps are the three things he says to plan.
