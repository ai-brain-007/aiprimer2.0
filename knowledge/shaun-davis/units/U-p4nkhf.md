## Description

People ask why landlords do not deal with the council themselves. The answer is rapport. Councils and providers are businesses that want to save money, so they never offer their best rate to a stranger. You earn the better rate by building the relationship first.

## Details

- Councils and providers will not offer you the best rate immediately.
- They are a business: they want to save their money as much as you want to make yours.
- It is hard to get the better rate out of them until you have built the relationship.
- The relationship is the biggest factor.
