## Description

You do not need dozens of contacts to make this work. One estate agent who trusts you can feed you deal after deal, because agents see properties long before you do. A joint venture where you split the risk and the profit lets you scale far faster than sourcing alone. That is why Shaun spends the days on rapport rather than on listings, and why an agency director offered Sandu exactly that after watching him work.

## Details

- Partner with one estate agent and they can give you lots of different deals. One good relationship can change your life in this business.
- Agents get properties much faster than you can find them yourself, so with one on your side you scale much quicker.
- JV terms in the example: divide the risk, divide the profits.
- Why the director offered it: he liked Sandu's personality, enthusiasm for business and youth.
- Deals from the JV so far: one rejected because the surveyor gave too many repairs; another in negotiation where the landlord would add a stud wall to turn a reception room into a bedroom, expected profit £450 a month.
