## Description

Sean used to picture success as a big house, a flashy car and holidays whenever he liked. Getting them changed nothing. He now says success is not a finish line you cross but a feeling: living on your own terms, providing for your family and team, and being accountable for a positive impact.

## Details

What success feels like to Sean now:
- Being able to live life on his own terms.
- Being able to provide for his family and for his team.
- Being accountable and responsible, and knowing the work makes a positive impact on the community. His organisation houses hundreds of tenants and he describes it as saving and changing lives daily.
- Creating reliable, stable careers where people can thrive and hit their own goals.

What it is not: the house, the car, the holidays. Those arrive, the novelty wears off within months and life goes on.
