## Description

Treat council staff as people, not as an institution. Build relationships with them on purpose and keep them up. That is how you get and keep contracts.

## Details

- Councils are just people; treat them like people
- Build relationships and be intentional about them
- Maintain the relationships over time
- Council staff are very busy, so do not waste their time
