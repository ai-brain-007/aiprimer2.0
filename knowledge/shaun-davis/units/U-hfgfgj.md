## Description

Other people's money arrives faster than your own savings, so put your effort into becoming someone people want to back. Shaun stopped trying to save tens of thousands once followers offered to invest. Sharing your journey is the quicker, authentic route to funding.

## Details

- Saving a deposit from a salary takes years. Raising it from people who trust you is faster.
- Shaun's investors came from people who had followed his journey and understood him.
- So the sequence is: share your journey, build a brand, and raise the money the authentic, quicker way.
