## Description

You do not have to drive two or three hours to look at a property. Shaun uses a company he calls Vubet, one of his game changers, to view properties, attend inspections, supervise maintenance, collect keys and inspect tenanted homes on his behalf. It is cheap and it lets you work in areas far from home.

## Details

What the company can do for you, in the order a deal runs:
1. Property viewings, at a couple of hours' notice. You get a full detailed breakdown: a video, comments on the property and its condition, and lots of pictures, so you have a full picture without travelling. Cost: around 35 to 40 per viewing.
2. Attend the council surveyor's inspection on your behalf if you cannot be there.
3. Help facilitate the maintenance from the remedial report: be on site to make sure the job gets done properly and inspect the work before you book the surveyor back out. Shaun calls this an absolute game changer.
4. Collect the keys from the estate agent or the landlord on your behalf.
5. Do your fortnightly tenant inspections once the tenant has moved in.

Shaun's view: it is cheap, it is affordable and it is a great way of leveraging, especially when the property is two to three hours away. He still recommends attending the council inspection yourself when you can, because that is where relationships are built.
