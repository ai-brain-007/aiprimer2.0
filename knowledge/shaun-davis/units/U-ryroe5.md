## Description

Some landlords have had a bad experience with the council and refuse to hear the word. Remind them that they are dealing with your company, not the council. Give them one person and one contact, and promise to keep them updated.

## Details

When the landlord complains about the council:
"Look, don't worry about that. We'll sort that. You've got one person, one contact, and that's me, and I'll update you."

The point behind it: the landlord's contract is with your company. The council relationship is your problem, not theirs.

Context in the video: a landlord was completely against Luton Council after a bad experience, had held about eight viewings and refused every council tenant.
