## Description

The hardest part of growing a new business is cash flow: you put money in up front and wait to get it back before you can do the next deal. When the council pays an incentive at the start, you skip that wait. That is why this strategy can scale quickly.

## Details

- Most new businesses struggle with cash flow: you pay a lot up front and must wait to pull your initial capital back out before you can scale
- In this strategy the council can pay an incentive up front (in the case study, £9,000)
- Having that cash at the start gives you the upper hand to scale quickly
- Sean says he has many students closing deals this way month after month
