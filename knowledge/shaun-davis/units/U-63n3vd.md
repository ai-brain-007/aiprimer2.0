## Description

Social housing pays the same amount every month, December or June, and you know exactly what you will get. After years in sales chasing the next deal, Shaun did not want to spend his life chasing the next tenant. Stability was the problem he set out to solve, because it unlocks his real goals: time freedom and location freedom.

## Details

- Social housing is simple: you know exactly what you get paid and it is consistent, whatever the month.
- He chose it because sales had meant constantly chasing the next sale, and he refused to spend his life chasing the next tenant.
- The question he asked of every strategy: what vehicle gives me financial stability?
- The stability serves the goals he started with: time freedom and location freedom.
