## Description

Sourcing paid well per deal, but every month Shaun had to find new investors and new properties. He wanted a vehicle where the work is done once and the income keeps coming. That test is why he left sourcing and built a rent-to-rent social housing portfolio instead.

## Details

- The question to ask of any strategy: what vehicle gives me financial stability?
- Sourcing failed the test: it paid £1,500 to £5,000 per deal, but every month you start again finding investors and properties.
- Preferred: recurring income from work done once.
- He describes the vehicle he moved into as safer than rent-to-HMO.

### Also taught as: Sourcing pays once; back-to-back pays every month

- Sourcing: paid once, maybe £3,000 to £5,000, then nothing
- Back-to-back: residual passive income; a 5-year deal paying up to £2,000 per month and beyond from one property
- The author's aim is residual passive income over time
