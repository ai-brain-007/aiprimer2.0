## Description

Beginners get excited and sign, assuming it will be fine. It is not fine when something goes wrong and the landlord points to the contract. One missed clause in 15 pages of jargon can cost you thousands of pounds.

## Details

The mistake:
- Contracts carry a lot of unnecessary jargon, and people can put things in different clauses.
- If you are new to the game it is easy to miss, and you may sign in excitement thinking it will be okay.
- When something goes wrong, the landlord will say: let's look at the contract, what did you sign?
- One missed clause in 15 pages could cost you thousands of pounds.
The fix:
- Read every contract through properly before signing.
- Have someone with experience check it over first; the hosts do this for every deal in their academy.
