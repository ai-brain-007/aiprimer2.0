## Description

Shaun credits his results to enjoying the daily work more than the outcomes. When you love the process, results arrive by default and more organically, and you have the resilience to keep going through hard times. Keep an aim, but find a way to enjoy the road to it.

## Details

- Get yourself to a place where you love the process more than the results.
- Two effects: results happen by default and more organically, and you gain the resilience to push through hardship until you reach the other end.
- There is always an aim, because you need something to strive towards, but you must find a way to enjoy the process itself.
- He is the kind of person who would still find something to do after selling or handing over the business.
