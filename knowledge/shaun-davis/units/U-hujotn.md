## Description

In car sales Shaun expected the people with the most product knowledge to sell the most. They did not. The ones who showed up every day, did the work and did not give up sold the most cars, month after month. His conclusion: talent is optional, showing up daily is not.

## Details

- His assumption when he started: the most talented salespeople, the ones with the most product knowledge, would sell the most cars.
- What he saw: the top sellers, day after day and month after month, were the ones who showed up every day, did the work, did not give up and were not lazy. They were not necessarily the most talented or knowledgeable. They were willing to do the work at all costs.
- Lesson: you do not need to be talented to be successful. You need the principle of showing up daily. He says this lesson has helped him massively in business.
