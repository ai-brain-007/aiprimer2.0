## Description

When Shaun rang his father to say he was about to quit BMW, his dad told him that anything he had ever put his mind to, he had succeeded at, and that he would smash it as a leader. Shaun was already 95 percent decided; the encouragement got him to hand in his notice the same day without a second thought.

## Details

- Shaun called his father from work to say he was thinking of leaving his job right now and asked what he thought.
- Father's reply: anything you have ever put your mind to, you have always succeeded; I believe in you; if you start your own business you will smash it, you are a leader.
- Shaun was already 95% there; the words pushed him over the line.
- He went straight back into work, wrote his notice and handed it in.
- He stresses how important a good relationship with your father is and calls his dad his hero.
