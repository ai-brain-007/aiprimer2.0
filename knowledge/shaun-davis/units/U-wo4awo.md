## Description

Many entrepreneurs will not start until everything is lined up. Shaun says things are never going to be perfect, so waiting for the perfect time means waiting forever. Start with what you have.

## Details

- The mistake: wanting all your ducks in a row before taking action. Shaun sees it in new and experienced entrepreneurs alike.
- The consequence: you wait forever, because things are never perfect.
- The fix: take imperfect action now, with what you have, where you are, with who you know and the knowledge you have.
