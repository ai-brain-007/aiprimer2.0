## Description

Do not build your whole business on one council or one town. Working across several areas and councils spreads your risk. The author recommends it strongly.

## Details

- You can scale within one area, but having multiple areas and multiple councils is a great way of mitigating risk
- The area itself does not matter; go where the demand is
