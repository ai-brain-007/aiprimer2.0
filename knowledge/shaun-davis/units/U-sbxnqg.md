## Description

A VA is a virtual assistant, someone who works for you remotely on admin such as arranging appointments. You can hire from the UK or from overseas, for example the Philippines, Nigeria or South Africa, where rates are far lower. Shaun paid around $3 to $5 an hour and agrees it was a game changer.

## Details

- Meaning: a virtual assistant.
- Where to hire: the UK, or overseas such as the Philippines, Nigeria, South Africa and many other places.
- Cost overseas: typically $3 to $5 per hour, a lot cheaper than the UK.
- What Shaun's VA did: arranged appointments and got tasks boxed off while he worked full time.
