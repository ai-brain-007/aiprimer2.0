## Description

Sandu's assistant had phoned Hart estate agents several times and been told never to call again because they do not do company lets. Shaun walked in, built rapport in minutes, and the same agent said yes and drove them to a viewing. It is his proof that in person beats the phone.

## Details

What happened:
- Sandu's VA had called Hart a few times. Hart said "Don't call us again" and rang Sandu to say they do not do company lets.
- Shaun and Sandu walked in anyway. Friendly greeting, quick pitch: looking for three to four bedroom properties, working with Luton Council, paying max £1,500 for a three bed, do you do company lets?
- Answer: they had quite a few, one at £1,700, and "we do do it". A viewing was arranged the same afternoon for a four bed at £1,500 in LU3, and the agent drove them there.
- The house: two reception rooms, one could become a bedroom, a bit of damp the management team was handling. A high contender.
- Shaun's take: kept it quick, short, sweet and straight to the point; being chauffeured to a viewing had never happened in his six and a half years.
