## Description

Shaun's daily habit. Every day he gets up and asks himself what he can do today to contribute to his legacy. It turns a big abstract idea into one concrete action a day, and for him that action is about helping someone.

## Details

1. When you get up, ask yourself: what can I do today to contribute to my legacy?
2. Answer with something that has an impact on a person, since that is what legacy means to him.
3. Repeat every day.
