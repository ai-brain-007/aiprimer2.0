## Description

The speaker breaks working on yourself into three areas and gives his own routine in each. Mentally he reads, listens to podcasts and pays for mentors. Physically it is the gym, spiritually it is prayer. The point is to be constantly working in all three.

## Details

- Mentally: read books, listen to podcasts, invest in yourself by getting mentors on different topics. The speaker's daily minimum is 10 pages of a book.
- Physically: go to the gym. The other host goes four or five times a week.
- Spiritually: praying is mentioned as the other host's practice.
- The point: work on yourself constantly across all three, because you become a stronger person in all those areas and everything else falls into place.
