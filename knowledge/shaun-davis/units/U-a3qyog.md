## Description

Money only comes into your pot from three places: a job, an investment, or a business. The author calls these the three core ways to make money, and they are also the only three ways to refill the cup once you have spent from it.

## Details

1. Sell your time for money: work in a job.
2. Invest your money and start making passive income.
3. Create value in the marketplace: make a product or service that people need, and people will pay you for it.

The author introduces them first as where money comes from (job, investment, business) and then again as the three things that fill the cup back up.
