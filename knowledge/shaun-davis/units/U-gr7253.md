## Description

The student keeps a direct line to every tenant. When something breaks they phone or text him straight away and it gets sorted. Problems are rare, but the relationship means you hear about them at once.

## Details

- All tenants phone or text the student when something is broken.
- It rarely happens, but when it does they contact him straight away and it gets sorted.
- A second tenant describes the same: raise a problem, hear 'leave it with me', and it is done.
- Shaun frames this as part of building relationships with tenants.
