## Description

A fast way to make a big decision without overthinking it. Write down the best case, the worst case and the likely case. When you look at the worst case on paper it is usually not that bad, and that is what lets you decide and commit. Shaun used it to hand in his notice on the spot.

## Details

1. Do the analysis there and then. Do not let the decision drag.
2. Write down three answers: What is the best case? What is the worst case? What is the likely case?
3. Look hard at the worst case and ask whether it is really fatal. In Shaun's words, it is not the case of going to die.
4. If the worst case is survivable, make the decision and commit to it quickly.
- Why it works: overthinking leads to procrastination and no decision. Seeing the worst case written down takes the fear out of it.
- His example: sitting at work after 18 months in property, he realised being there was costing him money. Worst case: the business fails and he goes back and gets another job in the trade. Not death, not homelessness. That was enough to decide immediately and hand in his notice.
