## Description

Shaun's biggest tip for anyone going online with their property journey is to be authentic. Be the same person on camera as you are in the room, and do not pose as an expert: saying 'this is what I'm up to' is enough. Authenticity is what makes you different. Without it you are like everyone else, and there is no reason to watch or follow you.

## Details

- Document what happens, including the hard parts, and share information as much as you can.
- Share your journey in the most authentic way possible; authenticity is the brand for Shaun, Luigi and their academy.
- Be aware that sharing invites offence and problems in this generation; do it anyway.
- Beginner version: you do not need to come across as an expert. Say 'right guys, this is what I'm up to, this is what I'm doing'. Luigi started that way and it worked.
- The rule in practice: how the hosts are on social media and on YouTube is exactly how they are in person. No difference, the same energy every time, and they treat this as something that will not change as the channel grows.
- Why it works: sharing the truth is what makes people engage. The biggest compliment the hosts get is how authentic they come across.
- The flip side: if you are not authentic you are not yourself, so you are like everyone else. Then nothing makes people want to watch you or engage with you.
