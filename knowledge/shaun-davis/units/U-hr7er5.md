## Description

Never build your business on one council. Councils change their schemes, budgets and leadership quickly, and the market moves too. If your only council stops, so does your income. Spread your deals across several.

## Details

- Have multiple councils on the go, not one.
- The mistake: working with one council and assuming everything is fine. Fix: add more councils before you need them.
- Reason: in business things change very quickly and the market is changing. Do not have all your eggs in one basket.
- Shaun gives this, together with getting a VA, as his first tip for scaling.
