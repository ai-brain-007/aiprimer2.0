## Description

In serviced accommodation you do not set one flat price. A pricing platform reads the market and sets the right rate for each date, so you charge far more when a special event fills your area.

## Details

- Use a pricing platform. The author used one he calls pricing labs.
- It analyses the market and sets the right pricing structure for each specific date.
- In a prime location with a special event on, you can charge three or even four times the usual amount.
