## Description

Many people only look at properties in their own area. The author says that box shuts out huge opportunities elsewhere in the country. The fix is to follow council demand and use systems and outsourcing to run properties at a distance.

## Details

- Mistake: limiting yourself to investing where you live
- Consequence: missing out on huge opportunities around the country
- Fix: focus on council demand; keep a spreadsheet or CRM of councils and average rents; systemise and outsource the management
