## Description

Before Shaun chose property he asked the same question he asks of any business: is there more demand than supply? Property had a long track record of growth and, in the UK, a housing shortage he had heard about his whole life. That gap is what made it worth pursuing.

## Details

- Always look at any business through supply and demand
- Why property passed the test for him:
  - People he heard growing up called property a good investment, so ignoring it would have been ignorant
  - There is lots of historical data showing how property grows
  - The UK has had a housing crisis for as long as he has been alive and needs more properties
  - Many people are on universal credit and councils cannot house their tenants fast enough
