## Description

School made Shaun feel like a failure, yet he always believed he was not operating at his capacity and could do more, be more and help more people. That belief, not qualifications, is what he credits for taking risks like selling cars with no experience. Keep it even when the system tells you otherwise.

## Details

- The belief: I am not operating at my capacity; I can do more, be more, achieve bigger things and help more people.
- Where it came from: he does not know; he says he has always had it, despite not doing well at school and the school system making him feel like a failure.
- How it showed: he took a car sales job with no experience simply because he believed he could sell cars.
- Shaun contrasts this with people who want to start a business but let something hold them back.
