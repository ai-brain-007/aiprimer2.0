## Description

Sean's read of the landlord's mind. Most want a profitable, zero-hassle solution and a long-term, safe and secure home for their asset. Not people in and out every few months but a five-year contract, no headaches, no voids, paid on time every month.

## Details

- Most landlords want a profitable, zero-hassle solution.
- All landlords want a long-term, safe and secure solution for their asset.
- They do not want people coming in and out every couple of months.
- The ideal: a five-year contract, no headache, no voids, paid on time every single month.
- Your pitch has to show how your service provides exactly this.
