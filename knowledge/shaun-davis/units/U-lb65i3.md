## Description

Both hosts feel the UK penalises rather than rewards entrepreneurs: higher taxes, higher staff costs and a stamp duty surcharge that jumped from 3% to 5%. Shaun quotes a podcast claiming 10,000 millionaires leave the UK every year, and sees few reasons to stay when a business can run remotely.

## Details

- Benefits of leaving the UK named: sun, tax benefits, environment.
- Claim heard on a podcast: 10,000 millionaires leaving the UK a year.
- Stamp duty: the surcharge Luigi pays on a purchase went from 3% to 5% of the price, which he calls a slap in the face.
- Business owners are made to pay staff more and are taxed more.
- Shaun's view: in the UK you get penalised for being a business owner and for making a positive contribution; other countries reward entrepreneurs, and the UK is losing good people.

## Notes

The 10,000 figure is second-hand from a podcast the speaker could not name.
