## Description

In temporary accommodation you house one family per property, so you can put one adult from that household on the maintenance account. When something breaks, they ring the maintenance company directly instead of you. You pay for the service, but the calls stop landing on your phone.

## Details

1. A temporary accommodation property holds one family, so the household stays the same. Pick one adult from it.
2. Put that person on the account with your maintenance company for that property.
3. Give them the company's number. For a gas, boiler, electrics, leaky tap or leak problem they call the maintenance company directly, not you.
4. If they need extra assistance, they can call your VA, who is on hand.
5. The maintenance company gets the job boxed off. That is the service you are paying for.

## Notes

Shaun presents this as one of the things he does specifically with temporary accommodation, where there is one family per property.
