## Description

Shaun does not pretend work-life balance is easy. If you want great success, something has to give, and for him it has been relationships. His bet is to work with tunnel vision for a few years while he is young and has the energy, so he can have a lifetime of freedom later.

## Details

- Work-life balance is a tough conversation because something has to give.
- Big success requires big sacrifices. He says honestly that many of his relationships have suffered from building the business.
- His reasoning: he is 32, so sacrificing a few years of tunnel vision now buys a lifetime of freedom.
- Do the hard work while you have the energy and the momentum. Later you do not fully retire, you just stop working so hard.
