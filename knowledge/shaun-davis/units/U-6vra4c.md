## Description

In business everyone has an opinion, and most of it is 'don't do this'. Shaun was told social housing tenants would trash his houses and kick doors in; Luigi was told not to buy during Covid. Both ignored the noise and both are glad they did.

## Details

- Expect a lot of opinions: 'don't do this', 'it's not worth it', 'pull out of the deal'.
- Shaun heard that social housing was not worth it because tenants trash the house, kick doors in and smash windows. He went ahead anyway and it became his main business.
- Luigi was told in Covid not to buy and to pull out of deals he had lined up. He saw no other option: he had quit his job, had five or six properties and was a full-time developer, so he kept buying.
- Judge the advice against your own situation and your own path, not against the fear in the room.
