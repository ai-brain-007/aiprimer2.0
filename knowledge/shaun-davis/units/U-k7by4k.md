## Description

Before you start a social housing business, decide which tenants you want to support. Different tenant types mean very different work, partners and stress levels. Shaun says it is not enough to say I want to do social housing; the first step is understanding the tenant type.

## Details

- Mother and baby
- Homeless
- Low-level tenants: for example anxiety and depression at a low level
- High-level tenants: for example drug addicts, alcohol addicts
- People just out of prison: you work with companies that house them and with probation officers
- Domestic violence victims: a safe house with the relevant support

Shaun's own choice: low level only (low-level anxiety and depression) plus several domestic violence safe houses. Even low level he calls super stressful.
