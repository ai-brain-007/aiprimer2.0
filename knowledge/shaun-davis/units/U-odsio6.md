## Description

Sandu's calls went straight to 'when can I view?'. That is efficient but forgettable, and it does not work on everyone. Open with a greeting and a bit of small talk so the other person lowers their guard and remembers you.

## Details

- Mistake: opening with your name and the request for a viewing time, nothing else
- Consequence: the call feels transactional; you sound like everyone else and are easy to forget
- Fix: say hi and ask how they are, mention something like the weather, bring more energy; it feels out of character at first but it lowers their guard and makes you memorable
- Direct and to the point is fine for some people, but not for everyone; when you want to be remembered, make it less transactional
