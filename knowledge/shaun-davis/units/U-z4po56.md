## Description

Running a business is lonely, and people who do not run one cannot really help you through it. Sean says being part of a community of entrepreneurs and high performers is crucial, and will move you further and faster than figuring it out alone.

## Details

- Loneliness in entrepreneurship is real. Sean felt it while running his business.
- Talking to family and friends is sometimes not the solution: if they do not run a business they will not understand how to help you navigate the problems.
- What works: like-minded people who understand business, so you can work through hardship, cash flow issues and growing pains together.
- Sean started the High Performance Academy four years ago for exactly this reason and wishes he had started it earlier.
- His strongest statement: being surrounded by other high performers is crucial to your success and will propel it further and quicker than suffering in silence.

## Notes

Sean runs a paid community and mentorship, the High Performance Academy, and promotes it at the end of the video, so this advice also serves his own offer.
