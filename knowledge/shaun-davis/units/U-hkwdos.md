## Description

By 2020 Shaun sat at his BMW desk thinking that being there cost him money, even though it paid him. His 60 hours a week would earn far more in his own business. When your business returns more per hour than your job, staying employed becomes the expensive choice.

## Details

- The reframe: the job pays a wage, but the same 60 hours put into the business would bring a much higher opportunity and reward, so the job is a cost.
- Where it came to him: at his desk around 2pm, after lunch when the dealership was quiet and he had time to think about his next move.
- What he did with it: decided in 2020 to leave the job.
