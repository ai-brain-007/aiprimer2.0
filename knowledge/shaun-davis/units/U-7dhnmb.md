## Description

Sean is currently using the Renters' Rights Bill to close landlords. The bill gives tenants more power and, through things like periodic tenancies, less security to the landlord. That makes a long-term, government-paid social housing contract look safer by comparison.

## Details

- Raise the Renters' Rights Bill in the conversation.
- Explain what it means for a private landlord: it gives the tenant more power and does not give the landlord much security, for example through periodic tenancies.
- Contrast with what all landlords want: a long-term, safe and secure solution for their asset.
- Then present social housing's long-term contract, guaranteed rent and zero hassle as the answer.
