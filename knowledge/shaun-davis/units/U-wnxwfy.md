## Description

The mistake Shaun sees most entrepreneurs make: they say they have no time, then spend the time they do have on research and emails. Having little time is fine. The fix is to make sure every hour you do spend in the business is on high value tasks.

## Details

- The mistake: focusing on low value tasks, then giving excuses about not having enough time.
- Why it matters: when you only have one day a week, that day has to move the needle or you stay stuck in the job.
- The fix: accept the time limit, outsource the low value work, and spend your own time only on high value tasks such as viewings, appointments and key calls.
