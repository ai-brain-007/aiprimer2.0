## Description

Most of selling is helping the other side understand exactly what you do. Then you give them confidence that you are the right people for them. Once they have that confidence, the rest falls into place.

## Details

- Educate the landlord or agent: what the scheme is, how it protects them, how it works
- Give them confidence that you are great for them and that they should do business with you
- The landlord in the video bought into the team's confidence and knowledge, not a discount
