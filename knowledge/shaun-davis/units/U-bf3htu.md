## Description

This is your pitch to the landlord in three words: no empty months, no repairs, no hassle. You guarantee the rent and take over running the property. It is why landlords say yes and why you can negotiate favourable terms.

## Details

What the landlord gets when they lease to you:
- Zero voids: the rent is guaranteed, so no empty months.
- Zero maintenance: you cover it.
- Zero hassle and zero headache from running the property.
- Long-term security: a multi-year commercial lease instead of a periodic tenancy.
You take all the hassle away from the landlord; that is the trade for the control you get.
