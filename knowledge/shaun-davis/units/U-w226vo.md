## Description

Eleven years before this interview Shaun stacked shelves at a Co-op for £4 an hour with no qualifications. He drove through the neighbourhood he now lives in wondering what its residents did for a living. The story is his proof that life can change quickly.

## Details

- Then: customer representative at Co-op, stacking shelves, £4 an hour, no qualifications, 11 years ago
- He used to drive down the streets where he now lives and wonder what the people there did for a living
- The difference from most dreamers: he did not leave it at a dream, he found a way to make it happen
- Now: living in that neighbourhood, running a multi-million pound business
- His takeaway: things can change quite quickly
