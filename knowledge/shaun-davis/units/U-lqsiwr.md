## Description

Listen for what has gone wrong for the landlord before, then show how your process prevents it. Michael's first landlord had suffered a nightmare estate agent, so he built his pitch around that. Another landlord had been let down by a previous social housing company, so Michael showed him a property he already ran.

## Details

1. While talking, notice the landlord's pain point. Examples from the video: an estate agent who let the property get ruined; a social housing company that did not look after the property as promised
2. Speak to that exact pain point
3. Show the procedures you have in place to stop it happening again, for example your inspection routine
4. Where you can, show a property you already run and the condition you keep it in
- Result on the first deal: the landlord gave Michael a 4-day head start before showing anyone else, and the deal closed in about 4 days at £990 a month profit
