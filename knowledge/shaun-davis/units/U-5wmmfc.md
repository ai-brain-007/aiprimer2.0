## Description

These are the two problems the Renters Rights Bill created for landlords, and the pain points your offer should answer. First, tenancies are now periodic, so a landlord cannot lock in a long-term let and faces repeated voids and fees. Second, a landlord can no longer evict a tenant unless the tenant breaches the contract.

## Details

What a landlord wants: a long-term, profitable solution from their asset. The Bill removes that power.

1. Periodic tenancies remove the long-term solution.
   - No more 12-month agreements. That is a thing of the past.
   - Constant voids and constantly having to find new tenants.
   - When they let through an estate agent, the agent usually charges a tenant finding fee, so extra costs every couple of months if turnover is high.
   - Worst on HMOs: the market is flooded and saturated with HMOs, so tenants have lots of options and choice.
2. They can no longer evict unless the tenant is in breach of contract, usually arrears. Shaun calls this the huge one.
   - The landlord is stuck with the tenant until the tenant decides to leave.
   - They cannot convert the property, change its use, turn a family home into an HMO or a care home, or move into social housing when their circumstances change.
