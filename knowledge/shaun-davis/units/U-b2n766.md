## Description

Becoming a provider is a form-filling step, not a pitch. The council sends an onboarding form, you complete it, and they run checks that focus on fraud against the company directors because the contract is government-backed. Ask the three core questions before this stage.

## Details

1. Ask the three core questions first: areas of demand, property types, rent settings; onboarding is a later stage
2. The council sends you an onboarding form
3. Fill out the form and you become a provider
4. The council runs checks: mainly fraud checks on you personally and on the directors of the company
5. A history of fraud will make onboarding very hard, because it is a government-backed contract
6. A new company with no turnover is fine; what counts is the value you bring
