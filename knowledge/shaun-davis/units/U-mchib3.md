## Description

The biggest problem Shaun sees in aspiring entrepreneurs: they want success handed to them. The knowledge is free on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok, so you can become an expert in anything. But the recipe only works if you mix it and cook it yourself, and that comes down to how badly you want it.

## Details

- The mistake: wanting success to land on your plate. People have the desire but are not willing to do the work.
- The reality: you can become an expert on anything, because so many people give valuable content on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok.
- The fix: the recipe is there, but mixing it and cooking it is your job. Ask yourself how badly you actually want it.
