## Description

Shaun wants the houses other people skip: ones that need a bit of fixing up. The rent is lower, so the profit on the council rate is higher. Two three-beds at £1,500 that needed work were exactly the bargains he was after.

## Details

- Do not mind doing a bit of work on a property; the profit will be better.
- Look for properties you can fix up a bit, priced accordingly. Two at £1,500 in Luton were called great bargains.
- Balance it against the viewing check: remedial work is fine when it is not too expensive.
