## Description

In Shaun's social housing model the company pays the gas and electric bills, and the tenants do not. That means every hour of heating left on comes straight out of the profit of that house. It is why he cares so much about heating controls.

## Details

- The business pays the gas and electric for the houses; tenants do not
- Heating left on 24/7 therefore has a direct impact on the profitability of that particular household
- This is the reason for fitting timer receivers instead of thermostats
