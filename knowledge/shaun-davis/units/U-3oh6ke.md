## Description

When the offer is built around the landlord's long-term benefits, the close gets easier and cheaper. Shaun says you will not need to pay above market value; if anything you will agree rent below market value, because the landlord is trading a little rent for certainty.

## Details

- Structure the offer around the long-term benefits: guaranteed rent, no voids, no maintenance, no hassle, a company tenant outside the Renters Rights Bill.
- Result at the close: easier agreement.
- Rent level: not above market value; if anything, below market value, justified by the benefits you give the landlord.
