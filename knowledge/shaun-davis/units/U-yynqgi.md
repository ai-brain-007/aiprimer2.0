## Description

Failure is not the opposite of success, it is on the way to it. Shaun used to picture himself in the middle with failure on one side and success on the other. The book Go for No showed him the real line: you, then failure, then success. Every setback is feedback you need to reach the goal.

## Details

- Source: the book Go for No, whose slogan is "yes is a destination, no is how you get there". He highly recommends it for entrepreneurs.
- Old mental picture: failure to the left, success to the right, you in the middle. New picture: you, then failure, then success, all in the same direction.
- Failure is part of the process and part of success. You have to fail your way to success.
- It is in the failure and the setbacks that you learn the most lessons (see the bicep curl analogy and the success is a lazy teacher card).
