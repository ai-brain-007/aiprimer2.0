## Description

On commission with a low basic, Shaun would not spend even in good months. He kept thinking: what if I sell nothing next month, what if the market changes? That fear stopped him enjoying the rewards of his hard work, and it is part of why he wanted income that did not reset every month.

## Details

- Cause: a very low basic wage in the motor trade, so income depended on each month's sales.
- Thought loop: what happens if next month I do not sell any cars, what if the motor trade or the marketplace changes.
- Effect: he did not want to overstretch himself and could not enjoy his hard work, even on holiday.
