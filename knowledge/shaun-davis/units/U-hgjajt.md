## Description

What Shaun says to a landlord or agent who has had a bad social housing experience. He acknowledges the past, separates himself from it, explains how his company is different, and promises what every landlord wants: a profitable, long-term, zero-stress lease.

## Details

Say, in this order:
1. Acknowledge: "Look, I know you've had this issue in the past. I know you've had this experience."
2. Separate yourself: "The key difference is I wasn't a part of that deal. It had nothing to do with me."
3. Present yourself: "This is me. This is my company. This is how we operate. This is how we're different."
4. Protect their asset: "This is how I'm going to be able to protect your asset."
5. Promise the outcome: "You're going to have a nice, profitable, long-term solution. You're going to have a long-term lease with a long-term tenancy. It's going to be zero stress."
