## Description

This is the path of a rent-to-rent temporary accommodation deal from first viewing to a tenanted property, as Shaun walks it. Each step shows what you must do and what a third party can do for you. Knowing the sequence lets you plan your few focused hours around the moments that matter.

## Details

1. View the property. Do it yourself or have a viewing company do it at a couple of hours' notice; they send a video, comments on the condition and lots of pictures for around 35 to 40.
2. If you are happy with it, contact the council. The council arranges a surveyor to come out and fully inspect the property.
3. Attend the surveyor's inspection yourself if you can. This is where you build relationships with the council and the surveyors. If you are not available, the viewing company can attend on your behalf.
4. After the inspection you receive a remedial report. It tells you exactly what you need to do to bring the property up to standard.
5. Get the remedial work done. You organise it on the back end; the viewing company can be there to make sure the job is done properly and can inspect the work before you book the surveyor back out.
6. Once the property passes, close the deal and get the agreement boxed off on the back end.
7. Put a lock box on the property so contractors can come and go without key problems. The viewing company can collect the keys from the estate agent or landlord for you.
8. Once the tenant moves in, inspect fortnightly at first, or even weekly, and taper to monthly and then quarterly as the tenant proves reliable. The viewing company can do these inspections too.
