## Description

Councils have an obligation to house the people on their register, and they do not have enough properties to do it. That gap is why they lease from landlords and from rent-to-rent operators, and why Shaun says temporary accommodation solves a huge problem.

## Details

- Councils have an obligation to house their tenants.
- They do not have enough properties.
- They need properties from landlords, or from people creative enough to build a rent-to-rent business.
- In COVID, Birmingham City Council was inundated with homeless people and needed providers to step up.
