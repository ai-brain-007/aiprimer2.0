## Description

At the end of day one the council emailed a price list showing £1,400 a month for a three bed, not the £65 a night agreed on the phone. At that rate nothing in Luton worked. Shaun calls it the scariest moment of the challenge; they called back and secured the phone rate.

## Details

- The email: a price list saying £1,400 per month for a three bed on a year lease, which is not what the officer had said on the phone.
- Why it mattered: at those rates the deals do not work; a whole day in Luton would have been wasted with the clock running.
- What they did: called her back. The email was just an automated one, and they secured the rate she had given by phone.
- Takeaway: when a written list contradicts what you were told, call the person and confirm before you change plans.
