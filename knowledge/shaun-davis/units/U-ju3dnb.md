## Description

A man lost his job, his partner and his house in Scotland, was signed off with depression and stress, and went to his parents. Two months later they gave him half an hour to pack a bag and leave. The GP sent him to hospital, the hospital was full, and he was told to sleep rough; days of phone calls led nowhere until Urban Homes called back with a place. He came to Birmingham the next day.

## Details

- Lost job, partner and house in Scotland; signed off work with depression and stress
- Went to be supported by his parents; after 2 months they gave him half an hour to get out with a bag
- GP sent him to a hospital; the hospital was full; he was told to sleep rough
- Spent days phoning as many numbers as he could with no result
- Urban Homes called back out of the blue while he was still in Warwickshire and offered a place; Tyler then called and said to let him know if he did not like it because he might have somewhere better
- He came to Birmingham the very next day and trusted the process
- He had no money, no family and no friends at that point; he says the team came in when no one else did
- Moved into his current house the day before his birthday, in what he calls one of the toughest weeks he has had
