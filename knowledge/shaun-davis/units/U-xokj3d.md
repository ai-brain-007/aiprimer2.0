## Description

An HMO, as Shaun uses the word, is a house share. Several tenants rent rooms in one house and share the bathroom and the kitchen. Most of his rooms before COVID were HMOs, which is why lockdown hit him so hard.

## Details

- HMO: a house share where the tenants share the facilities, meaning the bathroom and the kitchen.
- Before COVID a lot of Shaun's portfolio was HMOs.
- Weak point: when COVID happened tenants said they would no longer share a bathroom, a kitchen or living space with strangers, and the model failed in real time.
