## Description

When you sell a deal, the contract must make the investor's responsibilities clear before they sign. Otherwise they can come back six months later, blame you for a deal they failed to manage or tenant, and claim it does not work.

## Details

1. Have the correct contract and clauses in place with the investor.
2. Make sure the investor clearly understands what they are getting into before they sign.
3. Get paid.
4. The contract makes clear that once they have accepted the deal, it is their responsibility.

What this prevents: the investor returning in six months because they have not managed or tenanted the property properly and telling you the deal does not work.
