## Description

People with large rent-to-rent HMO portfolios lost everything during COVID. They bled money for months and then handed every property back to the landlords. The lesson the hosts draw is that a crisis can wipe out a strategy, so the strategy you choose matters.

## Details

- Rent-to-rent HMO for working professionals was their main strategy.
- During COVID they lost money for a long time, then gave all the properties back to the landlords.
- Landlords may have been more forgiving because it was COVID.
- Take-away: elements of a crisis can affect your property business, which is why strategy is so important.
