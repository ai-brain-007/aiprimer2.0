## Description

When an assessment shows someone needs a roof immediately, Shaun's team does not wait for the ideal room. They give the person any available room in one of their houses over the weekend, then move them on the Monday to the house that suits them better. The person is safe tonight and well placed within days.

## Details

1. Read the assessment and the person's conduct. If they clearly need somewhere immediately, act on that.
2. Fit around practical limits: the team leader works Monday to Friday, and this tenant could only travel down from Warwickshire at the weekend.
3. Give a room straight away in whichever house has one, even if it is not the best match, so the person is housed over the weekend.
4. When back on Monday, move them to the house that is a better suited environment for them.
5. Tell the person up front that a better place may follow, so they take the first room without fear of being stuck: "let me know if you don't like that place, I think I might have somewhere better for you."

## Notes

Told by Tyler, the housing team leader, with the tenant confirming.
