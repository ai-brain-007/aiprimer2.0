## Description

When you hand a property to the council or provider under the AP model, they pay you a lump sum per room before any rent comes in. Shaun calls it the biggest game changer of the strategy. It is separate from your monthly profit and solves the cash flow problem most new businesses have.

## Details

- On an HMO basis: about £600 to £1,000 per room, depending on where you are based.
- Paid upfront, separate from the monthly income.
- Examples from the video: seven-bed at £600 per room = £4,200; four-bed at £600 per room = £2,400; two-bed family let = £1,100.
- It is paid again each time somebody moves out (see the separate card).
- Use: keep it separate from monthly earnings, pay bills with some of it, or roll it into the next property.

## Notes

Amounts depend on the area; the figures here are from one council in Greater Manchester.
