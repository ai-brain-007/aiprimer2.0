## Description

Plenty of people talk confidently about things they have zero experience in. If you hand them your money you learn from someone who has not done it. Shaun's fix is to take every claim with a pinch of salt and demand evidence.

## Details

- The mistake: investing your hard-earned money in a teacher without checking they have done the thing.
- Why it happens: many people talk on subjects where they have zero experience.
- The fix: take what they say with a pinch of salt, look at evidence, and run the mentor due diligence checklist before you pay.
