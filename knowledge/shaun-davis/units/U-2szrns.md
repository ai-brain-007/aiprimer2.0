## Description

Shaun runs over 90 rent-to-rent social housing deals and splits them into supported and temporary accommodation. Supported accommodation pays well but has many moving parts, needs people on the ground and brings council audits. Temporary accommodation has no support element once tenants move in, so it is the model he is scaling nationwide.

## Details

- Supported accommodation: Shaun runs it specifically in Birmingham. Very lucrative if done correctly, but a lot of turning cogs: you need many people on the ground, property audits, and working closely with the council can be daunting.
- Scaling supported accommodation nationwide would mean a very large team and a lot of headache; Shaun judges it not worth the reward.
- Temporary accommodation: once the tenant is in there is no support element. It is 100% worth the reward and can be scaled with many different councils around the UK.
- Shaun's plan changed from national expansion via supported accommodation to national expansion via temporary accommodation.
