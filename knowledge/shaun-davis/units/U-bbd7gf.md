## Description

Shaun says closing deals is easier now than when he started in 2018. Regulation such as the Renters' Rights Bill and tenant-favouring changes have made ordinary letting a headache, so landlords want a secure, guaranteed, long-term solution. Social housing gives them that, and when you let to a provider Shaun says none of that regulation matters.

## Details

- Landlords have heard of the Renters' Rights Bill and the many regulations; they do not want the headache.
- When the property is let to a social housing provider, Shaun says none of that matters or counts.
- Landlords want a long-term profitable solution: secure, safe and guaranteed, not periodic tenancies.
- In 2018 it was much harder: landlords did not understand social housing and only saw the negatives, kicked-in doors and smashed windows.
- Now, with the friction of renting to a normal tenant and the rights all on the tenant's side, landlords are fed up, and social housing is the solution they want.
- For Shaun personally, it has never been easier to close deals.
