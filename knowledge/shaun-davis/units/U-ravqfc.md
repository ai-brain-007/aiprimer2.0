## Description

Shaun's figure for how long it takes to remove a tenant who stops paying and refuses to leave a buy-to-let. During that time the property costs you money instead of paying you. It is one of his main reasons for not starting with buy-to-let.

## Details

- Removing a tenant: roughly 6 to 9 months, especially if they refuse to leave.
- While it drags on, a property that made you 400 pounds a month becomes a monthly liability in cash flow terms.

## Notes

Shaun's own estimate from research he did at the time, not a quoted legal source.
