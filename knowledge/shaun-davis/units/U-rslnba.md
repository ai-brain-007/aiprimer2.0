## Description

A simple filter for what to hand off. Sean works out what an hour of his time is worth to the business and outsources any job worth less than that. It forces you to think about your own value instead of doing whatever lands on your desk.

## Details

1. Understand your value to the business. Sean frames this as self worth: what is an hour of your time worth?
2. Set a threshold. Sean's own figure: any job worth less than £500 per hour in his business gets outsourced.
3. For those jobs, leverage: coach and empower your team so they can do the work.
4. Use the time you free up to manage the business and work on it rather than in it.

The £500 figure is Sean's personal number for his business, not a rule he gives for everyone.
