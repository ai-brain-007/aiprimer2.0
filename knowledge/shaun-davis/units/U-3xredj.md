## Description

Shaun returns to this idea several times: failure and hardship are what build character, and you need character to run a business. That is why he treats failing as okay and why he will not hand his children wealth without the struggle that teaches them to keep it.

## Details

- Failure builds character; it is okay to fail
- You need character to run a successful business
- Hardship builds character; take it away and people lose what they are given
- Where he applies it: his own failed side hustles, his father's failures in front of him, and how he raises his kids
