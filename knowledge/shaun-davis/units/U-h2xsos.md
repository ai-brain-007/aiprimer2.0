## Description

What you say to yourself becomes your reality. Tell yourself you cannot and you will not; tell yourself you will succeed no matter what and your mind, even subconsciously, finds a way. During COVID Shaun told himself every day that he would succeed and would not be a failure.

## Details

- What you say becomes a reality. Thoughts become things. It becomes a self-fulfilling prophecy.
- If you tell yourself you cannot do it, that reality comes true.
- If you tell yourself that no matter what happens you are going to succeed and make this work, your mind will find a way, even on a subconscious level.
- His practice in COVID, when he had to let go of the serviced accommodation and HMO business and start social housing: failure was not an option. Every single day he told himself he would succeed and would not be a failure, and he came out the other end on top.
