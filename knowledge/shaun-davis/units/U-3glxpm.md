## Description

Selling is the skill Shaun rates above all others for running a business. He built it in car dealerships and says it transfers straight into property. His rent to rent portfolio, he says, was built on that sales background.

## Details

- Shaun's background before property was entirely sales: cold calling for his father's businesses, then car sales at Ford and BMW.
- He says sales teaches a lot about business and gives transferable skills.
- The rule as he states it: you cannot run a business if you cannot sell; you need to be able to sell.
- Applied to rent to rent: few people were doing it in Wolverhampton in 2018, and with his sales experience he says he was mopping up deals.
