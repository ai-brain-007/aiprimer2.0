## Description

You can make the same simple product sell for more in two ways. First, make its marketing look better so people perceive it as worth more. Second, add another product so the customer gets more real value. Together they can double the price.

## Details

Starting point: a cup of lemonade at a hypothetical £2.
1. Look at the marketing. Raise the value perception by making your stand better, more colourful and more eye-catching so it stands out. People who walk past and see an appealing stand accept a higher price.
2. Create more value by adding an additional product. Divide sweets into bags of 10 and sell the lemonade plus a bag of 10 sweets. Now you can charge £4.

Result: the price has doubled, through value perception (way 1) and value creation (way 2).
