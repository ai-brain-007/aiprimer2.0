## Description

Shaun tells his son the same thing he told himself. The easy route becomes hard later; the hard route now builds character and makes life easier later. He also notes that most people choose the easy route, which leaves opportunities open to those who do not.

## Details

- The rule: if you go the hard route now, your life becomes easier later. The easy route now becomes hard for you later.
- Shaun chose the hard route, leaving a comfortable family business for cutthroat car sales, to build his character.
- He says he has never thrived in his comfort zone and prefers being outside it.
- Applied later: when he sat in a council office and screenshotted four thick folders of HMO licences, no one else was doing it because people want the easy route.
