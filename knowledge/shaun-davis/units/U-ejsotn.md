## Description

In the corporate world, Shaun says, bosses assume people come to work for them and must do as they are told. That does not earn respect, and without care the team only performs when you are watching. Respect is earned, not demanded.

## Details

- The mistake: thinking people come to work for you, and that being the boss means they must do as you say
- Why it fails: it is not an effective way to gain respect; you do not demand respect, you earn it
- The consequence: when you just direct and call the shots there is no care, and the team works only while you are watching
- The fix: earn respect by bending over backwards for your team and helping them reach their goals
