## Description

When you get the council's housing department on the phone, three questions tell you everything you need to source the right property. Where do they want to grow, what property do they want, and what will they pay. With those answers you can go and find a match.

## Details

1. What specific type of areas are they looking to scale in?
2. What specific type of properties are they looking for?
3. What do they pay?

Once answered, find exactly what they need, match the two together and form a partnership.
