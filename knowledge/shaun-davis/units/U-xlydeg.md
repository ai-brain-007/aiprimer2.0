## Description

A tenant in the Moseley house was evicted from a previous home over rent arrears and found it far harder than he expected to get housed again. He had never lived in an HMO, a shared house, and arrived full of prejudices about them, but a year on he calls it the best one and tells the tenants of the other houses on the street so. The story shows who social housing serves and how the experience can change a person's outlook.

## Details

- Evicted for rent arrears; did not realise how hard it would be to find somewhere again
- Had never been in an HMO before and had prejudices about them
- Could not go private because he could not afford it; other options fell through
- Had never heard of Urban Homes before moving in
- A year on he says it is the best one and tells the people in the other houses on the street so, whether they believe him or not
- Living there has given him more weight and balance to talk properly to others; he now tells people "your life's not over" rather than the negative he would have said before
