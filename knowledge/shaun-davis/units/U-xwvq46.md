## Description

The author's template for a letter or email to an HMO landlord. Introduce yourself as a social housing provider, list the benefits, and end with a call to action and your contact details. Make it longer and more compelling than this sketch, but never heavy on jargon.

## Details

Template:
"Hey, I'm Sean. I'm a social housing provider and I'm looking for properties just like yours. I have government-backed contracts, long-term leases, zero voids, zero maintenance, zero hassle that comes with managing the property. So overall, this will be a long-term profitable solution for your asset."
- Include all the features and benefits
- Make it longer and more compelling than the sketch above
- Add a call to action with your name and address
- Not too long, not too much jargon
