## Description

People who call rent-to-rent illegal are judging it as if it were an ordinary tenancy. An assured shorthold tenancy is an individual tenancy for someone's home. A commercial lease agreement is a company let, a different contract with different rules. Mixing the two up leads to wrong conclusions about what is allowed.

## Details

- The mistake: viewing rent-to-rent through the lens of an assured shorthold tenancy agreement. That is an individual tenancy, not a company let agreement.
- Fix: sign, and think in terms of, a commercial lease agreement. That contract makes the arrangement legal and gives you full control to operate the property.
- Shaun's rule of thumb: he is not renting as an individual, he is renting under his company.
