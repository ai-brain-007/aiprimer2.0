## Description

Deals come from rapport, and rapport comes from talking about anything but business. Shaun will spend half an hour on a call with a council or landlord without touching business. That half hour saves a lot of time later, because people who like you offer you more property.

## Details

- Talk about absolutely anything; get them onto a friendship level.
- It works the same whatever you are doing and whoever you deal with.
- Shaun's habit: half an hour on a call with the council or a landlord about nothing to do with business.
- Payoff: that half hour makes you and saves you an awful lot of time; people become willing to offer you more and more properties.
