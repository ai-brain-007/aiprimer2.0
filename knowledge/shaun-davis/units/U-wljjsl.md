## Description

When Shaun explains the two options, he writes them down in front of the agent and leaves the paper behind. Writing holds their attention, because someone who is not listening starts wondering what you are writing, and it stays in their head afterwards.

## Details

1. Book a meeting and sit in the agent's office.
2. As you explain the two options, write them down on paper in front of them.
3. Leave the paper with them when you go.
- Why: a written explanation stays in the head. If you only talk, they may not be listening; when you write, they lean in and think this guy has a point.
