## Description

How Shaun opens a call with a provider found on Contracts Finder. He asks one question, whether they are looking to scale, then positions his company as a social housing business that helps other social housing companies grow by finding them properties through its network. The offer is to be their property-finding stopgap so they can focus on tenants.

## Details

Opening question:
- 'Are you looking to scale?'

Who you are:
- We are also a social housing company, and we work directly with social housing companies to help them scale and find properties.
- We have access to a large amount of properties through our network and we can help you scale.

The partnership pitch:
- We would love to partner with you. We love what you are doing.
- We would love to be part of the solution in finding properties and be that stopgap, so you can focus solely on the service to the tenants and we provide the properties.

Before the call, look at their website so you know what they represent and that it aligns with you.
