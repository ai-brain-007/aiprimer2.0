## Description

Online gurus say you must get up at 5 a.m. to succeed. Shaun says that is a myth. As an entrepreneur you choose your hours; what matters is that the hours you work are productive, not what time the alarm goes off.

## Details

- The claim you will see online: successful people get up at 5 a.m., so you must too.
- Shaun's view: there is no need to get up at 5 a.m. to be successful. The privilege of being an entrepreneur is working your own hours.
- The fix: whatever hours you work, be productive in them and move the needle on the business (see productive vs busy).
