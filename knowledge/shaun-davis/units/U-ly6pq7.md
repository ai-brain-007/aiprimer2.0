## Description

The main deal in the video. Sandu and Shaun turned up late to a direct-to-vendor viewing and the landlord was hostile. By the next morning the council had inspected, a holding deposit was paid and the keys were in hand, and the lease was signed that evening.

## Details

1. Thursday evening: arrive late, landlord cold; he only wants a secure tenancy and to protect his property.
2. Explain the scheme in person; he warms up and asks if you have more properties.
3. Straight after: thank-you message, landlord pack, daily contact.
4. Same evening: call the council, take a surveyor cancellation for 10:00 next morning, confirm with the landlord.
5. Friday morning: surveyor lists light remedial works; landlord gives first refusal for viewing first.
6. Offer a week's rent as holding deposit, ask for the OpenRent listing to come down, agree the start date and rent-free period.
7. Collect the keys the same day; lease sent for digital signing, signed that evening.
8. Following week: works done over the weekend, re-inspection Friday, tenants in the Monday after.
9. Outcome: a pipe burst on the tenants' first day and was dealt with; £500 a month profit for 36 months.

## Notes

Shaun believes it helped that the surveyor recognised him from Instagram or YouTube. Sandu felt stressed and overwhelmed by how fast things moved; Shaun says that pace is normal in business.
