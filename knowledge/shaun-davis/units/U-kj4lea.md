## Description

Nobody can bring you deals if they do not know what you do. When Shaun started in 2018 he went to every networking event and told absolutely everyone. Attention is the thing that turns into income.

## Details

- Tell absolutely everyone what you do. It is the only way you get seen.
- Go to every networking event and put yourself out there; build your brand.
- You need attention. Attention leads to income and money; this is crucial for a business.
