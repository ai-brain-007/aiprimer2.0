## Description

The student's advice to a sceptical beginner: if you are going to do this, do it with your whole heart. It is time consuming at the start and needs a lot of effort. People who dabble, or squeeze it around a full-time job without committing, struggle.

## Details

- Go for it wholeheartedly or not at all; it is not something you can just try at without your heart in it.
- If you do not go for it wholeheartedly you are going to struggle.
- It is time consuming, especially at the start, and you need to put in a lot of effort.
- He has seen people find it difficult while also holding a full-time job.
