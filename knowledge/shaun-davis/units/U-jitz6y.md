## Description

Working for your dad is comfortable: he would never sack you. Shaun says that is the problem. In a corporate environment like Ford nobody spoon-feeds you, there is no favouritism, and the professionalism and training are on a different level. That is where he grew.

## Details

- Family business: because it is your dad, the professionalism and training are different; he would have given you a good talking to but never sacked you.
- Corporate world: no spoon-feeding, no favouritism, nothing given.
- Shaun says he would be the same with his own son, so he does not blame his father for it.
- Why it mattered: the tougher environment is what built him.
