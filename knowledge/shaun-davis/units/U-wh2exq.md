## Description

Sean got the things he thought success was: the house, then a Porsche a couple of years ago. Within a couple of months he felt no different. He tells this to show that chasing possessions is a poor definition of success.

## Details

- Starting out, Sean wanted the big house, the flashy car and holidays whenever he wanted.
- He bought the house. He bought his Porsche a couple of years ago.
- Within a couple of months of having them the thought was: okay, this is it, I am here, I have got it. He did not feel any different.
- The novelty wore off and life went on. That pushed him to redefine success as a feeling rather than a finish line.
