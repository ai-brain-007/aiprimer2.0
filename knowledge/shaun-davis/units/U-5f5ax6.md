## Description

Your first deal is the hardest. Every deal you close gives you standing to close the next one, because you can show landlords the properties you already run. Momentum builds from there.

## Details

- The first deal helps you close the second; the second helps close the next, and so on
- Each closed deal gives you a leg to stand on with the next landlord
- Existing properties become your proof when you pitch new ones
- Michael went from zero to nine deals in three months on this effect
