## Description

In the hosts' academy, nobody signs a deal until a mentor has checked it over. The point is contract safety: the paperwork is where beginners get done over. Get every contract checked before you sign.

## Details

- Academy rule: members cannot go ahead on any deal unless one or both hosts have checked it over.
- Why: the contracts have to be checked so people are safe in the deals they do.
- The hosts have looked over many contracts where people were "proper done over".

## Notes

The rule is stated by the co-host and confirmed by Shaun ("100%").
