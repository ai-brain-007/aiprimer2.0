## Description

When a problem lands, measure it against the bigger things you have already dealt with. The speaker did this on the day of the recording with a staff issue: he told his colleague he would not over-stress because it was a very small fish in a big pond. It is a quick way to keep a problem in proportion.

## Details

- The situation: a problem with a staff member that day.
- What he said to James: I am not going to over-stress about it, I have dealt with much bigger things than this, this is a very, very, very small fish in a big pond.
- Why it works: the more experience you get, the more confidence you get, so past problems become the yardstick for new ones.
