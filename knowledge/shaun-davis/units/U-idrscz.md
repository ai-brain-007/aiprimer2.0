## Description

This is what Shaun says to an estate agent to win their properties. He frames it as two options for the same commission: lease to him and earn it for nothing, or rent to working professionals and carry all the tenant rights, headaches and staff cost. It is a win for the agent, the landlord, the tenant and you.

## Details

Opening: "Look, this is what I do. I do social housing. I'm a social house provider with a local council. I'm looking at scaling. I'm looking at going really big. This is how I can help you. This is how I can make your life easier."

Then the two options:
1. Lease it to me. Zero voids, zero operations, zero risk, because we do not have the same tenant rights as a tenant. Nothing changes for the landlord. Your 12 to 15% stays the same, but now you are paid for doing absolutely nothing.
2. Rent it to a working professional. They have all the tenant rights. Headaches, maintenance, neighbours complaining. You carry that burden for the same 12 to 15%, which means more staff, more manpower, more time and more energy. It is 100% not passive.

Close: why would you want to work harder? It is a win-win-win: the agent gets paid, you get paid, homeless people are housed and the landlord is happy.

## Notes

The 12 to 15% is the management fee he says many agents in Birmingham charge landlords.
