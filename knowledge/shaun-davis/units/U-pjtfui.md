## Description

In social housing, councils and providers are too busy to build relationships with you by email. Shaun's answer is to go where they already are: social housing events, wherever in the country they happen. Travel, book a hotel and treat it as the price of survival.

## Details

1. Accept that councils and providers are busy and hard to reach the normal way.
2. Think outside the box: where can you meet these people and get to know them?
3. Go to social housing events and get where these people are.
4. Travel across the country if you have to. Book a hotel or an Airbnb.
5. Do whatever it takes; your business only survives if you are willing to.

Result for Shaun: contacts with people all over the country, which he says contributed to his success.

## Notes

Given as a social housing example, but the same logic drives his whole relationship habit.
