## Description

A provider from Contracts Finder replied asking how you heard of them and which councils you work with. Shaun's answer gives just enough, avoids inventing a track record, and moves the conversation to a call, because you never close in an email.

## Details

The reply, in substance:
1. "We're property investors and we're new to this local area."
2. "We have access to a lot of properties."
3. "We'd love the opportunity to book a call with you to establish exactly what properties you're currently looking for and where you need them. We can then go out with our network and our resources and find the specific properties, and hopefully we can work together."
4. "Are you free for a call at some point today to discuss further?"

On the question "what councils do you work with": do not dodge and do not invent. Say you are property investors new to the area.

After the call: establish their needs and make sure there is synergy. If they say they are desperate, propose a face-to-face meeting. Email is for finding out more information, never for closing.
