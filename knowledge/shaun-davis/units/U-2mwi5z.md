## Description

Go where councils need housing, not just where you happen to live. Temporary accommodation is easy to systemise and outsource once a tenant is in, so distance is not the barrier people think it is.

## Details

- Do not limit yourself to investing where you live; that mindset misses opportunities around the country
- Temporary accommodation is easy to systemise: once a tenant is in, there is no support to provide, only management of the property, and that can be outsourced
- Research councils first (see the council spreadsheet card), then scale in the location that makes the most business sense
