## Description

Every council has an HMO department, and its website carries a public register of licensed HMOs. Licence holders must show their name and address, or their company's. That register is a ready made list of landlords you can contact with a guaranteed rent offer.

## Details

1. Note that councils and housing associations sometimes need HMOs for temporary accommodation.
2. Every council has an HMO department with a website.
3. On the website, find the HMO public register.
4. Once a landlord gets a licence, they have to display their name and address, or the company name and address if it is registered to a company.
5. Acquire this database of licence holders.
6. Market your services to these landlords, offering guaranteed rent.

Shaun has used this for 6 and a half years and says it dramatically helped grow his business.
