## Description

Shaun is not giving his children money or assets. He gives them knowledge, a foundation and a framework, then waits for them to bring him ideas. Together they map a game plan and he invests, but the child runs it while he coaches from the background.

## Details

1. Teach them the knowledge; give them the foundation and the framework to work with
2. Wait for them to come to you with what they have: "Dad, look, this is what I've got. These are the ideas."
3. Map out the game plan together
4. Invest in the idea and back them 100%
5. They run it and steer the ship
6. You stay in the background to help, support, coach, empower and mentor, making sure they are going in the right direction
- Why: it is far more powerful than handing over a bunch of money and assets, which they end up losing because they never had the hardship
- The aim: when he is gone, he can say hand on heart that they can steer the ship and pass it on to their kids

## Notes

He is open that he wants his kids to enjoy the lifestyle he works for; the point is that they must build the skill set alongside it.
