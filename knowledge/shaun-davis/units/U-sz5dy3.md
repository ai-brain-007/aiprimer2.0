## Description

Shaun's answer when a provider asks how you work. Keep it simple: you find out what they want, what property types they need and what they pay, then you find a property within three to four weeks, present it, and hand over the contract. Explaining it plainly makes it easy for them to say yes.

## Details

When they ask 'how do you work?', say:

- We offer a bespoke service. The way it works is very simple.
- We find out exactly what you want: what specific types of properties you need and what you pay.
- Then we go out and find a property, within 3 to 4 weeks.
- We present it to you and give you the contract, and the deal is done.

Shaun says calling it a bespoke service sounds better to the provider.
