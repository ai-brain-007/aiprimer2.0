## Description

Pick a business model that pays you whatever the economy does. After COVID the author's test for any strategy became: is it recession proof, free of peaks and troughs, and stable regardless of what happens? Temporary accommodation passed.

## Details

- What he wanted: recession proof, not full of peaks and troughs, safe regardless of what happens with the economy, the utmost financial stability.
- Serviced accommodation failed this test in COVID.
- Temporary accommodation passes it because the rate is set by the council and backed by the government, so income does not stop in a recession.
