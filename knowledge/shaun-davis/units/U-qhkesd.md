## Description

Five strangers sharing one kitchen leads to 6 a.m. calls about stolen butter. Put cupboards, a small fridge and, where you can, a sink in each room, so tenants keep their own food in their own space. It saves you a headache.

## Details

- Fit cupboards in each room as much as possible
- Give each tenant space for their own fridge in their room
- Where the layout allows, add a sink and a bit of kitchen space in the room
- Built-in wardrobes make things simpler and use the space well
- Reason: with five people sharing one kitchen you get early-morning calls about stolen food
