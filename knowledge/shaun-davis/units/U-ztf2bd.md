## Description

An HMO is a house with several unrelated people living in it, each usually renting a room. In this model the incentive is paid per room, so an HMO with more rooms brings a bigger upfront payment, but you also carry the bills.

## Details

- Expanded in the video as house multiple occupancy.
- On an HMO basis the incentive is about £600 to £1,000 per room depending on the area.
- With an HMO your profit is the rent gap minus the bills.
- Example in the video: a seven-bed HMO and a four-bed.
