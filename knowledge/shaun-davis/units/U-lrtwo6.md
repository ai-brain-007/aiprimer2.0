## Description

Shaun's first social housing deal came from a message in a WhatsApp group he almost never checked. A provider wanted a six-bed in Handsworth, and he had exactly that property on pause. He sourced it to her for a fee, and at the viewing a council officer explained how simple social housing really is.

## Details

1. Before the message: working from Tai's room in his small old house, 10 to 15 hours a day, calling councils and telling himself he would not eat until he got a yes.
2. A social housing provider posted in a WhatsApp group that she was looking for a six-bedroom property in Handsworth.
3. He had a six-bed in Handsworth he was meant to close the week before for working professionals and students. He had paused it and the landlord agreed to hold it.
4. Rather than take it on himself, he sourced it to her for a fee, because cash flow was what he was struggling with.
5. At the property he met a council officer who took to him, explained social housing in simple terms and said: why don't you do it, contact that person, contact this, do this, do that.
6. He realised he had been overthinking it the whole time. That one conversation changed his life.
7. He then called the council and became a provider himself.
