## Description

When the business starts earning, the temptation is to pay yourself all of it. Shaun's father taught him not to: the business needs time to breathe and grow. Shaun kept his job, took no money from the £6,000 a month his deals produced, and reinvested everything.

## Details

- The mistake: I am now making £5,000 a month, so let me withdraw £5,000 a month into my own pocket.
- Why not: the business needs time to breathe and time to grow.
- What Shaun did: the business made £6,000 a month in 2018 and he did not take a penny; he reinvested it and lived on his salary from BMW.
- Precondition: he kept working and earning decent money so he never had to dip into the business.
- Source of the rule: his father.
