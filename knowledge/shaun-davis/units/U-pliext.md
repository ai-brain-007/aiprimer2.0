## Description

A market signal Shaun read during lockdown. Hotel chains such as Travelodge had no guests and were being used to house homeless people. For him this proved the size of the demand for housing and pointed to where his rooms could go.

## Details

- During COVID there was a massive need to house people, especially the homeless.
- Hotels like Travelodge stopped having guests and were housing homeless people instead.
- Shaun reads this as evidence of how big the demand was, and as the reason he felt led to help.
- Homeless people were also calling his office directly, which confirmed the demand on his side.
