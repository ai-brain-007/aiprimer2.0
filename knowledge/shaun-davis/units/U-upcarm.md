## Description

Shaun hears people say they skipped an event because it was on the other side of the country. His reply: how badly do you want success? One contact at that event could be a life-changing opportunity, and seen that way the trip is always worth it.

## Details

- The mistake: not going to a networking event because of the distance.
- The question to ask yourself: how badly do you really want the success?
- The reframe: at that event you could make one contact that gives you a life-changing opportunity.
- Looked at through that lens, the event becomes worth going to.
