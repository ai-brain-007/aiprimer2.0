## Description

Most people lose hours every week without noticing. Shaun names the culprits: scrolling, reacting, thinking instead of doing, and trying to handle everything yourself. Then they say they have no time for property. The hours were there. They went to the wrong things.

## Details

- Where the hours go each week:
  - Scrolling
  - Reacting
  - Thinking instead of doing
  - Trying to handle everything yourself, which Shaun calls a common habit of new entrepreneurs
- The consequence: you lose hours every single week, then tell yourself you have no time for property.
- The fix: add structure and leverage. Spend your hours on high value tasks and hand the rest to a VA or a third party.
