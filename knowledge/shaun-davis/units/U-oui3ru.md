## Description

You cannot simply buy a property together with a business partner in Dubai the way you might in the UK. With a mortgage you must be related or own a business together, and a business mortgage needs six months of income in that business. Luigi's workaround would be deal sourcing for a fee.

## Details

- Two unrelated people cannot buy one property together with a mortgage.
- Options: be related, or hold the property through a business you share.
- A business mortgage requires six months of proven income in that business.
- Alternative Luigi is considering: source deals for others and charge a big fee, which many people there do.

## Notes

Luigi's understanding after six weeks in Dubai; check the current rules before relying on it.
