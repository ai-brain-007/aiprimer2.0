## Description

Three weeks after Shaun quit BMW, COVID hit. After two hours on hold he was told there was no furlough for someone who had just registered self-employed, only Universal Credit at £75 a week. He turned it down and decided to work his way out. This is the moment his back went against the wall and the social housing business began.

## Details

- Everyone at BMW was furloughed; a few more weeks and Shaun would have kept his wage.
- He assumed that having worked since 16 as a good taxpayer, HMRC would pay his wage too.
- He waited about two hours on the phone.
- He explained he had just registered as self-employed, started his business and it had failed, and asked for the furlough money everyone was talking about.
- The answer: Mr Davis, unfortunately there is no furlough for you. Because he had only just registered self-employed, there was no taxable income on record, so no support.
- The only option offered was Universal Credit at £75 a week.
- He refused it: £75 would not scratch the surface of what he was used to earning, and in his words it could have put him in a box.
- Result: back against the wall, no other way out, so he decided he had to figure it out himself.
