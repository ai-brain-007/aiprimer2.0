## Description

Shaun tells students not to obsess over a huge margin on each property. On the AP model every new deal adds its monthly income on top of what you already earn, so modest margins across many properties grow into a large, steadily rising income.

## Details

- It is not about making a massive profit on every single property.
- Every time you add a property, its money goes on top of what you already earn, on a monthly basis.
- So you are consistently growing.
- The student says this was the massive appeal for him.
