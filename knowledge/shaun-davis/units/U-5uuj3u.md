## Description

Michael had no idea how to become a provider before he was mentored. A mentor who has done it stops you making costly mistakes and shows you what a deal should look like. The mentor only works if you are willing to do the work yourself.

## Details

- Before starting, Michael had no knowledge of how to get into temporary accommodation
- Every time he did not understand a deal, he called his mentor and they crunched the numbers together
- The mentor, with over 100 units, could say what a deal needs to look like; Michael has lost no money across nine deals
- Benefits named: fewer costly mistakes, less time wasted, knowing who to speak to and what to say to councils and landlords, faster scaling
- Advice to newcomers: get a mentor who shows you every step from A to Z, and be willing yourself; the mentor cannot want it for you

## Notes

Shaun is Michael's mentor and runs the programme, so this is also a promotion of the author's own service.
