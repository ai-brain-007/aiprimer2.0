## Description

Being a high performer is not a one-off achievement. It means getting a little better every day, in yourself and in your business. Shaun and Luigi treat their own conversations as a way to catch what they should be doing better.

## Details

- Level up daily; get better every day.
- Reflecting on a conversation and noticing what you should not have said or done is part of that.
