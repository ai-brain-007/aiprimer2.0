## Description

These are the numbers Luton Council gave in June 2025 for temporary accommodation. You use them to work out whether a rental in the town leaves a profit. They are not negotiable and every agent gets the same.

## Details

- Property needs: three and four beds, anywhere in Luton.
- Nightly rates: £65 a night for a three bed, £75 a night for a four bed.
- Rates are not negotiable; the same for every agent.
- Monthly equivalent used by Shaun: £65 x 30 = £1,950 for a three bed.
- Demand: they would take 300 three beds if they had them.
- Bills: the tenant pays utilities; the landlord is responsible for repairs.
- Monthly rates Luton had quoted Sandu earlier: two bed £1,170, three bed £1,400, four bed £1,600, five bed £1,700.
- An automated emailed price list later said £1,400 per month for a three bed on a year lease; on calling back, the nightly rate quoted by phone was secured.

## Notes

Luton-specific figures from one phone call; other councils quote differently. Northampton and Luton had earlier told Sandu they were not doing it or the rates were poor.
