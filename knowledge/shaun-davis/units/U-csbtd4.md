## Description

In a negotiation, promise less than you will deliver, then deliver more. To do that you first need to know exactly what your service is worth and what value you bring, so you never oversell just to win the deal.

## Details

- Do not overpromise and underdeliver; underpromise and overdeliver
- It applies in the negotiation phase of a deal
- It requires understanding your worth and the service and value you bring to the table
- For the author that value is being a social housing provider with more than one exit for the asset (supported accommodation and temporary accommodation)
- A lesson from seven years in the business
