## Description

A gem from Luigi's grandmother: shy people get nothing. He is naturally introverted, but forcing himself to start a YouTube channel and Instagram opened doors, from collaborations and sponsorships to investors and partners. If you are holding back on a channel or social platform for your business, go for it.

## Details

- The rule: shy people get nothing.
- Application: put yourself out there even when it feels uncomfortable. Luigi had been too shy and too introverted, told himself to forget that, and started his YouTube channel and Instagram.
- What the channel brought: doors opened, collaborations, opportunities, sponsorships, investors and partners. In his words, there is loads you can do with it.
- The pull for the other host: he had been saying for ages that he would start a channel but kept asking himself whether he really had the time. Watching the doors open for his friend, and seeing how much value viewers get from the videos, made him decide he needed to do it now.
- Anyone thinking about starting a channel or social platform for their business and holding back should just go for it.
