## Description

Most people dream of a big house and stop there. Shaun believes in manifestation: you have to feel it, see it and picture yourself in it. But the part people skip is acting on the vision. Vision plus action is what turned his daydream into his home.

## Details

- Most people have visions of the life they want but never take the action to get it
- Shaun had the visions and the imagination to see himself there, and did not stop at the dream; he looked for a way to make it happen
- Manifestation as he describes it: feel it, see it, and vision yourself in it for it to become a reality
- Then take action; that is the step people do not do because they feel it is out of reach
- His belief: nothing is out of reach
