## Description

Shaun earns respect in two moves: he bends over backwards for his team, and he works out what each person wants and where he fits into that. When a team member can see the value you create for them, they work harder because you both have a stake in the outcome.

## Details

1. Bend over backwards for your team
2. Get a clear understanding of each person's goals and motives and what they are looking to achieve
3. Work out how and where you can position yourself in helping them get there
Why it works, from a business point of view:
- If a team member clearly sees the value you create for them and how you help them achieve their goals, they end up working harder
- You both have a vested interest: they work for you, you help them reach their goals
- It is a win-win: everyone is winning and everyone is motivated, instead of you just directing and calling the shots
