## Description

In 2019 the author pitched a social housing plan to Wolverhampton City Council, talking about numbers and how he would acquire houses, and got nowhere. When he rewrote the pitch around safeguarding, procedures and policies, they took him seriously and he became a provider soon after. The lesson: councils want delivery and compliance, not a sales pitch.

## Details

- 2019, Wolverhampton City Council, his local council at the time
- He did not yet know what social housing really involved
- First pitch: his ideas, how he would acquire houses, the numbers, how it would benefit the council
- Council reaction: they did not want a pitch; they wanted his delivery, compliance and competence, so they did not give him the time of day
- Second pitch: safeguarding, what happens if a tenant calls late at night, procedures and policies to keep the house to a high standard
- Result: they saw someone taking it seriously who would not cause them stress, and he became a provider very shortly after
