## Description

Shaun speaks to young entrepreneurs because giving value fuels him, the way using energy builds energy. He is open that it also serves him: it is a great way to build a personal brand. Both sides gain, and the value trickles down as the audience grows.

## Details

- The rule: to gain energy you use energy; to become more valuable you give value
- What he gets: energy from new entrepreneurs who are motivated, inspired and asking the right questions
- The selfish side he admits: speaking is a great way to build your personal brand
- The other side: people get lots of value and grow, a trickle-down effect
- Where he applies it: speaking engagements such as the Rich Young Minds event at Birmingham City Football Club
