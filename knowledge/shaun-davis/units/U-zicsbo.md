## Description

The steps for the middleman route. Learn what a provider wants, find a property that fits, agree a rent with the landlord below what the provider pays, then connect the two and keep the difference.

## Details

1. Call a provider and ask the three core questions: which areas, which property types, what rent they pay
2. Get a clear understanding of what they need
3. Go out and find a property that matches
4. Negotiate a rent with the landlord that is lower than what the provider will pay
5. Marry the two together: lease from the landlord, lease on to the provider
6. Your profit is the gap between the two rents; you do not manage the property or the tenants
