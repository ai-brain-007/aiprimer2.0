## Description

Shaun started out renting to ordinary professionals. It worked but brought headaches, mainly tenants not paying, and UK tenants have a lot of legal protection. During COVID he saw homeless people on one side and his empty houses on the other, and switched to housing them.

## Details

- Started with the usual professional tenants; it was good but came with headaches
- The headaches: tenants not paying, and tenants having a lot of security in the UK
- The turning point was COVID
- He applied his rule (create value, solve problems) and asked what he could solve
- There were lots of homeless people and he had all these houses
- That is how he got into social housing
