## Description

Send this to the landlord as soon as you leave the viewing. It thanks them, states clearly that you want the property, and opens the door for daily follow-up.

## Details

"Hey Dave, thank you for your time. Really appreciate you showing us the property. It's perfect for us. We definitely want the property, it's perfect for what we need it for, and we'd love the opportunity to do business with you."

Follow it with: "When we leave, I'll send you a pack." Then keep in touch every day.
