## Description

Set up a business landline and a call answering service before your first lead calls. Someone always picks up, the call is recorded, and you get the details to call back when you are free. It makes a one-person business look like a team, which reassures providers and landlords.

## Details

1. From day one, get a landline number for the business.
2. Sign up to a call answering service. Sean uses Soho 66 and has for years; there are other companies.
3. The service answers calls on your behalf, records every call and collects the key information.
4. They send you a voice recording of the conversation so you can call the lead back when you are ready.

Why it matters:
- Most people starting out still have a job and cannot answer the phone all day
- Once you market, people will call; if nobody answers, you look unprofessional
- Even a small company looks like it has several people, which subconsciously builds confidence with providers and landlords
- Start as you mean to go on: set the back end up properly at the start

How fast you respond to queries, and how professional you look while doing it, makes a big difference to whether deals close.
