## Details

- Background: corporate career at BMW before property; 18 months in business when COVID hit; age 32 at the time of the interview.
- During COVID: just under 100 rooms; around 40% of tenants not paying; no Airbnb guests.
- Now: founder of Urban Housing with a portfolio of over 600 rooms used for social housing, built over the last four years.
- Target: 1,000 rooms, which the interviewer expects within the next couple of months.

## Notes

The 600 rooms, the four years, the BMW background and the timing of the 1,000 target are stated by the interviewer, not by Shaun.
