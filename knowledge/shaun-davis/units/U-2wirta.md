## Description

Before choosing where to work, track every council you contact and what the local rent is. Comparing them shows which area is most profitable, and that is where you scale.

## Details

1. Make a spreadsheet of all the councils you have contacted, or set up a CRM system
2. Add a breakdown of the average rent in each area
3. Compare them: see which council is most profitable and makes the most business sense
4. Scale in that location
