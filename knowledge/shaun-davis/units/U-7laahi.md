## Description

Besides the three leasing strategies there is a fourth way to make money from the same contacts. You find a provider, you find a landlord whose property fits, and instead of leasing it yourself you charge the landlord a sourcing fee for the introduction.

## Details

1. Find a provider and learn what they need.
2. Find a landlord with a matching property.
3. Introduce them and charge the landlord a sourcing fee.
- Students of the academy are doing this alongside the three core strategies.
- Compare with back-to-back leasing, where you keep the property on lease and earn monthly instead.
