## Description

After the first sourced deal felt easy, Shaun kept sourcing and did about 30 deals in total. After lockdown many people wanted to start rent-to-rent HMO businesses, and he sold them the deals while he moved into a safer vehicle himself. He never treated sourcing as a business, and he undercharged on some.

## Details

- He fell into it by accident: the first sourced deal made him think he had earned a large fee just by having a conversation and solving a problem, so surely he could do it more.
- He built a good understanding of how to find and negotiate good deals. His view: if you can find good deals and negotiate good deals, you are set.
- Post-lockdown demand: people who had time to think wanted to start rent-to-HMO businesses. He no longer wanted rent-to-HMO himself, but offered to source them a deal.
- Total: nearly 30 deals, around the 30 mark.
- Fees: some £1,000, some £1,500, some £2,500, some £3,000. He says some were lower than they should have been.
- Why he stopped: it was a stepping stone that did not give him financial stability.

## Notes

The first sourced deal is quoted as £25,000 by Shaun and 22K by the interviewer; the transcript is unclear.
