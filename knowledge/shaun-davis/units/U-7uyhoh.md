## Description

To show that rent-to-rent is an ordinary business model, the author points at Premier Inn, Travelodge and the high street. Those businesses rarely own their buildings; they sign long commercial leases with landlords and developers. Rent-to-rent does the same on residential property.

## Details

- Premier Inn, Travelodge and the big chains do not own their properties; they find a landlord or developer and sign a long-term lease.
- Most high-street shops are not owned by the business trading in them; they are leased under commercial lease agreements.
- The author signs the same type of agreement, just on residential properties.
