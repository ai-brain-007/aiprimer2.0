## Description

Once a tenant moves into temporary accommodation, inspect often at first and less often as trust builds. Shaun starts fortnightly or even weekly, then steps down to monthly and quarterly once the tenant has shown they cause no issues. The taper saves you time and money without losing control early on.

## Details

1. Start: inspect every fortnight, or even every week, as soon as the tenant moves in. Shaun highly recommends this on temporary accommodation.
2. Once you have built a relationship with the tenant and can clearly see they are great and cause you no issues, move from fortnightly to monthly.
3. Then move from monthly to quarterly.

- The taper saves a lot of time and money.
- The inspections can be outsourced to the viewing company.
