## Description

Social media shows the lifestyle of a property investor. It does not show the work behind closed doors that keeps the business running. The speakers want you to know that this hidden part is a lot, and that you need resilience to carry it.

## Details

- What people see: the lifestyle, and whatever else they want to see on social media.
- What they do not see: what the operators have to do behind closed doors to keep the cogs turning and run the business successfully. The speaker says it is a lot.
- Both speakers wish they could show more of the real stuff that goes on, but confidentiality stops them.
- What it demands of you: resilience, and a way of pouring back into yourself so you do not burn out.
