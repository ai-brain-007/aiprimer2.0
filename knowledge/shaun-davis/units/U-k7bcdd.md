## Description

Michael's company is just over a year old and has no filed accounts, yet landlords sign with him and none have credit-checked his company. If you build rapport, the trust sits in you, not in your company, its accounts or a credit check.

## Details

- Build rapport correctly and landlords will not ask about your accounts
- None of Michael's landlords have credit-checked his company
- Be open and honest: Michael tells landlords he has been operating for under a year
- Back the honesty with proof from the properties you already run
