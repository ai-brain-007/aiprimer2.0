## Description

How Sean keeps temporary accommodation operations minimal. When a tenant calls with an issue, he or a VA makes one phone call and a partner maintenance company deals with whatever it is. That system is what gives him location and time freedom.

## Details

1. Tenant calls with a query.
2. Sean, or a VA, makes one phone call.
3. Whatever the maintenance issue is, it is boxed off by the maintenance company his business has partnered with.

Why it matters: with tenants staying months or years and maintenance handled by one call, operations stay very minimal, and Sean gets the location and time freedom he wanted.
