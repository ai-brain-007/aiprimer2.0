## Description

Why leasing appeals to owners more than selling. Shaun says most landlords did not get into property to flip it; they hold because property tends to double in value every ten years on average. A lease lets them keep the asset while someone else deals with tenants.

## Details

- Most landlords did not get involved in property to flip it.
- They want to hold because, as Shaun puts it, most property doubles every 10 years on average.
- So an offer that lets them keep the property and still get a long-term profitable return fits what they want.
