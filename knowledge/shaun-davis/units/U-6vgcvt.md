## Description

Struggles and obstacles are not just something to survive. Shaun says you need them, because they build the capacity, mindset, resilience and leadership skills that a bigger business demands. Without them you could not run the business you dream of.

## Details

- Shaun's reasoning: when he started, he dreamed of a business of today's size but did not yet have the capacity, mindset, resilience or leadership skills to manage it.
- Growing through the struggles is what now lets him run the business more efficiently and lead better.
- He describes the last five years as an emotional roller coaster with lots of tough times, and treats that as the price of growth.
