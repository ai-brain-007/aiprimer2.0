## Description

Shaun wanted his own car business but did not know the systems or processes. So he went to work in a car dealership for a few years to learn how it works and build his leadership skills, planning to start his own later. He calls joining Ford the best decision he made.

## Details

1. Decide the business you want to own.
2. Admit what you do not understand yet. For Shaun it was the systems and processes.
3. Go and work inside an established company in that industry for a few years.
4. Learn exactly how it works, the systems and all the processes, and build your leadership skills.
5. Then go and start your own.

Shaun left his father's business in 2014 to work at Ford with this plan.
