## Description

From Brian Tracy's book Goals, Shaun takes a three-step recipe: be clear on what you want, work out what it will cost you, then get busy paying that price. His own version is to understand the sacrifice, do it, and stay consistent until it pays off.

## Details

Brian Tracy's steps as Shaun quotes them:
1. Work on your goal: have a clear understanding of what you want to achieve.
2. Work out the price you will need to pay.
3. Get busy paying the price. Shaun calls this his favourite part.

Shaun's own reading of it:
1. Understand what sacrifice you will need to make.
2. Just do it.
3. Stay consistent and run the course of the game.
4. Only then will you succeed.

## Notes

Source named by the author: the book Goals by Brian Tracy.
