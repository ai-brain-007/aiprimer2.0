## Description

The price you pay for not knowing what you do not know. The example is a student who spent a year going nowhere alone, then closed within a month once shown the steps. Shaun links it to a plain test: if you are not making £5,000, £10,000 or £15,000 a month, there is a reason.

## Details

- Ignorance tax: what it costs you when you do not know what you do not know.
- The hosts call this a scary thing.
- The example is the student who tried on his own for a year, had good conversations with councils but did not know how to close, and closed within a month of getting step-by-step guidance.
- The remedy offered is to copy the proven formula rather than work it out alone.
