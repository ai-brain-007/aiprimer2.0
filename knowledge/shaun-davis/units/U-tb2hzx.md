## Description

Shaun's own motto, and his cure for waiting until everything is ready. Massive: go big. Imperfect: start with what you have, where you are and who you know. Relentless: play to win, not to take part. The energy of someone obsessed is also what makes others want to do business with you.

## Details

- Massive: the action needs to be big. There is no point going small; go big or go bigger.
- Imperfect: entrepreneurs, new and old, like to have all their ducks in a row before they act. Things are never going to be perfect, and if you wait for the perfect time you will wait forever. Take action with what you have, where you are, with who you know and the knowledge you have.
- Relentless: there is no point playing the game if you do not want to win it. The point is to win, not to take part, and winning takes relentlessness and obsession. Grant Cardone: "Be obsessed or be average." Live and breathe your business.
- Why it pays: people want to work with you and do business with you when they can see your energy and that you are a true student of the game, rather than someone blase, laid back and not switched on.
- Speed matters much more than perfection.

## Notes

Shaun presents "Take massive, imperfect, relentless action" as his own quote.
