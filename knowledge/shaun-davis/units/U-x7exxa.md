## Description

When rooms emptied during COVID, Shaun did not keep every property. He moved the remaining tenants from the weaker houses into the nice ones in great areas near the city centre, then handed the weaker houses and most Airbnbs back to landlords. You keep what has potential and cut the rest.

## Details

1. Sort your properties: the nice houses with potential, in a great area close to the city centre, versus the terrible ones you never spent much money on.
2. Move the remaining tenants out of the weak houses and into the nice ones.
3. Hand the weak houses back to the landlords.
4. Hand back most of the Airbnbs; keep the working-professional houses that run well.
- He also sourced a couple of his own properties on to other operators.
