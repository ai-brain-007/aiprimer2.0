## Description

Sean's own story of the moment his business nearly died. He left his job at the end of February 2020, COVID hit three weeks later and the HMO and serviced accommodation business he had built since 2018 collapsed. He uses it to show what discipline looks like when motivation is gone.

## Details

- 2018: Sean starts his business with rental HMOs and serviced accommodation. Everything is going well.
- 28 February 2020: he leaves his job.
- Three weeks later COVID happens and the business sinks like a stone.
- He has to quickly understand the two traits of a successful entrepreneur: create value and solve meaningful problems.
- He is not motivated at all. With his back against the wall he decides to come out swinging and relies on discipline to do what has to be done.
