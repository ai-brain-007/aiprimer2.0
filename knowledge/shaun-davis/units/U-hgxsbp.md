## Description

Handing out your mobile and personal email tells providers and landlords you are one person working alone. The moment you miss a call or a problem comes up, alarm bells ring. Use a business landline and an answering service instead.

## Details

- The error: marketing with your personal phone number and personal email address
- Why it hurts: it is not a professional image, and if you are not available when someone calls it looks like there is nobody behind the business
- The consequence: the last thing providers and landlords want is to work with just one person; an unanswered call when there is an issue rings alarm bells
- The fix: a landline number and a call answering service from day one, so someone in the business always answers
