## Description

Successful people and his father all warned Shaun: business, like life, is not smooth sailing. Markets change, people get ill, things go wrong even when today is good. He thought he was prepared; COVID three weeks after quitting showed him how soon it can happen.

## Details

- The warning he kept hearing from books and from people like his father: business is good now, but like life it brings personal issues, illness and market changes.
- Business is full of peaks and troughs; things going well today does not stop the market changing.
- There will be a time when things go wrong. Plan for it.
- Shaun's admission: he believed he was prepared but did not think it would happen so soon.
