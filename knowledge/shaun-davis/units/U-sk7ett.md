## Description

From years of mentoring, Shaun sees that chasing perfection is usually procrastination in disguise, and that procrastination, nine times out of ten, comes from not being clear on what to do. Once people have clarity they get the confidence to act.

## Details

- Perfection is often procrastination in disguise.
- Procrastination, nine times out of ten in his experience, is caused by a lack of clarity.
- Once people have clarity, they have the confidence to go out and take massive, imperfect, relentless action.
- So if you find yourself procrastinating, seek clarity first. His own offer is mentoring; the point holds with or without a mentor.
