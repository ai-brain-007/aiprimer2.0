## Description

The author's worked example of a back-to-back margin. A provider pays you £4,000 a month, you have agreed £3,000 a month with the landlord, and the £1,000 difference is your monthly profit with no operations. It shows how the model pays without owning or managing anything.

## Details

- Provider pays you: £4,000 per month
- You pay the landlord: £3,000 per month
- Your profit: £1,000 every month
- Work involved: collecting the rent; only structural problems need you to step in
