## Description

When a council expects you to cover the bills, do not just absorb the cost. Put the bills into your offer and ask for more. Councils usually pay the extra because they want to avoid the admin, not because they are short of money.

## Details

1. Find out whether this council pays the bills on temporary accommodation.
2. If it does not, make your offer with bills included.
3. Expect a bit more rent as a result.
- Why it works: for most councils it is about the headache of dealing with bills, not the cost, so they pay you extra to handle it.
