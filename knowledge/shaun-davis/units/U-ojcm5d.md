## Description

Some property people portray one character on social media and turn out to be someone else when you meet them. The speakers call this out because it breaks trust the moment someone meets you. The fix is simple: only show the person you actually are.

## Details

- The pattern: you follow someone on social media or watch their YouTube, you connect with them, then you meet them in real life and they are completely different.
- The speaker says he has seen a lot of this in the property world, and his reaction on meeting them is: who are you?
- Fix: make how you are online exactly how you are in person, so nobody can ever say that about you.
