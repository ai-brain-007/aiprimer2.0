## Description

Your first property is the hardest because you are learning the surveyor, the landlord and the works all at once. Once you have been through it, the next one is easier. Treat the first deal as your apprenticeship.

## Details

- Getting the remedial works done was itself a long negotiation with the landlord, who paid for a lot of it
- After one deal you understand how to talk to the surveyor, how to communicate and how to negotiate with the landlord
- Doing it alone and adjusting from your own mistakes is what cements the skill
