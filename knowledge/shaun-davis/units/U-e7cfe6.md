## Description

Landlords often want the rent to start the day the contract is signed. Ask instead to start it when the tenants move in, after the works and the re-inspection. A flexible landlord and a rent-free period can mean you pay nothing until the council is paying you.

## Details

- Ask at the point of agreement: "Regarding the contract start date, are you happy for us to start that whenever we're ready to go?"
- Aim for the start date to match the tenants moving in
- The usual landlord position is 'it's on the contract, the rent starts today'; this landlord was happy to start whenever the team said
- The Dave deal included a week-and-a-half rent-free period and no deposit
