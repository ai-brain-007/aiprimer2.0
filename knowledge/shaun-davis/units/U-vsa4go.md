## Description

When you are building a business around a job, a virtual assistant does the groundwork on the days you cannot: calls, emails, chasing landlords and agents. By the time your day off arrives your diary is packed with high-value tasks. Sean got his VA six months in and says it is the one thing he would do from the start.

## Details

How Sean set it up while still employed:
1. The VA spends her days on the low-value groundwork: on the phone, including 45-minute calls, following up emails, chasing landlords, chasing agents.
2. She packs his diary in advance for his day off.
3. On the day off he does only high-value tasks that move the needle.

What Sean says about VAs:
- He has four VAs at the moment and it works amazingly well; they are super hardworking.
- Low cost and effective.
- Not just for people starting out; for businesses of any size.
- He hired his first VA about 6 months in and wishes he had done it from the very start.
- His recommendation to any business owner: let a VA take admin and lead generation; it moves the needle very quickly.
