## Description

You do not choose a strategy in the abstract; your city chooses it for you. Look at what your local council needs and fall into the model that fits. In this Manchester council the AP model works well; elsewhere it may be another of the three.

## Details

- Look at your city and your area and understand what demands the council has.
- You tend to get chosen by the model rather than choosing it: you fall into whichever one suits.
- In the council shown in the video the AP model works well.
