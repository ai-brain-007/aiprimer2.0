## Description

At a viewing you are checking the house against what the council will demand and testing whether the landlord will do a company let. Ask a few pointed questions, look for the usual surveyor items such as damp, window restrictors and doors, and give the agent a clear next step. Damp is usually a ventilation habit, not a building fault, and minor works are fine as long as they are not expensive.

## Details

1. Ask the agent: "How long has this property been empty?" and "Have you had much interest?"
2. Count the reception rooms. A second one can potentially become a bedroom.
3. Look for any sign of damp or mould; the council will be all over it. Ask who is dealing with it (here the property management team).
   - Common cause: no ventilation. No extractor fan in the bathroom and windows kept shut while showering, especially in the cold.
   - Fixes: fit a fan first; keep the window slightly open; test any existing fan actually works.
4. Check whether windows already have restrictors. Each one present is one less job.
5. Open and close every door; a door that does not close will be flagged.
6. Ask: "Is the landlord open to a company let on this one?"
7. Ask the price and whether the landlord is open to any negotiation on the rent.
8. Close with the agent: "If you can put it to the landlord for us and let him know exactly what we do, let us know what you need from us and we'll take it from there."

Accept a bit of remedial work if nothing is too expensive or impossible. The council surveyor will also ask for minor works before handover.
