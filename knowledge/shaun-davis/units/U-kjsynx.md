## Description

Asked for his single greatest skill, Shaun says empowerment. He keeps his team motivated, happy and doing their jobs. Behind it sits self-awareness: notice when there is an issue, dig down to its foundation and solve it.

## Details

- Greatest skill: empowering a team, keeping them motivated, keeping everyone happy and doing their job.
- How: be self-aware. When something is wrong, acknowledge that there is an issue, get to the foundation of what the issue is and actually solve the problem.
