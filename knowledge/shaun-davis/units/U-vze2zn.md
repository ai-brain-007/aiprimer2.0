## Description

When a council tells you it is not taking on new providers, do not hang up. Ask who its current approved providers are, then contact those providers and build a relationship with them. It turns a no into a lead.

## Details

1. Call the council.
2. If they say they are not taking on any more providers, ask: "Who are your current approved providers?"
3. Make contact with that provider.
4. Form a relationship with the provider from there, and lease your property to them under private sector leasing.
