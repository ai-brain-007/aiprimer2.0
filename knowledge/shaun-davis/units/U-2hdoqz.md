## Description

Under the nightly rate model, damage caused by the tenant comes out of your pocket. The author's two defences are a good relationship with the tenant and regular inspections. A good relationship helps, but never replace inspections with the tenant's word.

## Details

- You are responsible for the property; tenant damage is paid by you
1. Build a great relationship with the tenant; a tenant who respects you is more likely to respect the property
2. Do not take the tenant's word for it; do regular inspections
- A good relationship does not guarantee good condition, so you need both steps
- If you are far away, pay an inspection company to do the inspection for you
