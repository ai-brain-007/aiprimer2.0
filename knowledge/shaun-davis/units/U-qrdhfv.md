## Description

Most people ask friends and family for advice about goals those people have never reached. Shaun says that is like asking someone who is not a doctor for medical advice. They cannot advise on what they have not been through, so you get the wrong advice.

## Details

- The mistake: getting advice from the wrong people, usually friends and family
- Why it fails: they have never been through what you want to achieve, so they cannot advise on it
- His example: if your parents are not property entrepreneurs, do not ask them for property advice
- His comparison: seeking medical advice from someone who is not a doctor
- The fix: get advice from people who have done what you want to do, for example by following property entrepreneurs if you want to be one
