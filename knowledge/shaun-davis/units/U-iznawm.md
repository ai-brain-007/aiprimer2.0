## Description

What Shaun wants a landlord or agent to think after meeting him. These are the traits that make someone choose to work with you even after a bad experience. Demonstrate them; do not just claim them.

## Details

The landlord should come away thinking:
- I like his character.
- He does what he says he is going to do.
- He is reliable.
- I can trust him.
- He is on it.
- If there is a situation, he will solve it.
- Most importantly, he is competent.

If you can demonstrate these to the landlord or estate agent, they will want to work with you.
