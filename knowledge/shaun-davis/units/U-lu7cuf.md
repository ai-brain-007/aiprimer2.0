## Description

The heart of Shaun's pitch. When a landlord signs a commercial lease agreement with your company, the Renters Rights Bill does not apply, because the property is not anyone's principal residence and the tenant is a company, not a person. You give the landlord back the long-term certainty the Bill took away.

## Details

- Sign a company let agreement, a commercial lease agreement, with the landlord.
- The Renters Rights Bill does not come into effect because the property is not a prime principal residence.
- You are not renting as an individual; your company is the tenant.
- Shaun presents this as a great selling point: it restores the long-term profitable solution landlords want and turns their pain point into your opening.
