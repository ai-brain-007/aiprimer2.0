## Description

The trap Shaun fell into when he started. A plumbing problem meant he drove to the property to fix it himself; a new property meant he painted it himself. Doing everything kept him busy and kept the business small.

## Details

- The mistake: handling every task personally. His examples: going to a property to figure out a plumbing issue himself, and painting a newly taken property himself.
- The consequence: the business could not grow while he was doing everything, and he admits he really struggled to stop.
- The fix: let go to grow. Hire experienced people at every level, then manage and empower them so you can lead and grow the business instead. That was when the business started to grow.
