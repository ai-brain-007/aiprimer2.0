## Description

Do not go looking for a property until you have a provider who wants to scale. Only the provider can tell you which areas, which property types and which rents make a deal work. Get that brief first, then go and find exactly what they asked for.

## Details

The order Sean insists on:
1. Find an active social housing provider that is looking to scale (start with the council, then Contracts Finder).
2. Ask them the three core questions: what areas, what specific property types, what they pay per property.
3. Only then start building relationships with agents and looking for the exact properties the provider needs.
4. Marry the provider's need and the property together; the deal follows.

The mistake he sees most often: people find the property first, before closing the council or a provider. They end up holding a property with no active provider that wants it, and they cannot judge whether the deal stacks because they never learned what the provider wanted.
