## Description

The properties Shaun took on shared the same tired look: magnolia or dirty walls, brown or green carpet, carpet in the bathroom. Nobody wants that. Tenants want fresh and simple: white walls and a feature wall. Less is more.

## Details

Signs of a dated property:
- Old furniture, in some cases a 30-year-old wardrobe
- Magnolia walls
- Dirty walls
- Brown or green carpet
- Carpet in the bathroom

What people want instead:
- Fresh
- Simple, less is more
- Nice white walls
- A feature wall
