## Description

Shaun warns that the moment you believe you know everything is the moment you start to sink. It is the beginning of a decline, not a peak. The fix is to keep the student mindset no matter how far you get.

## Details

- The mistake: deciding you have learned enough and know it all.
- The consequence: that day is your downfall; you begin sinking and end up in decline.
- The fix: stay a student to the game and keep investing in your education.
