## Description

Michael inspects every property he takes on, every quarter. He looks for damp and mould, and he goes mainly because tenants who do not want to be a bother never report faults. The report also reassures the landlord that their property is in good hands.

## Details

- Inspect each property every quarter
- Look for problems such as damp and mould
- The point is not to catch tenants damaging the property. Tenants often stay quiet about faults because they do not want to bother you, so the visit tells them you are happy to jump on any problem
- Spot what could be going wrong and get someone out immediately to fix it
- You can hire an inventory company to do the inspection for you and produce a full, detailed report, so you do not have to visit
- Give that report to every landlord; the landlord can also join a physical inspection or sign off the inventory report
