## Description

Every council has a check sheet its inspector works from. Ask for it as soon as you start with a new council, go through it yourself and fix everything before the inspection. Otherwise you get a list of remedial works and lose two weeks.

## Details

1. When you start with a new council, ask for the specific check sheet the inspector will be using.
2. Go through the check sheet yourself before the inspector comes.
3. Make sure every item is boxed off.
4. Result: you are more likely to pass straight away and get the property onboarded as soon as the inspector comes out.

What happens if you skip it: the inspector comes out, gives you loads of remedial works and you waste a further two weeks. The author says it seems basic but many people forget it.
