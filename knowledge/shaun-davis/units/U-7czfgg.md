## Description

People imagine being their own boss as lounging around with nobody to answer to. Sean says it is the exact opposite. You swap the 9 to 5 for the 5 to 9, and you answer to your team, your landlords, the council and your residents.

## Details

- The error: expecting that running your own business means chilling on the beach.
- The reality: the saying Sean quotes is, if you want to leave your 9 to 5, be prepared for the 5 to 9.
- You never reach a point with nobody to answer to. Sean answers to his team, to landlords, to the council, and to his residents as a duty of care. He has to keep the cogs turning.
- Being a boss is not for everyone. It can be very stressful, so you need to understand how you deal with that stress.
