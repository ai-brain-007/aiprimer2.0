## Description

The victim mentality is the story that nothing ever goes your way, all your businesses flop and your breakthrough never comes. Shaun and Luigi call it one of the worst mindsets a person can have: it attracts more negativity, and while you ask those questions you are not growing, because the lesson is right in front of you and you cannot see it. The fix is to focus on what you want, take control of what you can, and ask what the situation is teaching you.

## Details

- Signs: 'nothing ever goes my way', 'things don't work out for me', 'all my businesses flop', 'why does this always happen to me', 'why am I not getting my breakthrough'.
- Effect: it attracts more negativity, in line with the idea that what you focus on grows.
- Cost: while you ask those questions you are not growing, because you are not looking. The lesson is right in front of you but you are not able to see it.
- Fix: focus on what you want and take control of what you can. Ask 'what is this teaching me?', learn from it, and take accountability for what you did or did not put in place.
