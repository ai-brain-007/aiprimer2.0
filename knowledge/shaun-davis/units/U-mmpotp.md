## Description

A social housing provider is the organisation that sits between you and the council. You supply the property; they supply the tenants, the contract, the support and the guaranteed rent. Knowing who they are is the first step to finding them.

## Details

- Types of provider: registered providers, housing associations, charities, CICs, and organisations that deliver accommodation and support for councils
- Their position: the middle point between the council and you
- What you bring: the property
- What they bring: the referrals, the contracts, the support and the guaranteed payments
- Why they matter to you: they can place tenants, pay you guaranteed rent and work with you long term
