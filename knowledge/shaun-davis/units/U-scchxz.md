## Description

You can earn from a property without owning it. The idea is to control an asset that produces cash flow, for example by leasing it from a landlord and leasing it on to a council. This is what makes a low-cash start possible.

## Details

- Owning is not the only route. Controlling an asset that produces cash flow also makes money.
- In practice the author does this through rent to rent: lease the property from the landlord or agent, lease it back out to the council.
- Controlling the property lets you make money from it without ever owning it.
