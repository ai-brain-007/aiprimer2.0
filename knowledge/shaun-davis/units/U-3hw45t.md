## Description

Asked for his top tips for anyone on the journey, Shaun gives three. Choose the people around you and take advice from those who have done it. Do not compare your start to someone else's twentieth step on social media. Work on yourself first, because the business cannot outgrow you.

## Details

1. Surround yourself with the people you want to be like. If someone is where you want to be, speak to that person. Do not take business advice from loved ones who have no successful business; find someone who has done it, ask how they started and get them to mentor you. Use social media and the networking events in your niche to find that circle.
2. Treat social media as a blessing and a curse. Do not compare your step one to someone else's step twenty, because you get discouraged and some of it is not even real. Be authentic, be who you are and go for it.
3. Work on yourself first. Your business cannot outgrow who you are, so always put yourself first. He only started to achieve success when he did, and then everything else happened organically.
