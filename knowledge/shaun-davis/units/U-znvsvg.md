## Description

A lock box is a small key safe fixed at the property. Michael puts one on every property he takes on. If there is an emergency while you are away, anyone on your team can get in and make the place safe.

## Details

- Fit a lock box on every property you manage
- Purpose: emergency access. Example given: a gas leak while you are out of the country
- Anyone from your team can get in, make the property safe and secure the problem without waiting for you
