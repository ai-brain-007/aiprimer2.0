## Description

Most providers call the council only to say they have a property. Michael asks how their day has been and picks up on anything he hears in the background. Officers remember him and move faster for him because the call is with a person, not a transaction.

## Details

- Council conversations are usually purely transactional; do not open with the property
- Ask how their day has been
- Listen for something in the background, like a dog barking, and talk about that
- Talk about anything other than what you called for, so that next time they remember you
- The payoff: they are willing to work a lot quicker for you
- Make their lives as simple as possible in return
- Shaun adds: officers will offer you what they have, recommend you to colleagues and bend over backwards to get you in sooner
