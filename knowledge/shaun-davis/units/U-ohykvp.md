## Description

Many landlords have never heard of temporary accommodation. Michael's third landlord was one of them. He explained the model before pitching, and the deal closed in two days at £1,000 a month profit.

## Details

- Find out whether the landlord knows what social housing and temporary accommodation are
- If not, make them aware of everything before you pitch the service
- Example: third property, found direct on OpenRent, landlord educated, closed in 2 days, £1,000 a month profit
