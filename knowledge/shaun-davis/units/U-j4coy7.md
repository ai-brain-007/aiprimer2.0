## Description

Ask the council how long a surveyor takes and take the first slot they have, even a cancellation the next morning. Then call the landlord and make attending effortless. If they cannot be there, offer to collect the key, meet the surveyor and bring it back.

## Details

1. Ask the council directly: "What is the waiting time for a surveyor to come out?" Accept any cancellation.
2. Call the landlord straight away: "I've got some fantastic news. The council are saying the surveyor is able to come out tomorrow at 10:00 a.m. Would that work for you?"
3. If they hesitate: "If you can't come, don't worry. We'll come and meet you wherever you are, get the key off you, do the survey and bring the key back to you."
4. Confirm and move on; do not let the slot slip.
