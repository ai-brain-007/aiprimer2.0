## Description

In life and in business you are only one contact away from a completely different path. Shaun says this is what makes business exciting. Keep making contacts and checking in, because the one that changes everything can arrive on an ordinary day.

## Details

- Shaun's own case: one message in a WhatsApp group he rarely checked, and one conversation with a council officer, set the direction of his whole business.
- That conversation told him which direction to go and who to speak to.
- Keep working the phones and the groups even when nothing is coming back.
