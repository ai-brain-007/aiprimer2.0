## Description

Start each day with something that gives you energy so the day is set up for success. Shaun's options are a walk, the gym, a book or something motivational to listen to, and he does all of them. The routine matters; the exact hour does not.

## Details

- Build a solid morning routine. Shaun calls it key: set your day up for success.
- Fill it with things that generate energy. His list: go for a walk, go to the gym, read a book, listen to something motivational. He loves doing all of the above.
- You do not need to get up at 5 a.m. to do this (see the 5 a.m. myth card).
