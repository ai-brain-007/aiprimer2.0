## Description

Michael tells landlords that his company does not live in the property, so the law that protects residential tenants does not apply to it. That makes a company easier to remove than an ordinary tenant, which is one more reason a deposit is not needed.

## Details

- The property is not the company's own residence; nobody from the company lives there
- So the tenant act does not apply and the company does not have the same tenant rights
- A landlord can get a company out much more easily than a tenant
- Used as an argument for not paying a deposit
