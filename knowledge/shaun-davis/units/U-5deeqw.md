## Description

During COVID, landlords had empty properties they could not fill. Shaun offered to take them on with a rent-free period at the start, in return for long-term guaranteed rent. He could prove the market was dead because his own rooms were not filling even at six weeks rent free. His first two deals came with three months rent free.

## Details

1. Understand the landlord's position: the property is empty and, in lockdown, they are not going to fill it.
2. Make the offer: I can take it off you, but I am going to need some time, and it will be worth your while because you get long-term guaranteed rent.
3. Back it with proof from your own portfolio. Shaun showed his own properties on six weeks rent free with no enquiries and said: this is what I do, I am management, so you have no chance of filling it.
4. Ask for the period you need. He asked for three months because nobody knew how long COVID would last.
5. Use the rent-free months to get tenants in and start making profit before rent is due.
- Result: three months rent free on his first two deals. He calls this his breakthrough, and being creative with deal structure is what let him scale.

## Notes

The argument worked because it was true at the time: landlords could not argue with it. It depends on a market where the landlord genuinely cannot fill the property.
