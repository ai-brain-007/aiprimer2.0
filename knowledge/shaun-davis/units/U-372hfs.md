## Description

Councils cut budgets, restructure and change leadership, so any one scheme can end. What stays constant is the people who need housing. When a scheme closes, the council still has to put those tenants somewhere, so it creates a new scheme. Follow the tenants, not the scheme.

## Details

- Councils make changes, make cuts and bring in new leadership structures.
- The tenant never changes. The people are still there and the council has to put them somewhere.
- So councils create different schemes, put different processes in place and open different opportunities for landlords.
- Practical use: when a council tells you a scheme has ended, ask where the tenants are going and get involved in the replacement scheme.

## Notes

Shaun gives this as one more tip specifically for temporary accommodation.
