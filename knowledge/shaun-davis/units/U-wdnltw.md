## Description

Habit five is to keep going when results take longer than you hoped. Shaun ties consistency to expectations: if you know from the start that it will take years, slow results do not knock you off course. Nothing worth having comes easy.

## Details

- Manage your own expectations about how long it will take before you start.
- Understand that it will not happen overnight; play the long game.
- Nothing worth having comes easy.
- Shaun's own path: understand the sacrifice, do it, stay consistent, run the course of the game, and only then succeed.

## Notes

Shaun calls this a huge one. The five-year expectation card gives his method for it.
