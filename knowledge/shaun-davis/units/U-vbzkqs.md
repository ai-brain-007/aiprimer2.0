## Description

When you check tenants into a property, look at the neighbours. Michael saw a landlord refurbishing two doors down, knocked, introduced himself and offered to help. They met two hours later and he closed the property a week later with no money down.

## Details

1. Notice landlords nearby, for example one refurbishing a property or one who approaches your tenants
2. Knock on the door and introduce yourself with something like: "Look, I've just taken on the property two doors down. I would love to have a conversation with you to see how I can be of assistance to you"
3. Meet quickly; Michael met this landlord 2 hours later
4. Show the property you already run and the condition you keep it in
- Result: closed a week later, no deposit, no money down
- This landlord had been let down by another social housing company
- Michael also only works with landlords he likes
