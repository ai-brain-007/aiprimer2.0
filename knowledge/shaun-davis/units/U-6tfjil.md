## Description

A mentee messaged Shaun worried that political change was ruining her life. His reply: worry about what you can control, not the what-ifs outside it. Anything else only stresses you out.

## Details

- Identify what is inside your control and act on that.
- Do not spend energy on things outside your control, such as what a government might do.
- Reason: worrying about the uncontrollable does nothing except stress you out.
- Context: the mentee's worries were mostly 'what if this happens' questions with no answer yet.
