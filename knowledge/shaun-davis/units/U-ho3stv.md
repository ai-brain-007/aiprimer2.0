## Description

Where you live and who surrounds you shapes how hard you push. Stay in your home city your whole life and you get complacent without noticing. Luigi moved to Dubai, felt like he was at the bottom again, and that pressure made him quit nicotine within weeks.

## Details

- Environment plays a key role in growth.
- In the UK, a decent car, a business and some properties put you in the upper bracket, so you can coast. In Dubai, Luigi had no car, no apartment and no business there, and felt right at the bottom. He says that was good for him.
- Result of the new environment: he quit vaping and nicotine pouches after years.
- Shaun's rule: someone who stays in their home city their whole life becomes complacent and comfortable, often subconsciously. There is no growth in your comfort zone.
- Once your business runs remotely you do not need to be in the UK, so choosing your environment is a real option.

## Notes

The Dubai part is Luigi's experience; the comfort-zone rule is Shaun's.
