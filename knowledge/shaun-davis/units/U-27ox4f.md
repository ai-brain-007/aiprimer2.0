## Description

When the council surveyor inspects a property there are only three possible results. Knowing them takes the fear out of the visit. The most common one is a yes with a list of small jobs.

## Details

1. Yes: the council is happy and takes the property on.
2. No: the property is a shambles, something is not fixable, and they do not want it.
3. Most likely: they want the property but there are a few bits of remedial work. Do those and they take it on.
