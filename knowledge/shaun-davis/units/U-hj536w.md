## Description

Leverage means getting other people to do the tasks that do not need you, so your limited time goes on the work that moves the needle. While working full time, Shaun had his partner Ashley and a virtual assistant arranging appointments and boxing off admin, and he alone spoke to landlords and did the sales. Today it is his first tip for anyone who wants to scale: get yourself a VA.

## Details

- Anything low value (research, emails, arranging appointments and viewings, sending letters) can be outsourced to other people.
- Shaun's set-up while employed: himself, Ashley and a VA.
- Ashley worked a full-time job at first and joined the business after about 9 months; the family income from Shaun's job covered this.
- The VA arranged appointments and got tasks boxed off.
- Only Shaun did the sales and spoke to landlords, the work that really moved the needle.
- Scaling tip number one is leverage, and step one of that is getting yourself a VA.
- In his temporary accommodation business the VA is on hand for tenants who need assistance beyond calling the maintenance company directly.
