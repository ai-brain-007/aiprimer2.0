## Description

You do not need a perfect schedule, endless free days or a perfect life to start. You need a system that makes the most of the hours you have and removes your excuses. Shaun says that with structure, leverage and discipline you can build a temporary accommodation business around any job, lifestyle or responsibility.

## Details

- You do not need to be perfect.
- You do not need a perfect schedule.
- You do not need endless free days.
- You need a system that maximises your time and eliminates all your excuses.
- With that system you can build a rent-to-rent temporary accommodation business around any job, any lifestyle and any responsibility, no matter how busy you think you are.
