## Description

Working in the business means doing the daily tasks yourself. Working on the business means managing it, improving it and steering it. Sean found the second one a much more productive way to grow, and it only becomes possible once you outsource.

## Details

- Working in the business: running around doing the jobs yourself.
- Working on the business: managing the business, coaching and empowering the team, tracking whether things move the needle.
- How you get there: outsource and leverage the lower-value jobs first. See the £500 per hour rule.
- Sean's verdict: a much better, much more productive way to grow.
