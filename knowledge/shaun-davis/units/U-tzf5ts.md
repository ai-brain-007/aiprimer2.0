## Description

Before anyone moves into one of Shaun's houses they go through an initial assessment. The team checks the person's needs and confirms they are eligible for the service. If they pass, they can move in immediately, so the assessment is the gate between a referral and a filled room.

## Details

- Every new tenant goes through what the team calls an initial assessment, also referred to as a pre-assessment
- Purpose: assess their needs and make sure they are eligible for the service
- Outcome: if they pass, they can be moved in immediately
- Who runs it: the housing team leader. Tyler was doing one in a living room during the visit, and assessing tenants before move-in is part of his day-to-day role.
- The assessment also tells the team how urgent the need is and which house will suit the person; see the weekend placement technique
