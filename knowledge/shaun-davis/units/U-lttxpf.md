## Description

Typing 'social housing providers near me' into Google gives you a long, random list and no idea who is actually growing. You get overwhelmed and waste calls on providers that are not looking for property. Start with the council or Contracts Finder instead.

## Details

- The error: searching Google for providers near you
- The consequence: you get overwhelmed, and there is no guarantee any of them wants to scale
- Fix 1: start with the council, who know which providers are active
- Fix 2: use Contracts Finder to find providers that have just won a government-backed contract, so you know they need properties
