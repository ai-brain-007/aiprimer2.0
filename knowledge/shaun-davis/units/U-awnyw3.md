## Description

A Rolex starts at around £10,000 and a Casio costs around £50, yet both just tell the time. The gap is value perception built by marketing and design. The author uses it to show why a better-marketed product gets paid far more for the same job.

## Details

- Rolex: costs on average starting from £10,000. Casio: around £50.
- Both do the same essential job: tell you the time. The kids' answers (it is more luxurious, rich people buy it, for style) all point to perception, not function.
- Rolex created that perception through marketing and clever design.
- The pricing lesson: if you sell your watch for £2,000 and someone else has marketed theirs so it sells at £10,000, you both still sell, but count how many £2,000 watches you have to sell to match one of theirs. Better marketing means you get paid more per sale.

## Notes

The watch prices are the author's rough figures ("on average", "I don't know £50"), not researched numbers.
