## Description

Shaun's picture for progress: do not stare at the whole mountain, just take the next step. You cannot plan the full climb because the future is unknown. Keep moving one step at a time and things work themselves out as you go.

## Details

- Do not try to climb Mount Everest in one go; that is not possible when nobody can see the future.
- Put one foot in front of the other and figure it out along the way.
- Do not slow down while you figure it out.
- Things work themselves out as you go along.
