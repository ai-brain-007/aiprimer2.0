## Description

Many estate agents hear social housing and picture bad tenants, kicked-in doors and smashed windows. Say the words at the start and you can sabotage the conversation before it begins. Describe who will live there instead.

## Details

- The mistake: opening with the words social housing.
- Why it hurts: it puts the agent on the back foot straight away, because many agents associate social housing with bad tenants and damage.
- The fix: say who the occupants will be, for example families. If the deal is temporary or emergency accommodation it most likely will be families, so that is the pitch Shaun uses.
- Only say families if that is genuinely what it is.
