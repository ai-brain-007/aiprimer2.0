## Description

Sean's favourite quote and the idea under his approach to setbacks. A loss is not fixed; how you look at it decides what you get from it. Reframing is the skill to build.

## Details

- The quote Sean returns to: when you change the way you look at things, things begin to change.
- Applied to losses: when you take an L, the important thing is to understand how to reframe it.
- The reframes he uses: look at your own part instead of blaming others, and ask what the loss is teaching you.
