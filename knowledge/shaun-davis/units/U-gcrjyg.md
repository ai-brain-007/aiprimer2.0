## Description

Shaun is clear that property and business do not make you wealthy overnight. If you come in looking for quick riches you have picked the wrong sport. Business is a long-term game measured in decades, so set your clock accordingly.

## Details

- The mistake: expecting to get wealthy overnight or to get rich quick from business.
- The reality: it takes a very, very long time; business is a long-term game and you have to think in decades.
- The fix: manage your expectations, give it at least five years and plan for the next decade.

## Notes

The transcript reads that business is probably the right sport for getting rich quick; from the surrounding sentences he almost certainly said it is not.
