## Description

To make a new habit stick, set the bar low enough that it is easy, then raise it once the habit is there. The speaker's ten pages a day is the worked example: small, easy, daily, and it grows on its own. The habit comes first, the intensity later.

## Details

1. Pick a small daily minimum, small enough that it is nice and easy. The speaker uses 10 pages of a book.
2. Do it daily to build the habit.
3. Progressively increase the intensity once the habit is there. In practice you often exceed the minimum on your own, for example reading 20 pages instead of 10.
4. Keep the minimum as the floor you never go under.
