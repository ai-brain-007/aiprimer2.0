These are the figures behind the deal closed in the video. The gap between what the council pays and what the landlord receives is your monthly profit. Small money in, steady money out for three years.

## Details

- Rent to landlord: £1,450 per month (advertised on OpenRent at 1,400)
- Paid by the council: just under £2,000, so £1,950 per month
- Net profit: £500 per month
- Term: three-year commercial lease, 36 months
- Rent payment date: 19th of every month or the following day
- Deposit: none; rent-free period of a week and a half
- Shaun's illustration: put in £600, get £500 a month for 3 years, 500 x 36 = £18,000
- Sandu's actual cash into the deal after the works: '130' in the transcript, said to be paid back within 2 months
- Timeline: council on Tuesday, landlord met Thursday evening, keys Friday, contract signed the same evening
