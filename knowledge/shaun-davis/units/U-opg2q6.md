## Description

Whoever ends up living in the property, you are the one who signed the contract with the landlord. If the property gets trashed, the landlord holds you responsible, not the council or the provider. So vet the tenant or the housing provider before you hand over the keys.

## Details

- Applies to all three schemes.
- The mistake: not doing due diligence on the tenant or on the housing provider you lease to.
- The consequence: if the property gets trashed, it is your responsibility, because you signed the contract with the landlord and the landlord will hold you responsible.
- The fix: do your due diligence on the tenant or the housing provider before placing or leasing.
