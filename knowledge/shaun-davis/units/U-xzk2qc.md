## Description

A remedial report is what you get after the council's surveyor has inspected a property. It tells you exactly what needs doing to bring the property up to the council's standard. Your job is to get that work done and then book the surveyor back out.

## Details

- When you get it: after the council surveyor has fully inspected the property.
- What it contains: exactly what you need to do to get the property up to standard.
- What you do with it: organise the maintenance on the back end. A viewing company can be on site to make sure the job is done properly and inspect the finished work before you book the surveyor to come back out.
- Once the property passes, you can close the deal.
