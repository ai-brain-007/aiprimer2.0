## Description

As an investor, Shaun judges spending by its return. Money spent on his own development is the one investment he calls infinite, because he cannot put a price on what it gave him. So treat education as an investment, not a cost.

## Details

- Look at education the way an investor looks at any deal: what is the return on investment?
- Shaun's verdict on his own development spend: an infinite return, impossible to price.
- The quote he lives by: invest in yourself, it pays the best interest.
