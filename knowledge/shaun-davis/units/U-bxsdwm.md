## Description

Do not put everything into one scheme. Shaun runs a mixture of Local Authority Leasing, Private Sector Leasing and nightly rates, so that the safe guaranteed income balances the riskier, better paid nightly work. His favourite is nightly rate, but he keeps the other two beside it.

## Details

- Run a mixture of all three temporary accommodation strategies.
- Purpose: to mitigate the risk.
- Shaun's all-time favourite is the nightly rate, because the reward is higher, but he says plainly that it is the riskier strategy.
