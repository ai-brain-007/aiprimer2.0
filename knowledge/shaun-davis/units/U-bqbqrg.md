## Description

Costs to start depend on the strategy, but the basics are small. Around £300 covers registering the business and a professional image, and insurance runs about £75 a month for a small business. Back-to-back leasing adds nothing on top, because you are not running the property.

## Details

- Business setup, all in: about £300. Covers registering the company, a domain, a professional email address and a virtual telephone number so you have a proper landline rather than a mobile.
- Insurance, all policies together, when the business is small: about £75 a month, or roughly £500 to £600 if paid up front for the year.
- Back-to-back leasing: no further startup costs, since you are only finding and providing the property. You still need professional indemnity and public liability insurance.
- Also recommended: membership of the PRS, the redress scheme.
- Most insurances can be bought after the first deal brings money in.
