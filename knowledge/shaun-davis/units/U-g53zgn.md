## Description

An entrepreneur's job is to create value and solve problems. In property right now the biggest problem is housing, and councils are the ones who need it solved. Help them and, the author says, you will never be out of business.

## Details

- The property market rewards people who can solve problems.
- The role of the entrepreneur is to create value and solve problems.
- The biggest problem right now is housing, specifically councils housing vulnerable people.
- If you can help councils solve that problem you will never be out of business.
