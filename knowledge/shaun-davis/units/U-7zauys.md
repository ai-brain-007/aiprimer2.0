## Description

Furnish your houses well, not just cheaply. Shaun's reasoning is simple: a house done to a good standard feels homely, and a tenant who feels at home wants to keep it clean. One tenant said he feels house proud even though it is not his house, and Shaun's reply was that it is his home.

## Details

- Always furnish to a good standard
- The logic: good standard, feels more homely, tenant wants to keep it clean
- Tell tenants it is their house and their home, not just a room they rent
- Result seen on inspection three: a well-maintained new build where the tenant says he feels house proud and it is the best place he has lived
