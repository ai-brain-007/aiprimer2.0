## Description

People often ask Sean why he chose social housing over other property strategies. His answer comes down to five reasons, all about stability and speed. Together they explain why he calls it a strategy for all seasons.

## Details

- Recession proof: it works in all seasons, unlike Airbnb or professional lets, which failed him when COVID hit and tenants lost jobs
- Zero tenant marketing: the tenant is there up front, so you can scale with confidence
- Government-backed: financial stability for you and a selling point for landlords
- Premium rents: the UK housing crisis means councils are desperate and pay a premium, giving better monthly cash flow
- Upfront incentives: in some council deals you are paid at the start, which funds the next deal
