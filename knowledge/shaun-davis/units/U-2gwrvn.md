## Description

Point your attention at opportunities and at the tasks that move the needle, not at obstacles. Hold yourself accountable for being productive rather than busy, and use automation, leverage, outsourcing and hiring to keep your own focus on high value work.

## Details

- Where focus goes, energy flows, and results show.
- Focus on opportunities, not obstacles.
- Focus on the things that move the needle: high value tasks.
- Hold yourself accountable to your productivity. Do not just be busy, be productive.
- Ensure you are automating, leveraging, outsourcing and hiring. Shaun calls these crucial to success and to moving the needle.
