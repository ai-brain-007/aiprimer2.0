## Description

Shaun grew up in a good household where his father left a normal job to become an entrepreneur. He saw the hardship and the failures first hand, worked for him from around 18 or 19, and watched the business gain momentum. That gave him the confidence that he could do it too.

## Details

- His parents were entrepreneurs; his dad had a normal job and then took the risk
- He saw first hand the hardship and the failures his dad went through
- What he remembers most vividly from childhood is his dad failing, getting back up and saying this is my dream
- At about 18 or 19 he went to work for his dad for a few years, when the business started to gain real momentum
- What it taught him: if you really put something in and go hard, you can achieve something
- The result: the confidence to think he could do it too
