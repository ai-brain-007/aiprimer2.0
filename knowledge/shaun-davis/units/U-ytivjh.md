## Description

Shaun uses his own beginning as the case study for belief. In 2018 he had no property experience, no relationships and £1,500 to his name, and he believed he could build a multi-million pound business. Six and a half years later he has a multi-million pound social housing portfolio.

## Details

- It has not been easy; the point is the belief plus the willingness to keep going.
