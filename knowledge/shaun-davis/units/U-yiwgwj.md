## Description

Sean lived with a stranger for a week and helped them close a social housing deal from scratch, without knowing which council or area it would be. The deal closed in two and a half days. His point: the method is not hard, the difference is attitude and how badly you want it.

## Details

- Challenge: live with a stranger (Sandu) for one week and help them close a deal start to finish
- Sean did not know in advance which council or where he would end up
- What he relied on: experience, knowledge, work ethic and an attitude to grow and learn
- Result: the deal closed in two and a half days
- The lesson: with the right attitude, willingness to work and ambition you can change your life quickly
