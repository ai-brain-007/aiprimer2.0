## Description

The student will not place anyone in a property he would not put his own family in. Shaun calls this a duty of care and says it is why the student will last in the business: caring about tenants and having a relationship with them gives you longevity.

## Details

- Rule: do not put somebody in a property you would not put your own family member in.
- Shaun: you have a duty of care to provide a good product to the market; the properties in the video were in great condition and the tenants happy.
- The student made mistakes on the way, but once you realise this is a big factor, you do not change it.
- Providers weed out who has the right quality of property, so this standard also helps you get deals.
