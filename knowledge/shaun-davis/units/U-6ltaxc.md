## Description

The speaker used to wish he had a big business. Looking back, he did not have the mindset to handle the stress that comes with one: a single small problem would ruin his whole evening. Now his problems are far bigger but, because he has grown as a person, he deals with them and moves on. The story shows why you grow yourself before the business.

## Details

- Back in the day he kept wishing he had a big business and asking why he did not have it yet.
- Looking back, he did not have the mindset to handle the stress that comes with a big business.
- Then: one little problem would get into his head and ruin his whole evening.
- Now: much bigger problems and issues, but because he has grown as a person they do not affect him as much. He deals with them in a way that looks like he has forgotten about them straight away: solution, bang, done.
- Before, he would not have been able to deal with them at all.
