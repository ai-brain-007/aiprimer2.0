## Description

Instead of partnering with a provider, you can be the provider yourself. Some councils let you call and sign up directly; others go through the government gateway, where Contracts Finder is the way in. Going direct means the risk and the tenant management sit with you, though temporary accommodation needs little management and maintenance can be outsourced.

## Details

Two routes into social housing:
1. Partner with existing providers, found on Contracts Finder or elsewhere.
2. Become a provider directly with a council.

How to go direct:
- Certain councils: just call them and become a provider.
- Certain councils go through the government gateway; there, Contracts Finder is your best bet.
- Shaun did this with Sandhu on the challenge: they became a provider directly with Luton Council.

Two things to know about going direct:
1. The risk is all on you, not on a provider.
2. You are the provider, so you manage the tenant.

What management involves:
- Temporary accommodation does not take much: a family lives there and only contacts you if there is an issue with the property.
- Even that can be outsourced to a maintenance company.
