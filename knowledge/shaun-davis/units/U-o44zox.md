## Description

Shaun lives by this quote. When the money is not there, panicking does nothing; a calm, logical mind finds another route, like raising finance or selling a stake. Panic puts you in a state where you cannot make competent decisions, so you make the wrong ones.

## Details

- The quote: when you lack resources you must become resourceful.
- In practice for Shaun: instead of panicking about payday, he raised £100,000 of finance and negotiated with the council.
- In practice for Luigi: with 14 days to pay an investor, he sold shares in a property to raise the £50,000.
- Why calm matters: when you panic you are not in a fit state of mind to make competent decisions, so you end up making the wrong ones.
- Stay logical, stay calm, work the problem.
