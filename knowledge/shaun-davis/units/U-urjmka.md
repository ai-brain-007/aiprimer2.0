## Description

Growth in the gym comes from tearing the muscle and letting it repair. Shaun says your mind and your business work the same way: the setbacks are the tear, and the lessons you draw from them are the repair where growth happens.

## Details

- Doing bicep curls, you push yourself to your limits and tear the muscle. The growth happens in the repair.
- Same with the mind and the same in business: it is in the failure and the setbacks that you learn the most lessons.
