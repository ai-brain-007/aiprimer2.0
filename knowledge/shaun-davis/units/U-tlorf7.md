## Description

The calculation that turned Shaun away from the classic buy-to-let route while he was still a car salesman. A 70,000 pound deposit would take six or seven years to save and net only a few hundred pounds a month, and one non-paying tenant could turn that into a monthly loss. It is why he went looking for other structures.

## Details

1. Deposit needed for a first buy-to-let: 60,000 to 80,000 pounds.
2. Saving that from a normal job looked like a 6 to 7 year goal, and only possible with a high income.
3. Cash flow from a 70,000 pound investment: about 300 to 400 pounds a month on a good month.
4. If the tenant stops paying and refuses to leave, getting them out could take roughly 6 to 9 months.
5. Conclusion: an asset producing 400 pounds a month could soon become a monthly liability, so he looked at other structures.
