## Description

Asking a landlord to keep paying bills sounds like a big ask until you remember what HMO landlords already do. Luigi covers bills, maintenance and the headache on all fifteen of his working professional HMOs. A landlord with an HMO is used to it, so pushing bills their way is a normal conversation.

## Details

- Half of Luigi's properties are working professional HMOs: 15 of them.
- He covers the bills, the maintenance and the headache on all of them.
- So an HMO landlord is already used to paying bills; the ask is nothing new.

## Notes

Told by Luigi, the co-host, from his position as a buy-side landlord.
