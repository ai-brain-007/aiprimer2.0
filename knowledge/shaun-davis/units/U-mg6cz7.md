## Description

Anyone can build up assets and hand them to their children. Shaun calls that the lazy way and the fastest way to lose the wealth. Real legacy is teaching and training your kids so they can run the ship after you and pass it on again.

## Details

- True legacy is in the education and the knowledge, not the asset base
- Handing assets to kids is lazy and the quickest way to lose your wealth
- Instead, teach and train your kids
- The test: when you are gone, can they steer the ship without it sinking, and pass it on to their kids?
- That chain is how you build a true legacy
