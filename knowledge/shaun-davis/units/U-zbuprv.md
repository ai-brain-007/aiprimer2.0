## Description

In rent-to-rent serviced accommodation you owe the landlord the lease every month whether or not guests book. The landlord only cares that you fulfil the contract. That makes it riskier than deal sourcing, where you commit to nothing.

## Details

- You acquire the property via rent to rent, so every month you pay the lease to the landlord.
- The landlord does not care whether you are booked. He wants his rent and his contract fulfilled.
- Result: a big commitment with no guarantee of income.
- The author's answer: move to a model with a guaranteed payer, temporary accommodation leased to the council.
