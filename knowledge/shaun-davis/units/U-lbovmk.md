## Description

Before you pay anyone to teach you, Shaun wants you to check them out. Many people sell advice on things they have never done. Run through these questions and look for evidence, not talk, before your hard-earned money goes to someone.

## Details

Ask about anyone you are about to pay for mentorship:
- Have they got the experience?
- Have they got any proof of what they have been doing?
- Have you spoken to someone who has been on their mentorship?
- Do they have reviews?
- How long have they been going for? Have they just popped out of the woodwork recently?
- Can you go on their social media and see posts from three or even five years back? That is a good sign of someone who has been in the game long enough.

Take claims with a pinch of salt and look at evidence.

## Notes

Shaun offers his own Instagram history back to 2020 as the kind of proof he means.
