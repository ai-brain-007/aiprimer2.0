## Description

With serviced accommodation or student and professional lets, you spend time and money finding tenants and you might still end up empty. With temporary accommodation the council supplies the tenant. You can take on a property knowing there is someone to fill it.

## Details

- Serviced accommodation and housing professionals or students needs a lot of tenant marketing, and there is no guarantee you will let the property.
- In temporary accommodation, the tenant is guaranteed by the council.
- Result: you can acquire property with confidence, knowing a tenant is waiting at the end.
