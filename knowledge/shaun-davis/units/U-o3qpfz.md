## Description

Say you want to move your life and business to Dubai. Someone who has never taken a risk will call it super risky and ask why you would leave your comfort behind. Someone who has done it will tell you how it benefited them and how they did it. Only the second answer lets you make an informed decision.

## Details

- The plan: take your life and business, build something in Dubai and start a whole new life there.
- Ask someone who has never taken a risk and you hear: that sounds super risky, why move to the other side of the world when your convenience, comfort and lifestyle are here? They say it because they have never taken risks.
- Ask someone who has done it and been through the motions and you get their true perspective: how it benefited them and how they did it.
- That gives you actual information on what can and will happen for you, not guesses about what might happen.
- Only then can you make an informed decision that is best for you, not for anyone else.
