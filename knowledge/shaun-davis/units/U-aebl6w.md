## Description

You see the lifestyle side of property on social media but rarely the daily reality. That is partly a choice: much of what happens in social housing is confidential, and filming a full day would show things that could cause massive problems for your business, especially with councils. Expect to keep a lot of your work off camera.

## Details

- Both speakers wish they could document more of the real, day-to-day work.
- Why they do not: if you vlogged your full day, certain bits in that vlog would cause massive problems for your business.
- Councils are named as a particular area of sensitivity.
- In social housing a lot of the material is confidential, so it cannot be shown.
