## Description

Shaun calls this one of the best pieces of advice he can give. If your emotions swing all over the place you will sabotage the opportunities in front of you, and as a leader your moods spread to your team. He watches himself closely and tries to act logically instead of emotionally.

## Details

- Learn to manage your emotions effectively or you will sabotage your own success. With your emotions all over the place you cannot condition your mind to spot opportunities, and you sabotage the ones already in front of you.
- Team effect: a negative, reactive, emotional leader whose mood drops at every little challenge passes that ethos to the team. They adopt the same mentality, and the leader becomes unapproachable.
- His practice: be very conscious of yourself and try to move very logically rather than emotionally.
