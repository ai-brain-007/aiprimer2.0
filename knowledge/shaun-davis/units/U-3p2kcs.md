## Description

Follow one £10 note and you see that money never sits still: it goes from you to a shop, to the shop's supplier, to a worker, and finally to a bank. The lesson is that money is always moving, and the question is whether it moves through you or past you.

## Details

The journey as the author lays it out:
1. You hold £10.
2. It goes to a shop.
3. The shop passes it to a supplier.
4. The supplier pays a worker.
5. Finally it goes to the bank.

What it teaches: money is always in motion. Ask yourself whether you are watching it move from the outside or standing in the middle so that it passes through you.
