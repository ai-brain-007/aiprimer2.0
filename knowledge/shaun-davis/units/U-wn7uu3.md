## Description

Before the council places tenants, a shared house has to meet HMO fire standards. The author lists the three items that brought this five-bed up to standard. Knowing them tells you what to budget for and what to check on a viewing.

## Details

- Fire doors (they come with the hinges)
- Emergency lighting
- A fire alarm system (this property got a brand new one)
- The author calls it very simple to bring a property up to this standard
