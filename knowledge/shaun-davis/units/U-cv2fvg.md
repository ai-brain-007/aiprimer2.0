## Description

It is easy to blame a recession or the party in power for missing your goals. Shaun's rule: as long as you blame someone else, you will never succeed, because no politician has your interests at heart. Get up every morning and take charge of your own financial situation.

## Details

- Common excuses: 'we were in a recession so I didn't hit my goals', 'Labour got into power so I can't achieve my goals'.
- Blaming someone else hands over your power, and you stay broke for the rest of your life.
- Politicians and governments do not have your best interest at heart; nobody in power is trying to make you rich personally.
- Only you have the power to control your own financial security.
- Daily habit: get up and say 'I want to be in control of my financial situation', and do not let anyone stop you.
