## Description

Sean calls the Contracts Finder approach targeted marketing. You are not calling random names; you are calling providers who have a contract to fill and money to pay for it. That is why it closes back-to-back leases faster than cold searching.

## Details

- Random marketing: Googling providers and calling anyone you find
- Targeted marketing: calling providers who have just been awarded a contract, so you know they need properties
- Result: you are more likely to close a back-to-back lease with the provider
- Every contract on Contracts Finder is a batch of warm leads (eight suppliers on one contract means eight leads straight away)
