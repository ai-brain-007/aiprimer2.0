## Description

For his first couple of years Shaun tried to do things himself and did not outsource as much as he could. Looking back, he calls it the biggest thing that held him back and stopped him closing deals. The turnaround came when he let go fully and spent his time leading the team.

## Details

- The mistake: spending the first couple of years trying to understand leverage while still doing things himself and not outsourcing enough.
- The cost: it was the biggest thing that held him back and prevented him from closing deals and moving the business forward.
- The turnaround: the minute he let go fully, he could work on the business, spending his time managing, empowering and coaching the team.
- The result: location freedom and time freedom to grow the business and work on the things he is passionate about.
