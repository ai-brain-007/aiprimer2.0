## Description

When you call a provider, tell them you found them through the contract they just won. It shows you did your homework, gives you instant common ground, and makes you stand out from everyone else cold calling them.

## Details

1. Before you call, read the contract page on Contracts Finder: what it is for, the framework, the end date.
2. Open the conversation by referencing how you found them and which contract it was.
3. Use that shared ground to ask whether they are looking to scale under that contract.

Why: it creates common ground straight away and makes you stand out.
