## Description

Habit seven is to stop chasing quick wins and build something that lasts. When Shaun started thinking about legacy and generational wealth, it changed what he did, how he moved and who he spent time with. Success comes from doing today what your future self will thank you for.

## Details

- Stop chasing quick wins; build something real that will last.
- Think about legacy and generational wealth. Ask: what can you do to build generational wealth?
- That question shifts your perspective on what you do, how you move and who you spend time with.
- A lot of success comes from doing things today that your future self will thank you for.
- Think about the bigger picture; without long-term thinking you will always fall short.
