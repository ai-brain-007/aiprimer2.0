## Description

Every council has a housing sector, and inside it a temporary and homeless accommodation department. That department is your way in. You call it, learn what they need, then go and find it and offer it back to them as a partnership.

## Details

1. Find the temporary and homeless accommodation department inside the council's housing sector. Every council has one.
2. Call that department. Find out exactly what the demand is, what the tenant type is and exactly what they are looking for.
3. Ask the three core questions: which areas they want to scale in, which property types they want, and what they pay.
4. Go out and find exactly what they need.
5. Match the two together and form a partnership with the council.
6. Throughout, be very clear about what you can do and what your intentions are, so they take you seriously.
