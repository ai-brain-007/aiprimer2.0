## Description

Do not pitch first. Get the landlord to tell you about their experience in property and why social housing interests them now, so their problems are all on the table. Then present your service as the answer to exactly those problems. Sean borrows Alex Hormozi's line: make the offer so good they feel stupid saying no.

## Details

1. Ask about their pain points. Useful questions: What has been your experience in property? Why are you now interested in social housing? Why is this a viable option for you now?
2. Listen carefully and let them extract all their pain points. Sean pictures ten minutes of the landlord telling you all their problems.
3. Sell your service as the solution to those specific pain points.
4. Result: after laying out their problems and hearing your solution, they feel stupid saying no. Sean quotes the slogan of Alex Hormozi's book: make your offers so good that people feel stupid for saying no.

The skill is being able to effectively and competently extract that information.
