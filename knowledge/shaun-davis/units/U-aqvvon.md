## Description

This is the recorded call Sean made during his documentary, approaching Luton Council as a temporary accommodation provider. It shows the exact questions to ask: what you need, where, how the process works, what you pay, and how big the demand is. Copy the order and the tone.

## Details

Opening:
"Hi, it's Sean Corn. We're a temporary accommodation provider and we've got access to quite a lot of properties in the local area in Luton. We're looking at working with Luton Council to provide temporary accommodation. Is this something that you guys are still offering at the moment?"

When they ask what size properties you have, turn it back to their need:
"Ideally, what I need to try and establish is exactly what you need. We can get access to one, two and three-bedroom properties."

Write down their answer and pin the location:
"Three and four beds. All right. Cool. Two seconds. Let me write this down. Where specifically?"

Ask how the process works:
"Can you explain how the process works in terms of working with you?"

Ask about money:
"How does it work in terms of the payment, just so we know what we're working with? What's your nightly rates at the moment?"
"Are these rates negotiable at all?"

Ask about demand:
"Just out of interest, what is your current demand? Do you have a lot of tenants on the waiting list?"

Close without committing:
"It's good for us to hear, because we are looking to expand in the area. Leave it with me. We're going to have to crunch some numbers now, speak to our network, and see what we can get and take it from there."

Sean introduces the clip as calling a provider, but the person on the line is speaking for Luton Council (agent and landlord approved contractor's list, property negotiators). The transcript renders his name as 'Sean Corn'. Filler words have been removed from the script; the citations keep them.
