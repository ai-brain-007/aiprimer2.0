## Description

Landlords are not looking for excitement. They want rent that arrives for years without hassle, and that is exactly what a long social housing lease gives them. Lead with this when you pitch, and objections about break clauses or subletting get much smaller.

## Details

- What a landlord wants: a long-term, profitable, sustainable solution.
- That is what you offer: a multi-year lease with a set payment.
- It is the first of the two things the author says a landlord cares about; the second is whether they can trust you as a person.
- Use it to explain the one-sided break clause: most landlords do not mind because the deal is long-term.
