## Description

Building a relationship is half of sales; the other half is keeping it alive. Other people want the same property, so the viewing is not the finish line. Get the person's personal number, send a thank-you and a landlord pack, and stay in front of them every day until the deal is done. Being credible and memorable is what keeps you in first place.

## Details

1. Be very personal in the conversation itself; that is how rapport forms.
2. Always get the personal number (personal WhatsApp), not just the office line. Shaun says he maintains every relationship this way.
3. Straight after the viewing, send a thank-you message saying the property is perfect for you.
4. Tell them you will send a landlord pack when you leave. It makes you more memorable than everyone else and gives them something to show their partner.
5. Message every single day with an update, repeating that you definitely want the property and would love to do business with them.
6. Maintain the relationship until keys and contract are in hand. Maintain it well and you get lots of deals.
