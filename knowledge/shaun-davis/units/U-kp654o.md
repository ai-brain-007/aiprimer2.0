## Description

Agents and landlords do not know your jargon. If your pitch needs explaining, they switch off and say no. Shaun keeps it to one plain line, "we work with the council", and gets straight to the point.

## Details

- People do not understand "temporary accommodation" or "rent to rent". Explaining it confuses them, and confused people say no.
- Say instead: "We work with the council. We're looking for three and four bedroom properties." Direct, straight to the point.
- It is all about the way you come across; the same town that "doesn't work" for others worked within an afternoon.
- The full rapport recipe: build rapport, find common ground, keep it simple.
