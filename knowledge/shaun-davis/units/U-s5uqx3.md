## Description

You do not rebuild a house to get social housing tenants in. Shaun's refurb is a lick of paint, magnolia walls to white, a feature wall and newer furniture. The aim is to make rooms appealing enough to fill quickly, and he paid for it from the profits of his first deal.

## Details

- Lick of paint throughout.
- Change magnolia walls to white.
- Add a feature wall for appeal.
- Replace old furniture with new, more modern pieces.
- Purpose: make the house more appealing so tenants move in quickly.
- Funding: his first deal needed no refurb; the second, a couple of months later, needed light work and he paid for it from the first deal's profits.
