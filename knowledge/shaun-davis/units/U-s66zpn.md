## Description

Shaun argues that nobody, at any level, can see the road ahead. He is eight years in and does not understand the next level; people with 20 years in business tell him the same. Even companies worth billions go bust because they could not foresee tomorrow. So not knowing is normal, not a reason to wait.

## Details

- People with 20 or more years in business are in the exact same situation.
- If anyone knew the future, everyone would be a billionaire.
- Companies worth billions still go bust because they did not know what tomorrow would hold.
- Technology is evolving fast, which makes the future even harder to see.
