## Description

What you give your attention to is what expands in your life. Focus on the negative and you get more negativity; think negatively and you attract negative things. So point your attention at what you want, not at what you do not want.

## Details

- Applies to business: the thing you focus on most is what grows.
- Applies to mindset: focusing on the negative brings more negativity in. Shaun calls it a self-fulfilling prophecy.
- Luigi's takeaway from the conversation: focus on what you want rather than what you do not want, because day-to-day noise makes it easy to lose sight of that.
