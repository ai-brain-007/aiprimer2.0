## Description

Landlords are handing over an asset worth a lot of money, especially in London. What they weigh most is not who pays a bit more but who they can trust not to trash it. Show that they can reach you and that you will show up in hard times.

## Details

What a landlord wants to know before they hand you their property:
- Can they trust you with an asset they earned with hard work
- If there is an issue, can they get hold of you and your team
- Will you work in the hard times as well as the good times
- Someone paying more rent who might trash the property is the last thing they want
- Get this across and the figures stop being the biggest factor
