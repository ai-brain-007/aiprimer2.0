## Description

Four groups of people end up in temporary and emergency accommodation. Knowing them tells you what kind of demand a council has and what kind of property it needs. Shaun lists the homeless, domestic violence survivors, people hit by emergencies, and refugees and asylum seekers.

## Details

1. The homeless. Numbers are rising because of the cost of living, evictions and high energy prices. People who cannot maintain their home get evicted, the council has to house them, and they go into temporary and emergency accommodation.
2. Domestic violence. Depending on the severity, the person needs a safe house, sometimes at the opposite end of the country. They also go into temporary and emergency accommodation.
3. Emergency situations such as fire and floods. The tenants need to be placed in temporary and emergency accommodation.
4. Refugee and asylum housing needs. When people are granted access into the country they are first housed in temporary accommodation until they get leave to remain, then they move to permanent accommodation.
