## Description

Councils respond to people who sound certain and know what value they bring. If you present as someone still figuring it out, you will not get a reply. Speak with certainty and confidence.

## Details

- Do not position yourself as a time waster, as someone who does not know what they are doing, or as someone trying to figure it out
- Speak with certainty and confidence
- Help the council officer understand what value you bring to the table
- Result: they are more likely to be responsive and to email back faster
