## Description

Social housing and temporary accommodation sit under one umbrella, and there are four ways in. Becoming a provider yourself is hard and full of red tape. The hosts recommend and teach the other three: buy and lease to a provider, rent-to-rent with a back-to-back lease to a provider, or rent-to-rent temporary accommodation with the council.

## Details

1. Become a social housing provider yourself: a lot of red tape, very hard to do.
2. Buy a property and lease it to a social housing provider.
3. Rent-to-rent with a back-to-back lease to an existing provider: no involvement in the care or support side.
4. Rent-to-rent temporary accommodation: work with the council directly.
- Options 2 to 4 are the ones they recommend and teach, to keep it simple for beginners who find social housing confusing.
