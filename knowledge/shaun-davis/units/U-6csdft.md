## Description

How Shaun handled a room that smelt of cannabis during an inspection. He asks to speak to the tenant, names what he can smell, hears the denial, restates the rule without arguing, and closes with a clear instruction and a follow-up inspection. It is short, calm and leaves no doubt about what happens next.

## Details

1. Ask to speak to the tenant: "Can I see you, please?"
2. Name the problem directly: "I can smell weed coming from your room."
3. When the tenant says he smoked outside, describe what you noticed: "It smells really strong coming from your room." and "When you opened the door, it just hit me." Point out that everyone who walked into the house noticed the same thing.
4. Restate the rule: "You can't smoke anywhere on the premises. You can smoke cigarettes, but not weed. There's a park across the road. If you want to do it, just go and do it across the road."
5. Close with the instruction: "Best thing to do then moving forward is just don't. Don't do it at all on the premises."
6. Set the follow-up: "I'm going to come back tomorrow and do the inspection."
