## Description

In every area you operate, you need a plumber, a gas engineer and an electrician ready to call. Do not pick a random name from Google. Use reviews from local Facebook trade groups or Checkatrade to find rated tradespeople.

## Details

- Three trades per area: plumber, gas engineer, electrician
- Avoid: picking a random person off Google
- Method 1: join local Facebook forums and trade groups for a new area and get recommendations and reviews
- Method 2: on Checkatrade, type in the trade and the area to get the top-rated trades for your task
