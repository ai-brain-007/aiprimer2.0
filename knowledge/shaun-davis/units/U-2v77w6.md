## Description

Landlords hear social housing and picture high-need tenants, kicked-in doors and smashed windows. Temporary accommodation is not that. It is ordinary families who are grateful for a home and want to settle down. Correcting this picture is part of winning landlords over.

## Details

- The fear: high-level tenants, doors kicked in, windows smashed.
- The reality in temporary accommodation: normal families on the council register, usually with young kids, who want to get settled.
- Fix: explain the tenant type to the landlord. Nine times out of ten there is no hassle, though issues can arise wherever there are people.
