## Description

Most people think leading means telling others what to do. Shaun says it is about understanding people, genuinely caring and nurturing them. Swap the question from what can I get to what can I give, and ask each person what their goals are and how you can help them get there.

## Details

- Understand people and genuinely care about them
- Leadership is not just telling; it is nurturing and being of service
- Change the question from "what can I get?" to "what can I give?" and "how can I help serve you?"
- Ask each team member what their goals are and how you can help them achieve their personal goals
- Everyone needs something different, so you have to figure people out
