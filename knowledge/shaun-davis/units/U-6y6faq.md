## Description

When builders work on your property, poor work can be hidden the moment a floor goes down, and you only find out when damp appears later. Shaun's rule: watch them, and make sure they are competent rather than just talking a good game. Have competent people you can call to put problems right.

## Details

- Mistake: leaving builders to it and taking their word that the job is right. Consequence: they can hide poor work; once the floor is up you cannot see what is underneath, and the damage shows later as damp, a relocated tenant and a lot of headache.
- Fix: watch builders while they are in your properties and check that they are competent, not just bragging.
- Fix: keep competent people around who can come in, dissolve the problems and get the work done to a good standard.
