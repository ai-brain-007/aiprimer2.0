## Description

Contracts Finder also lists tenders that have not been awarded yet. Keep an eye on those in your area, and when one closes you can be the first to call the winner. You reach the provider before anyone else knows they have a contract.

## Details

1. On Contracts Finder, look at the 'future opportunity' section (the filter you normally untick).
2. It shows the tenders the government has coming up.
3. Track the ones relevant to your areas and keywords.
4. When a tender closes and is awarded, call the winning provider straight away and offer to partner.
