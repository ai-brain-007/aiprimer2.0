## Description

Expect most estate agents to say no. That is normal, not a sign that the model is broken. Keep knocking on doors until one wants to work with you.

## Details

- You will collect a lot of nos and a lot of rejection before you find your agent
- There are many estate agents, so a few refusals cost you nothing
- Keep going until you find one that wants to work with you
