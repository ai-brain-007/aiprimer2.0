## Description

Shaun's closing list of how to train your mind: positive self-talk, train your focus, visualise success, and a no excuses mentality. Each one has its own card; this is the set as he gives it.

## Details

1. Positive self-talk: what you say becomes reality.
2. Train your focus: where focus goes, energy flows and results show.
3. Visualise success: your mind does not know the difference between a vivid picture and reality.
4. Develop a no excuses mentality: success is your duty.
