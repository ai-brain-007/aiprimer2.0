## Description

Sourcing a property for someone earns a one-off fee of perhaps £3,000 to £5,000 and then stops. A back-to-back lease on the same property can pay up to £2,000 a month for the length of a five-year deal. That is why the author calls it sourcing, but better.

## Details

- Sourcing: paid once, maybe £3,000 to £5,000, then nothing
- Back-to-back: residual passive income; a 5-year deal paying up to £2,000 per month and beyond from one property
- The author's aim is residual passive income over time
