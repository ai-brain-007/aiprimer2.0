## Description

When new opportunities keep landing on your desk, the safest move is to double down on the thing that already works. Shaun compares it to watering grass: keep watering and it keeps growing. You can start something new, but never let it take over from the business that is already paying you.

## Details

- Always do what is doing well. If something works, do not give it up; keep doubling down on it.
- Shaun's own case: social housing has worked extremely well since 2020, he has 500 rooms and says he has only scratched the surface, so looking into a new shiny penny is pointless for him.
- Starting something new is not wrong in itself. The rule is to keep the main thing the main thing.
- The mistake to avoid: something is working well, you make a new project the main thing, and you lose the original thing that was good in the first place.
- Reason: whatever you focus on most is what grows. Spread your focus and the original business stops growing.

## Notes

Given as Shaun's answer to Luigi, who was at a crossroads between his existing businesses and starting something new in Dubai.
