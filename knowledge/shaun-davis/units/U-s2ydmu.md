## Description

Marketing is what makes you stand out, and it is what first makes people believe your product is worth paying for. Make your offer look more appealing and more people come through the door and pay more.

## Details

- Marketing is what makes you stand out.
- Marketing creates the initial value perception: before anyone tries your product, the way it looks tells them what it is worth.
- Applied to a stand: nice, beautiful, colourful, with colourful writing, so it looks appealing to someone walking past. Then you can potentially charge more.
- The chain the author draws: more appealing means better marketing, more people through the door, and more money paid to you.
