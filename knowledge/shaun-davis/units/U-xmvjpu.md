## Description

Temporary accommodation and nightly rates come out of a council's own finances. Many councils are going bust from inefficiencies and the cost of living, but their tenants still need housing. That is why single occupants are being moved to the A model, which DWP pays for.

## Details

- Nightly rate temporary accommodation is paid from the council's own funds
- Many councils are going bust: inefficiencies, cost of living and other causes
- A bust council has no funds to house tenants, but the tenants are still there
- Result: single occupants are pushed to the A model, paid directly by DWP, which saves council funds and still meets their obligation
