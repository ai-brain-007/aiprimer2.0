## Description

Car sales taught Shaun that one bad month or one bad valuation gets you sacked on the spot. He says business is the same: there are no second chances. Do the work, drop the excuses and the victim mentality, and get the result.

## Details

- In car sales you always felt one step away from being sacked: one bad move, one bad part exchange valuation, one missed detail.
- People who did well for months were let go after a single bad month.
- Shaun's transfer to business: business is cutthroat and there are no second chances.
- The rules: do the work, do not give excuses, do not have a victim mentality, just get the result.
