## Description

When you pitch a landlord or an agent, put the council at the front of your offer. The rent is council backed and government funded, which gives them confidence in you. Many landlords got stung in Covid, so also point out that this strategy is recession proof.

## Details

- When trying to close a landlord or an agent, lead with the fact that this is a council backed, government funded strategy.
- Effect: it gives the agent or landlord the utmost confidence in you and your strategy.
- Add that the strategy is recession proof. Many landlords, Shaun included, got stung in Covid because of the nationwide crisis, so this lands.
- Combine with the guaranteed rent message: the rent is paid directly by the council, so it really is guaranteed.
