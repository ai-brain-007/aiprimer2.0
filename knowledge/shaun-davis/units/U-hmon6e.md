## Description

The academy community works because everyone moves in the same direction and helps each other up. When someone brings negativity in, the hosts tell them straight that it will not be tolerated. Protecting the group's energy is part of leading it.

## Details

- Keep everyone moving the same direction: growing, pushing to the next level, closing deals, helping each other.
- When negativity appears, address it directly: 'this will not be tolerated, we are a positive community, we lift each other up'.
