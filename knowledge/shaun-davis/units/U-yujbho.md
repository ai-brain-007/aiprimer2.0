## Description

When a problem hits, own your part in it. The speaker says most problems happen because you did not have something in place, did not do something, made a mistake or have a bad habit. Finding that cause is the lesson the problem is teaching you.

## Details

- Take accountability that the thing happened because of something on your side.
- The usual causes the speaker lists: you do not have something in place, you have not done something, you made a mistake, or you have a bad habit. It could be anything, but most of the time it traces back to one of these.
- Pair it with the question: what is this teaching me?
