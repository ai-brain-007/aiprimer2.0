## Description

Shaun's serviced accommodation business depended on Booking.com and Airbnb for its bookings. When they shut their calendars in COVID he had no bookings and was still paying all the rent. He calls it a schoolboy error. The fix is to take bookings into your own hands and never rely fully on a partner.

## Details

- The mistake: not having enough direct bookings, so all the income depended on online travel platforms.
- The consequence: when Booking.com and Airbnb shut their calendars overnight and no one was allowed to travel, the bookings stopped while the rent kept going out.
- The fix: always take things into your own hands and do not rely on partners.
