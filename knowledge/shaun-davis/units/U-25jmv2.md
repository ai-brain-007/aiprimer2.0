## Description

Landlords burned by social housing have told Shaun flatly they will never do business with him. He keeps going, meets them face to face, and the guard drops once there is a relationship. He tells it to show that a no over the phone is not the end.

## Details

- The landlord had a bad experience with social housing and said categorically: I will not do any business with you.
- Shaun kept pushing and pushing, then met them face to face.
- In person, the landlord let their guard down because there was now a relationship, and the deal followed.
- The same pattern happened with landlords who were offered more money elsewhere but chose him because they trusted him.
