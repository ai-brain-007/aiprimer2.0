## Description

Ambitious people carry a constant feeling that they are not doing enough. Shaun calls it the curse of being an entrepreneur. Knowing it is a built-in feeling, not a true signal, helps you resist chasing every new idea.

## Details

- The feeling shows up even when things are going well; Shaun says he sometimes sits in bed thinking he needs to do more, more, more.
- It makes it hard to find balance and to decide where to spend your time.
- Shaun's answer to it: keep doing what is doing well rather than adding more.
