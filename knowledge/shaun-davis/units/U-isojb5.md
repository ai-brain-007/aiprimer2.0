## Description

Shaun's rule for a crisis. When your back is against the wall you have two options, sink or come up swinging, and he chooses swinging. He now says he is glad it happened to him, because the pressure forced him into action he might never have taken otherwise.

## Details

- When your back is against the wall there are only two options: you sink or you come up swinging.
- The mindset behind it: he had worked hard for 18 months to build something and refused to let it crash down within weeks; he describes himself as a very resilient person.
- Sometimes your back needs to be against the wall for you to take massive action. In his case he had no choice but to take it.
- Looking back, he tells people he is glad it happened to him that way.
