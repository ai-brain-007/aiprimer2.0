## Description

Spending money on things you do not need drains your pot until almost nothing is left. The author's first lesson is to understand how money works instead of just spending it.

## Details

- The error: spending what you have on things you do not need.
- The consequence: the cup gets emptier and you are left with only a little.
- The fix: understand how money works before you spend it, refill the cup through a job, an investment or a business, and treat money as seeds to plant rather than eat.
