## Description

Once the council is boxed off, the fastest way to a deal in a new town is to walk into every lettings agent on the high street, one after another. You pitch, ask for viewings today, and when one says no you keep moving. Three agents in one afternoon produced one viewing and a chauffeur.

## Details

1. Make the plan for the next few days: visit every estate agent in the town.
2. Walk in, ask for lettings, build rapport, find common ground, keep the pitch simple.
3. Ask about three and four beds and whether you can view today.
4. If an agent says no, that is fine. There are loads of agents. Keep it moving, go to the next one.
5. Expect to win some and lose some; judge the day by the total, not by one door.

Day one result: three agents visited before 5pm, one same-day viewing. Next step he mentions: go direct to vendor as well.
