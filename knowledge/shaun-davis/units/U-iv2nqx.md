## Description

When a landlord asks what you are offering, say who you work for and why their property fits. Then answer the worry behind the question, which is usually protecting the property. Keep it to a few sentences and let them ask.

## Details

Opening:
- "We work with the council. We've been tasked to find them three to four bed properties to house families, and your property fits our standards."

When the landlord says they want a secure tenancy and to protect the property:
- "That's what we do. We cover everything."



The landlord's typical concern in the source: "I need to have a secure tenancy agreement. That's all I'm really worried about. I want to protect my property."

## Notes

Delivered by Sandu with Shaun beside him, in a viewing where the landlord had started off cold.
