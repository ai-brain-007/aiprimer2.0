## Description

New businesses struggle with cash up front. The student treats the incentive as a separate pot from monthly earnings. He uses some of it to pay bills when needed and rolls the rest into acquiring the next property.

## Details

1. Keep the incentive separate from your monthly earnings.
2. Use some of it to pay bills if you need to.
3. Roll the rest into another property, found through OpenRent, a similar site, or a sourcer.
