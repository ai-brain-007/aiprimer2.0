## Description

Sean tried to be his own bookkeeper in year one to save costs. His accounts were a mess, year end was a nightmare and the accountant charged him more to redo everything from scratch. The fix is simple: get a bookkeeper or accountant from day one.

## Details

- The error: trying to do everything yourself and penny pinch on costs, including inputting your own books without knowing what you are doing.
- The consequences Sean hit: accounts in a mess at the end of his first year; an absolute nightmare trying to figure things out; the accountant charging more because he had to go through all the books and redo everything from scratch because it was all wrong.
- The bigger cost: in the long run the penny pinching sabotaged his success and slowed him down.
- The fix: get a bookkeeper or an accountant straight away who can manage your books. If starting again he would not even entertain doing it himself.
