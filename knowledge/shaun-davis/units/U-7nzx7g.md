## Description

Social media shows you people twenty steps ahead of you, and some of what you see is not even real. Comparing your beginning to their highlight leaves you discouraged. Shaun's answer is to be authentic, be who you are and go for it.

## Details

- The mistake: comparing your step one to someone else's step twenty on social media.
- The consequence: you get discouraged, and some of what you are comparing yourself to is not even real.
- The fix: be authentic, be who you are, and really go for it.
- Social media can be a blessing and a curse; this is the curse side.
