## Description

Shaun does not treat negotiation as pressure. It is helping the other party understand the choice they already face, laid out honestly. When the deal is genuinely better for them, they say yes on their own, and the best deals are wins for everyone involved.

## Details

- Lay out the other side's current setup and the options open to them.
- Show how each option affects their work, risk and pay.
- Aim for a deal that is a win for everyone: agent, landlord, tenant and you.
