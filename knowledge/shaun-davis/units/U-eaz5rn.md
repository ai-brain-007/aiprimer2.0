## Description

Before you buy or lease anything, find out what the council actually needs. Shaun says every business starts with due diligence, and here that means understanding the demand and the tenant type in the council you want to work with. Do not assume demand exists; check it.

## Details

- Any business or investment: do your own research and due diligence.
- For temporary accommodation, understand two things first: the demand and the tenant type.
- Make sure the specific council you plan to work with has sufficient demand before you go further.
