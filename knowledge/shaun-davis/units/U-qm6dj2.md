## Description

Words Shaun suggests a baker use when walking into a local shop. You introduce yourself, show the range, point to your reviews, and leave free tasters with no pressure. It is written to give value first and make the shop's answer easy.

## Details

Opening:
"Hey, I'm a baker. I bake cakes, and I can do a variety. This is what I've got. I've got some great feedback."

With reviews in hand:
"Look, this is my Google reviews. This is what it is. I'd love the opportunity to sell these cakes in your shop. Here's the tasters. They're free. Try them and let me know what you think."

Offering terms after a yes:
"Okay, don't pay me nothing up front. I'll leave them there and let's see if you can sell them."
or
"Look, this is the retail, and this is what I'm going to sell it to you for."

What not to say as an opener: "You want my cakes? You want to sell my cakes?"
