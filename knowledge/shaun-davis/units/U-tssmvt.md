## Description

Getting ignored and reaching the wrong person is normal. Treat outreach as a numbers game, build resilience, and make sure you reach a decision maker rather than wasting calls on people who cannot say yes. Use a team so the volume does not fall on you.

## Details

- Expect to be ignored; it is a numbers game and you need resilience
- Do not waste time pitching people who cannot decide; speak to the decision maker
- Councils are very busy and do not need time wasters
- Use leverage: a team member can do the calling and follow-up for a fraction of what you would expect
