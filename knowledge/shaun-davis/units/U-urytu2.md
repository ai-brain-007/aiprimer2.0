## Description

You do not need thousands in the bank before you start. Most insurances can be arranged after your first deal, so the order is: close a no money down deal, get the money in, then buy the cover. This is how students with no savings get going.

## Details

1. Structure the first deal so it needs no money down.
2. Get the money coming in as quickly as possible.
3. As soon as the money arrives, get all your insurances boxed off.
- Most insurances, though not all, can be taken out after the first deal.
- Many students do not have thousands ready, so this order is how the academy structures it.
