## Description

Before property, Shaun sold cars on a low basic salary and high commission. The basic did not cover his bills, so every month he had to sell or go short. He says that pressure was the best preparation for business, because business is constant pressure too.

## Details

- Car sales: a low basic wage and high commissions. If you are not a hard worker and do not get up and sell cars, you make no money.
- His basic salary was not enough to cover his bills, so his back was against the wall every month.
- Most people would not like that level of pressure, but business is constant pressure, so when he moved into business he was already used to handling it and the transition was easier.
- Two lessons he carried from car sales into business: the power of showing up and simply doing the work, and the discovery that the best sellers were not the most talented but the most consistent (see those cards).
- He started in business because he wanted to create a financial future for himself and his family and take it into his own hands.
