## Description

Landlords and agents who were burned by social housing before will still work with you if they believe in you personally. Build enough rapport and they stop seeing the old tenant and start seeing you. Shaun says this holds even for landlords who swore off social housing.

## Details

- Many of Shaun's landlords had bad experiences with social housing before he met them.
- If they truly believe in you because you have done the work of building rapport, the past will not matter. They are now dealing with you.
- Show them character, reliability, trustworthiness and competence and they will want to work with you.
