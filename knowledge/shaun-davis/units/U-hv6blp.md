## Description

People shy away from joint ventures because they hate giving away half the profit. The hosts flip it: in a JV you never have to pay anyone back, expectations are set from the start, and you can do far more deals with far less risk. The trade-off is that a bad partner is very stressful.

## Details

- With a JV partner you split the profit; their money is tied up in the deal, but there is no repayment deadline hanging over you because the expectation is set at the start.
- With borrowed finance you keep the profit but must repay on a date whether or not the project is done.
- Volume argument: splitting profit but doing many more deals beats keeping all the profit on a few.
- Risk argument: much less risk because there is no finance to pay back.
- Downside: a bad JV partner is very stressful. Luigi says about 95% of his partners have been amazing and he has learned a lot.
