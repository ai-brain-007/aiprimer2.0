## Description

By the time a property appears on Rightmove, every other investor can see it too. You end up in a crowd and deals are hard to close. Shaun rarely uses it and sources off market through agents instead.

## Details

- The mistake: looking for rent to rent properties on Rightmove.
- The consequence: by the time a property gets onto Rightmove you are competing with everyone else, and it becomes quite hard to close.
- The fix: close off market deals by building relationships with agents in the area, so they send you properties before they are listed.
