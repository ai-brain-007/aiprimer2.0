## Description

Register each tenant on a direct debit portal so their top-up is collected automatically and you are told the moment something goes wrong. The author uses a service the transcript calls Gold Cardless. The most valuable alert is a cancelled direct debit, which lets you ring the tenant before rent is lost.

## Details

1. Register the tenant on the portal
2. The portal tells you three things: when a payment succeeds, when a payment fails, and when the tenant cancels the direct debit
3. On a cancellation, get straight on the phone to the tenant, find out why and mitigate any loss of rent before it happens

## Notes

The transcript spells the service Gold Cardless, which is probably a mishearing of the direct debit provider GoCardless. The author says he is not sponsored by them.
