## Description

The steps Sean's organisation follows with each supported accommodation tenant. Assess the risks and needs on arrival, write a plan made for that person, work it weekly with a support worker, and aim to have them living independently in 6 to 12 months.

## Details

1. When the tenant comes in, do a full risk assessment.
2. Make sure they understand exactly what their needs are and how you are going to support them.
3. Create a support plan for that tenant. It is tailor made, not generic.
4. The support worker goes into the tenant with that plan every week to support them.
5. Goal: move them from homeless, into your accommodation as a safe and secure environment, and back out into independent living within 6 to 12 months. Sean also describes working on their mindset and skill set to get them back into employment in the same window.

Why the stop gap is needed: going straight from high mental health need and unemployment back into society is, most of the time, not viable.
