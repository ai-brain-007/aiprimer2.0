## Description

A company let means the tenancy is in your company's name, not the occupant's, so your company is responsible for the property. For the landlord it means you take the tenant, the maintenance and the rent risk off their hands under one contract. Agents will ask whether you "do company lets", and their answer decides whether you can work with them; big chains and some independents restrict it.

## Details

- What it is: you are not renting directly to the tenant. You rent to the company, and the company is responsible. Shaun calls this the game changer for the landlord.
- What the landlord gets:
  - You manage the tenant.
  - You manage the maintenance, including on the way in.
  - You guarantee the rent, paid in full even if the house sits empty for 12 months.
  - You sign a contract and both sides are locked in for the whole term.
- The question agents ask: "Do you guys do company lets?"
- Restrictions you will meet:
  - Nationwide brands (Connells) can only do company lets when a business rents for its own employees.
  - Some agents have changed their rules: if the company is a letting agent or is subletting, they will not do it.
  - In the end the landlord decides what kind of people they accept, including whether a company can be the tenant.
