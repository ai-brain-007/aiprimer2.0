## Description

Entrepreneurs have to decide quickly and then commit. Overthinking turns into procrastination and no decision at all. Fast decisions also make people want to work with you, and they keep you level with competitors who live and breathe their business.

## Details

- Decision-making is crucial as an entrepreneur. Make the decision and commit to it, quickly.
- Overthinking leads to procrastinating and never deciding. Use the best case, worst case, likely case analysis to cut through it.
- People love to work with people who can make fast decisions. Velocity matters.
- The people you compete with are on it, living and breathing the business, absolutely obsessed. If you take ages to think about it and do not decide quickly, you will always fall short.
