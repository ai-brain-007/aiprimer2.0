## Description

You do not need many partners. One provider that wants to scale is enough to build an income, because you keep adding properties for them. Shaun puts the margin at a minimum of £500 to £1,000 per month.

## Details

- Only one provider is needed to change your life, provided they are looking to scale and take things to the next level.
- Scale with that provider property by property.
- Margin: a minimum of £500 to £1,000 per month.

## Notes

Shaun does not say explicitly whether the £500 to £1,000 is per property; the earlier £800 example suggests it is.
