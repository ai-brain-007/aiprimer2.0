## Description

Shaun's worked numbers for a back-to-back lease. A provider offers 2,500 pounds a month for a three-bedroom property in London or the south, you negotiate 2,000 pounds with the landlord, and you sign both leases. The 500 pound gap is your income every month.

## Details

1. The provider says it will pay 2,500 pounds for a three-bedroom property in London or somewhere down south.
2. You negotiate the property and might pay the landlord 2,000 pounds.
3. Sign a lease with the landlord for 2,000 pounds and a lease with the provider for 2,500 pounds.
4. You make 500 pounds every single month for doing, in his words, absolutely zero.
