## Description

A week before Sandu walked in, another temporary accommodation provider pitched the same agency and was turned away because they did not like him. Sandu spent half an hour being friendly and showing genuine interest, and came out with a viewing. Same business, different person.

## Details

- The competitor was refused on personality, not on the model
- Sandu chatted for about half an hour, talked about the agent's home country, school and traditions
- The agent booked him a viewing on a £1,500 property and agreed to put the company let to the landlord
