## Description

Shaun's coaching method over the two days: on day one he does the agent visits while the learner watches; on day two the learner leads and he watches, steps in only if they struggle, and debriefs on what could have been better. You learn this by doing it, not by hearing it.

## Details

1. Day one: the coach does a few visits so the learner can see how it is done.
2. Day two: the learner takes the lead at every agent. The coach is there to watch, coach and advise.
3. The coach steps in only if the agent asks lots of questions and the learner is visibly struggling.
4. After each visit: show exactly where it went wrong and what could have been done better.
5. Reason: that is how you learn, by actually doing it.

## Notes

The learner's own reflection after day one: knowledge in the game gives you the confidence.
