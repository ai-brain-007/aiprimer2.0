## Description

A six-bed new build had serious damp in room one and the kitchen. The cause was outside: the ground had been built up too high against the wall, bridging the damp, and drainpipes were not connected into the gullies, so waste water ran down the side of the house. The room had to be relocated and the ground dug out and re-laid.

## Details

What was wrong:
- The ground level outside was too high, bridging the damp and letting dampness into the building
- Drains were not connected into the gullies, so water ran down the side of the property
- The high floor hid the problem: nobody could see that the pipes were not connected
- Damp appeared all along that side, in room one and in the kitchen

Consequences:
- Major issues, a lot of headache, and the tenant in room one had to be relocated to another property

The repair:
1. Dig out the ground to its correct level
2. Connect the pipes into the gullies so waste water is taken away
3. Lay slabs, planned to be finished in one day
4. Big clean up and fixing everything the next day

Lesson Shaun draws: the original build was a terrible job; you need competent people who can come in, dissolve the problem and get it done to a good standard.

## Notes

Explained on site by Chris, the contractor doing the repair, with Shaun.
