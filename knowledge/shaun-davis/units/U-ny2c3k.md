## Description

Shaun's darkest business moment, from 2023. A social housing provider he put deals through was a franchise; the head office cancelled the franchise and Shaun was left owed about £60,000 the day before payday, with eight staff to pay and a month's holiday booked. He raised £100,000 in finance, stayed calm, and recovered every penny from the council three months later.

## Details

- Setup: working with a registered social housing provider that paid Shaun for deals put through them. The provider was a franchise of another head office.
- Trigger: a mix-up with head office; head office cancelled the franchise; the provider owed Shaun about £60,000.
- Timing: the day before payday. About eight staff, rent due, his own pay, and a month-long holiday about to start.
- Response: no panic. He became resourceful and raised £100,000 in finance to get through.
- Recovery: he called the council and got every penny back, but only after three months.
