## Description

Shaun's step-by-step advice to a tenant starting a cake business on how to get shops to stock it. You make tiny free tasters, walk the row of local shops, lead with what you offer rather than a sales pitch, back it with Google reviews, and take the risk off the shop with sale or return.

## Details

1. Make very small taster versions of the product, for example mini muffins.
2. Walk along the local road where there are lots of shops and go in with the tasters.
3. Come from a place of value, not from trying to sell. Introduce yourself as a baker, show the variety you make, mention the good feedback you have had.
4. Set up a Google page for the business first, so customers can leave reviews. Shaun offered to write one himself as a customer. Then collect more from other people.
5. In the shop, show the Google reviews, say you would love the opportunity to sell in their shop, and leave the free tasters for them to try and get back to you.
6. When they come back with a yes, offer terms. Option one: sale or return, no payment up front, leave the product and see if it sells, then agree the price. Option two: state the retail price and the price you sell to the shop for.
