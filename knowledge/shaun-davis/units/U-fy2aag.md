## Description

This is how Shaun opens with a council and what he asks before he hangs up. You introduce yourself as a provider with access to properties, then ask the questions that tell you whether the numbers work. Luton Council said yes to taking on more within one call.

## Details

Before you dial: make sure you are through to the temporary accommodation department, not a general line.

Opening:
"Hi, it's Sean. We're a temporary accommodation provider and we've got access to quite a lot of properties in the local area. We're looking at working with the council to provide temporary accommodation. Is this something that you guys are still offering at the moment?"

Then establish exactly what they need. Write the answers down:
1. What size properties do you need? (Luton: three and four beds.)
2. Where specifically? (Anywhere in Luton.)
3. How does the process work, in terms of working with you and price?
4. What are your nightly rates at the moment?
5. Are the rates negotiable?
6. What is your current demand? Do you have a lot of tenants on the waiting list?
7. Who covers the bills, the tenant or us? Who does repairs?

Close:
"Leave it with me. We're going to have to crunch some numbers now, speak to our network, and see what we can get and take it from there."
