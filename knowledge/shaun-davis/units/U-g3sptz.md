## Description

Asked what is hardest about being your own boss, Sean names two things: loneliness, especially in the early days, and a heavy load of stress you have to learn to manage. Both have a remedy he explains elsewhere.

## Details

1. Loneliness. Sean felt it strongly in the early days. He no longer feels it because he has a community around him, and wishes he had built that community earlier rather than later.
2. The heavy load of stress, and understanding how to manage it. His answer is learning to recalibrate your mind with activities that reset you.
