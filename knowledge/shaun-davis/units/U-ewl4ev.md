## Description

Social housing providers get paid to house the tenant and paid again to support the tenant. Because they make money on both sides, they can afford to pay you a bit above market rent to get properties quickly. This is why a back-to-back margin exists.

## Details

- With most social housing strategies the provider is paid to house the tenant and paid to support the tenant.
- Making money from both ends means they are willing to pay a bit of a premium to acquire the property.
- Companies looking to scale pay a premium to acquire properties fast, because more properties mean more revenue for them.
