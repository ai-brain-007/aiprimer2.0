## Description

Shaun's stated reason for loving social housing is the daily impact on people. Tenants arrive from every walk of life, some homeless, some sleeping in cars or on the street, and the job is to give them a safe space, good accommodation and real support. Hold this as the standard for what your houses are for.

## Details

- Tenants come from all walks of life; some are homeless, some are sleeping in their cars or in the streets
- What the business is there to provide: a safe space, quality accommodation and quality support
- Shaun says the business gets to genuinely change lives, and even save lives, on a daily basis
- Tenant stories in the video: a man evicted over rent arrears, a long-standing tenant now starting a cake business, a man told to sleep rough after the hospital was full, a man whose partner put him out when he lost his job
