## Description

A viewing takes five minutes. Michael spends the best part of an hour with the landlord and talks about anything except the property. Get them talking about themselves, find common ground, and the deal takes care of itself.

## Details

- Budget an hour for a viewing that only needs five minutes
- Talk about whatever the landlord is into: golf, tennis, anything, and follow it wherever it goes
- Say nothing about the deal or the figures at this stage
- Aim to get them talking about themselves and to find common ground; you want to know them as a person and let them know you
- Shaun does exactly the same in his own landlord conversations
