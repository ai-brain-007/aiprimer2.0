## Description

Luigi's darkest moment: a fixed-return investor was owed £50,000, the remortgage ran three weeks late, and the investor's insolvency solicitors served a statutory demand giving 14 days to pay. Luigi sold shares in one of his properties, paid the investor, then completed the remortgage and bought more with the surplus.

## Details

- Deal: a private investor on a fixed return, owed £50,000 at the end of the agreement. Luigi had kept him updated that the remortgage was about to complete.
- Three weeks late, the investor went to insolvency solicitors and had a statutory demand served at Luigi's door.
- The demand gave 14 days to pay before court action.
- Fix: sold shares in one of his properties for £50,000 and repaid the investor.
- Afterwards the remortgage came through, leaving extra cash he used to buy more properties.
- Lesson he drew: leverage your own assets rather than borrow more from investors and risk the same thing again. He now has only one very small investor.
