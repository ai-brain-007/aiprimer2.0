## Description

Shaun's starting rule for 2026: if he had to begin again with nothing, he would build monthly cash flow before saving for a buy-to-let deposit. Owning property stays the end goal, but cash flow gets you out of your job and funds the journey. You do not wait years for your first purchase.

## Details

- Do not start by trying to save huge deposits for buy-to-lets, and do not spend years waiting to buy your first property.
- Do not sit there saying you need money before you get started.
- Owning the asset is still the end goal, because it is always good to own the asset.
- Use quick cash flow strategies such as rent-to-rent and rent-to-social housing to build income fast.
- Run them alongside your property development journey, not instead of it.
