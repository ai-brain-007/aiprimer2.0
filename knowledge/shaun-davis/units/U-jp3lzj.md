## Description

Large chains are run from a head office that cannot bend its rules, so they rarely take a company let. Shaun prefers small independent agents who decide locally. He still walks into the chains, staying positive but realistic, because it costs nothing to ask.

## Details

- Large estate agents are controlled by a head office. Head office cannot do it; Connells could only rent to a business housing its own employees.
- Independent, smaller agents make their own decisions and are the ones to look for.
- Do not expect to close the nationwide brands on the high street, but still try. Stay positive but realistic.
