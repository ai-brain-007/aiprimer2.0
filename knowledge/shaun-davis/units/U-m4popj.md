## Description

On a zero-operations deal, the question is not how much rent you make but how fast you get your money back. Shaun looks at the capital he puts in and how quickly he can pull it back out to use again. The faster it comes back, the sooner it can work on the next deal.

## Details

- Look at Local Authority Leasing deals from a return on investment point of view, because there are very minimal operations.
- Ask: what capital am I putting in, and how quickly can I get that capital back out?
- Typical capital Shaun mentions: £2,000, £3,000 or £5,000 into a deal.
- Goal: get the money back out fast so it can start working again on the next deal.
