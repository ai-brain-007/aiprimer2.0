## Description

When you approach a shop or any potential customer, offer something useful first instead of asking them to buy. Shaun's point is that a cold ask gets "who are you?", while a free taster and a clear offer gets a conversation. Give first, then let them come back to you.

## Details

- Wrong approach: walk in and ask "you want my cakes? you want to sell my cakes?" The answer is "No, who are you?"
- Right approach: introduce yourself and what you make, offer free tasters, show your feedback, and ask for the opportunity
- Let them try it and get back to you, rather than pushing for a decision on the spot
