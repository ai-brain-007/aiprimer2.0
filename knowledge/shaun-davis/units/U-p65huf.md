## Description

Deal sourcing means finding investors, finding and analysing property deals, and selling those deals to the investors for a fee. It is cheap to set up and the lowest-risk of the three strategies, but you have to keep finding new investors and new deals every month.

## Details

- Set-up cost: about £3,000 for compliance.
- Earnings: around £3,000 to £5,000 per deal on average.
- The work: go out every day and find investors. Then find the properties, analyse the deals and make sure they stack up. Then sell the deal to the investor.
- Risk: the lowest of the three. You are not committing to anything long term. Once the investor accepts the deal it is their responsibility.
- Downside: you start every month on a blank canvas, chasing investors and deals again.
- Needs a proper contract with clauses so the investor cannot come back later and say the deal does not work.
