## Description

Shaun uses lottery winners to show why character matters more than money. He says 80% of people who win millions are broke within five years. They never built the character or resilience, they do not know how to spend, and they only consume, and consuming does not grow you.

## Details

- Claim: 80% of millionaires who win the lottery are broke after 5 years
- Why, in his view:
  - They have not built the character
  - They do not have the resilience
  - They do not understand how to spend
  - They are consumers, and consuming does not make you grow
- The alternative: give value instead of only consuming

## Notes

Shaun gives no source for the 80% figure.
