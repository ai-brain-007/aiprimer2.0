## Description

What people pay is driven by what they believe something is worth, not just by what it does. Brands build that belief on purpose through marketing and design. Once you see this, you can raise the price of a simple product without changing what it does.

## Details

- Two watches both tell the time. One sells for about £50, the other from £10,000. The difference is perception.
- Rolex has created the perception that its watch is a luxurious item, that people want it, and that it is worth a lot of money.
- They built that perception through marketing and clever design.
- Lesson three in one line: value perception is key, make people want to give you their money.
- The author shows two ways to create the same effect with a simple product (see the lemonade stand).
