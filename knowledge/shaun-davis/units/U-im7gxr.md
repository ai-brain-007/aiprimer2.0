## Description

Success comes from turning up every day and doing the work whether or not you feel like it. Shaun says that if he relied on his emotions he would not show up half the time. He built the habit instead, so that his feelings and his actions are no longer connected.

## Details

- Show up every single day and do the work, regardless of how you feel. It is a crucial component of success.
- Do not rely on your emotions or your motivation to get you working. Build a habit instead. Shaun did the work on himself to build the habit and now shows up automatically.
- He admits there are many days he wakes up and genuinely does not feel like showing up. He has repeated it so often that there is no correlation between how he feels and the action he takes.
- This is how winners win, time and time again: not a gifted, motivated personality, but the willingness to show up regardless.
- Where motivation fades, discipline and habit carry you (see the motivation and discipline card).
