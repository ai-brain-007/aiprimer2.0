## Description

Shaun judges every business by two questions: does it solve a big enough problem, and am I creating value? He sees these as core traits of an entrepreneur. Temporary accommodation passes the test because it houses people who have nowhere else to go.

## Details

- Trait number one of an entrepreneur: creating value and solving a problem.
- Before joining any business, ask: is this solving a big enough problem? Am I creating value?
- Shaun believes temporary accommodation does exactly that, and that you get to make a genuine, huge impact.
