## Description

When several providers want the same house, the one who gets the council inspector out first wins. Move the moment a landlord shows interest. In the video another agent had enquired first but lost because they were slow to call the council.

## Details

- Call the council the same day and ask when a surveyor can come; take any cancellation slot
- Call the landlord immediately to fix the time
- Viewing first can earn you first refusal from the landlord
- Time is of the essence from the viewing to the keys; the Dave deal went from cold viewing to keys in under 24 hours
