## Description

One of Sean's two social housing strategies. You house homeless people who are unemployed and have a mental health need, and you provide specific support based on their needs, with the aim of moving them to independent living within 6 to 12 months. It is more complex than temporary accommodation, with more red tape.

## Details

- Who you house: the homeless, specifically people who are unemployed and have a mental health need.
- Your two responsibilities: house the tenant, and provide support to the tenant. Not random support: specific support based on their needs.
- How: a full risk assessment when the tenant comes in, a tailor-made support plan, and a support worker who visits weekly with that plan.
- The aim: from homeless, to a safe and secure environment in your accommodation, to independent living within 6 to 12 months.
- Complexity: more high level and more red tape than temporary accommodation. It works differently in different councils; Sean only does it in Birmingham.
- Income: paid directly by the government.
