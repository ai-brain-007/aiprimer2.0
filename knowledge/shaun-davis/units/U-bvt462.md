## Description

Every time you take on a property, knock on both neighbours' doors, introduce yourself and leave your name, number and work email. If a problem arises they call you, not the landlord or the council. Complaints that reach either of those make you look incompetent.

## Details

1. On taking a new property, visit the neighbours on either side in person; the author did this himself at first and now sends a housing manager
2. Give them your contact name, phone number and work email address
3. Any issue then comes to you
- Why: complaints to a rent-to-rent landlord or to the busy council make your business look incompetent
- You are the provider and responsible for your tenants, for the house being managed well and for the neighbourhood being happy
