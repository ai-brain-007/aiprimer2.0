## Description

Sean boils outreach down to three things. Keep a list, get to the person who can say yes, and mention their contract. Do all three and you stand out from the crowd.

## Details

1. Build a contact list of providers (from the council and Contracts Finder).
2. Find the decision makers inside each provider.
3. Reference their contract when reaching out so that you stand out.
