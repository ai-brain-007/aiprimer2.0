## Description

When a council tells you it no longer pays nightly rates, or has changed or is overspending, do not hang up. Ask where the tenants are going. The council has to house them somewhere, and that question leads you to the new scheme.

## Details

Situation: you call a council and they say something like 'we don't do nightly rates any more, we've changed', or 'we're overspending'.

Say: 'Okay, well what's happening with all those tenants? Where are you putting those tenants?'

Why it works: they have to put them somewhere, so the answer tells you which new scheme, process or landlord opportunity they have created.
