## Description

Because social housing is paid directly by the government, Sean can promise a landlord three things private tenants cannot: a long-term contract, genuinely guaranteed rent and a zero-hassle, fully managed property. Cover all three and there is very little reason left to say no.

## Details

1. Long-term contracts. Possible because you are paid directly by the government.
2. Genuinely guaranteed rent. Same reason.
3. Zero hassle. You manage the whole property, so if the landlord has confidence in you they will believe it will be hassle-free.

These map onto what landlords want: profitable, secure, long-term, no headaches. Cover all three and you leave very little reason to refuse the offer.
