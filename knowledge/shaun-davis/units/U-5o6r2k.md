## Description

Do not post a contract and wait. Email the commercial lease for digital signing to both parties, use software that tells you when the landlord opens it, and phone them to say it is on its way and you are happy to explain anything.

## Details

1. Prepare the commercial lease with the agreed terms.
2. Send it to your own email and the landlord's for digital signing.
3. Watch the open notification from the signing software so you know when to expect questions.
4. Call the landlord: "I'm just letting you know we've sent across the contract. If there's anything you don't understand, just give me a call. More than happy to assist."
5. Once signed, you are good to go.
