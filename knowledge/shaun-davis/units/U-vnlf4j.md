## Description

Motivation is not steady. It shows up in bursts, usually at the start of something new or when things go well, then it fades. That is when discipline and habit have to take over. Build the habit of showing up while you are motivated so it is there when you are not.

## Details

- Motivation comes in bursts, typically at the start of something new or when things are going well.
- When motivation fades, discipline needs to occur.
- So show up regardless of how you feel and build the habits now, so that when motivation fades you still show up.
- Discipline is hard to build outside your purpose (see the purpose card).
- Everyone feels different levels and bursts of motivation at different times. Winners are not gifted with a motivated personality; they show up regardless.
