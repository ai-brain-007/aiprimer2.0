## Description

Most people think they need £50k to £200k before they can touch property. The author says the real blocker is bad information, not cash. He started with under £10,000 and now runs hundreds of rooms across the UK.

## Details

- The common belief: you need £50k, £100k or even £200k to get started.
- The author's own start: less than £10,000. Today he has hundreds of rooms across the UK housing people every day.
- What actually stops people is misinformation: believing property is only about owning houses.
- Closing advice: do not let the idea of needing big money hold you back.
