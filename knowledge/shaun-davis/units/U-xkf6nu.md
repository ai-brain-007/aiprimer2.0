## Description

Money is worth having because it gives you choices. Without it you have none, and even generous goals like helping family or people in need take money. You cannot change the world while you are broke.

## Details

- Money gives you options; without money you have no options.
- Changing the world and doing charitable things takes money; you cannot do it if you are broke.
- What the kids said being rich would let them do, all confirmed by the author: take the family on holiday by jet, help family, help friends, and go to a homeless shelter to donate food or money.
