## Description

A direct-to-vendor deal means you deal with the landlord yourself, with no agent in between. It closes faster because there are no agency credit checks on your company. In the video the landlord never asked how long the company had been trading or for personal guarantees.

## Details

- Found through OpenRent, where you message the landlord directly and book the viewing
- No credit checks, so much easier and simpler to get over the line than an agent-introduced property
- The landlord did not ask for trading history (the company was 3 months old) or personal guarantees
- Rapport replaced paperwork: the landlord checked with his partner and went ahead
