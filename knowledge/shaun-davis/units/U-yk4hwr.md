## Description

Both hosts describe Dubai as a place where strangers talk business in hotel lobbies and where you can leave your door unlocked. They put the safety down to respect for the law and for the leader, which they contrast with the UK.

## Details

- People in Dubai are entrepreneurial and open for business; Luigi met business contacts by chatting to strangers in a hotel lobby.
- It is safe enough not to lock your door.
- Their explanation: everyone respects the law and everyone respects the leader, whereas in the UK they feel no one respects the law.
- Neither host knows how the legal system actually works; this is their impression.

## Notes

Personal impressions from a short stay, not researched claims. Luigi had been in Dubai about six weeks.
