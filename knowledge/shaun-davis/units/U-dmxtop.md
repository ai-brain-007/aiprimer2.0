## Description

Your rent to the landlord is fixed for three years, but the council rate is not. After twelve months go back and ask for more. Any increase goes straight to your profit in years two and three.

## Details

- Fixed cost: the landlord's rent under the 36-month lease
- After one year, ask the council to raise its payment
- The £500 monthly profit rises in years two and three by whatever increase you secure
