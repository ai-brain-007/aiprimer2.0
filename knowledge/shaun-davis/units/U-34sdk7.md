## Description

Of all the rent-to-rent models Michael has tried, temporary accommodation needs the least of his time. Tenants treat the place as their home and look after it. When something breaks, you make a call and a tradesperson fixes it, so you can run the business from anywhere.

## Details

- Tenants look after much of the property themselves because it is their home
- When hands-on work is needed, it is minimal
- You are not the plumber or electrician: when something goes wrong, call someone out to fix it
- Typical effort on a property like his second one: about two phone calls and 5 minutes a month
- Inspections can be outsourced to inventory companies, so you never need to visit
- Result: you can be anywhere in the world and still be on top of every problem
