## Description

The biggest lesson Shaun learned while scaling. You cannot do everything yourself, so you hand the work to experienced people and move into the role of manager and facilitator. He says the business only started to grow once he did this.

## Details

- The team is crucial. You cannot do everything yourself.
- Letting go means hiring people who are experienced at all the different levels of the business.
- Your new role: facilitator and manager. Manage the people, empower them, and make sure they can do their job to the best of their ability.
- That frees you to manage, lead and grow the business.
- Shaun struggled with this at first and says it was essentially the point when the business started to grow.

## Notes

The interviewer picks this out as one of the two gems of the conversation.
