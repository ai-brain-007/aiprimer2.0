## Description

Most placements under the nightly rate model are ordinary families who cause no trouble. Occasionally a council will place a desperate tenant with mental health problems or a drug or alcohol addiction. Those tenants need closer management and regular inspections.

## Details

- Most of the time: families, no mental health issues, no trouble
- Occasionally: a desperate tenant with a level of mental health need or an addiction to drugs or alcohol
- If so: manage that tenant actively and do regular inspections
- A nuisance tenant disturbs the neighbours and makes you look incompetent
