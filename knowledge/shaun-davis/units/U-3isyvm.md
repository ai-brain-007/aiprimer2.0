## Description

Shaun hates paying deposits to landlords: the money is dead and it piles up fast across many properties. The student negotiates them away. It comes down to how well you get on with the landlord and how you word the ask.

## Details

- Deposits are dead money and accumulate very quickly as you scale.
- Example: someone Shaun spoke to had nearly £30,000 tied up in deposits because they did not realise avoiding them was an option.
- How: it depends on how well you get on with people, how you put yourself across, and the wording. Worded in the right way, landlords will work with you.
