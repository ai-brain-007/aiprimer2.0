## Description

This property had been run by another social housing company, and run badly. The landlord came back, was horrified by what he found, took the house back and refurbished it himself. It shows what poor management leaves behind and why looking after the property matters.

## Details

- A previous social housing company ran the property poorly
- The landlord came back to all sorts going on and was horrified
- He took the property back and refurbished it: painted the whole house, changed the fire alarm, fitted new carpets
- The author then took it on under rent to rent
