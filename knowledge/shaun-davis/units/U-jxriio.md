## Description

With a dozen small jobs across a house it is easy to forget one. Walk round with Post-it notes and stick one wherever work is needed. Peel each off when the job is done, so you can see progress and nothing gets missed.

## Details

1. After the survey, walk the property with a pad of Post-it notes.
2. Stick a note on every item the surveyor flagged: doors, windows, walls, boxing-in.
3. As each job is completed, peel the note off.
4. Do a final walk-round: any note still up is a job still open.
