## Description

Instead of leasing to the council itself, you lease your property to a housing association or to a provider the council has already approved. Many councils only work through their approved providers, so this is often the way in when the council will not take you on directly.

## Details

- Who you lease to: housing associations and council approved providers.
- Why it matters: for temporary and emergency accommodation, certain councils only work with their approved providers and will not take on a new provider directly.
- Like Local Authority Leasing, this is a back-to-back lease with zero operations: you lease the property out to the provider and they manage the tenant.
- If the council will not take new providers, ask who its current approved providers are and approach them. That question has its own card.
