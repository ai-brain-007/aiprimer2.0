## Description

Rent to rent means you lease a property from a landlord on a commercial lease or company let, pay them a guaranteed rent, and then use the property for your own business. Here that business is leasing it on to the council for temporary accommodation. It lets you build cash flow quickly without buying property.

## Details

1. Sign a commercial lease or company let with a landlord directly.
2. Offer the landlord a guaranteed rent.
3. Use the property for your own purpose.
4. In this case, lease it back out to the council and run temporary accommodation in it.

- Shaun has used rent to rent for 6 and a half years and has over 90 rent to rent social housing properties.
- He presents it as the way to build cash flow very quickly and acquire properties for the strategy.
