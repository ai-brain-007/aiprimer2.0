## Description

Know the council's exact requirements, then have the people and suppliers lined up to meet them. That means reliable tradesmen for remedial work and furniture suppliers who deliver on time, because the furnishing rules change from council to council.

## Details

1. Understand exactly what the council requires
2. Have competent and reliable tradesmen in place to do the job and any remedial work
3. Plan the furnishing: you will need to furnish the property
   - Some councils require a full set of bedroom furniture
   - Some councils only ask you to provide a bed and expect the tenant to provide the rest
4. Have good suppliers ready to deliver in a reasonable time frame
