## Description

When you take a landlord's property, you agree how long you keep it and who can walk away early. The author takes properties for three to five years with a break clause of three to six months that only he can use. The risk sits with you, so the security should too.

## Details

- Lease term with the landlord: usually 3 to 5 years.
- Break clause: typically 3 to 6 months.
- The break clause is for you only, not the landlord, because the risk is all on you.
- Why: you may have to invest in the property, and the provider or council wants security. You do not want a tenant placed and then the landlord asking for the property back after three months.
- Most landlords accept this because what they want is a long-term profitable solution.
