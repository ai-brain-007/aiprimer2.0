## Description

Go straight to property owners and tell them what you offer. Online, that means posting on social media, running ads and telling everyone what you do. Offline, it means printing leaflets and handing them out in local shops or paying someone to deliver them.

## Details

Online:
- Talk about what you do on social media, loudly and often.
- Run ads.
- Tell absolutely everyone what you do.

Offline, which Shaun says works just as well:
- Print leaflets.
- Hand them out in local shops.
- Or pay someone to go out and deliver leaflets for you.

### Also taught as: Online ads for landlords

- Keep the ad very simple
- Make the benefits very clear: features and benefits
- Remember you are solving the landlord's problem; showcase how well you solve it
