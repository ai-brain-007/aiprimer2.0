## Description

A story about buying when everyone says stop. During lockdown, Luigi kept buying deals he had lined up while people around him said to pull out. One of those deals was massively below market value and he pulled all of his money back out on the refinance.

## Details

- Context: Covid lockdown, everyone saying 'pull out of the deal, don't buy it'.
- Luigi had quit his job, owned five or six properties and was developing full time, with deals already lined up.
- He completed anyway. One lockdown deal was the best deal he has ever done: massively below market value, and he recovered all of his money on the refinance.
