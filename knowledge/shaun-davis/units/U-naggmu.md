## Description

A run-down property is a chance, not a problem. Give the landlord two options: you bring it up to a liveable standard or they do. When they cannot, you agree a deal that reflects your work. The student says there are many ways to turn a deal once you understand the model.

## Details

1. When a property is in a poor state, offer the landlord a choice: you carry out the works and bring it to a liveable standard, or they do.
2. If they are not in a position to do the works, come to an agreement. In the video this ended at £570 a month for a two-bed that returns just under £1,200.
- Principle behind it: once you understand the model there are many different ways to turn a deal.
