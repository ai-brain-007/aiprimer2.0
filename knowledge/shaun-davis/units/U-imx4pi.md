## Description

People judge you on how you look, how you speak and what they find when they search you online. Providers and landlords buy from people they know, like and trust. A clean, professional presence makes relationship building easier and deals faster.

## Details

- People will judge you on the way you look, on your brand, on how you present yourself and how you speak
- People will search you online, so you need an online presence: social media, a business page, a personal brand or a website
- You do not strictly need a website, but it looks much better
- People buy from people they know, like and trust
- A professional image and a great brand help you close more deals quickly
