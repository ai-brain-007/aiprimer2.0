## Description

A Grant Cardone quote Shaun uses as his answer to "I need money first". You do not need money to make money; you need courage, creativity and commitment. It is the mindset behind every no-money strategy in the video.

## Details

- Courage: go out there and actually get it done. Put yourself forward.
- Creativity: find a structure that works without capital instead of waiting to save.
- Commitment: the third C.
- Shaun says the quote made him realise he needed to be more forefront and remove his limitations, because every limitation is self-imposed.

## Notes

Attributed to Grant Cardone. Shaun expands on courage and creativity; commitment is only named.
