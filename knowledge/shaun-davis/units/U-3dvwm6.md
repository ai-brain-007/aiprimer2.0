## Description

The rent in social housing comes from government-backed contracts. That is the strongest reassurance you can give a landlord about being paid, and Sean uses it as his main pitch when taking on properties. Lead with it.

## Details

- The strategy is government-backed, which gives you confidence in its financial stability
- Tell landlords the same: their rent is backed by a government contract
- Use it as a massive selling point to close properties from landlords
