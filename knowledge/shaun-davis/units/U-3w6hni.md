## Description

Councils differ on whether the provider or the tenant pays the utility bills. If you do not check from day one, a deal that looked profitable may not be. Ask before you commit.

## Details

- Some councils make the provider pay the bills; others make the tenant pay
- Consequence of not checking: the difference can decide whether the deal makes a profit or not
- Fix: get a clear understanding from day one with each council
