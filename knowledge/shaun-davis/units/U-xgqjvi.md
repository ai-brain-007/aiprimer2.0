## Description

A deal where you put no money in: no deposit and no rent paid upfront. Because your investment is zero, any profit is an infinite return. Michael has never paid a deposit across his nine properties.

## Details

- No deposit and no upfront rent
- Maintenance may be split between you and the landlord
- With zero money in, the return on investment is infinite
- Michael's fourth property: no money down, £500 a month passive profit
- Michael has paid no deposit on any of his properties; Shaun has paid a deposit on about three of his
