## Description

A question to ask yourself every day: what can I do today to move the needle in my business? It sorts real progress from busy work. Shaun repeats it three times in the talk and pairs it with outsourcing, delegating and automation so you have the time to act on the answer.

## Details

1. Each day, ask yourself: what can I do today to move the needle in my business?
2. Do that, instead of being busy and procrastinating in disguise.
3. Combine it with outsourcing, delegating and working on automation so the low value tasks do not eat the day. Then the needle moves a lot quicker than if you try to do everything yourself.
- Shaun's promise: do this and you will be successful and get the results you deserve.
