## Description

The more people share a house, the more care you need over who you move in. One wrong person in a house of many can spread trouble like wildfire, and two people who trigger each other can make the whole house a nightmare. Put extra energy and focus into placement for your bigger houses.

## Details

- Bigger houses need more energy and focus on who you are moving in
- Mistake: putting the wrong person into an environment with many people. Consequence: it spreads like wildfire onto everybody else.
- Mistake: putting two people together who trigger each other. Consequence: an absolute nightmare.
- Fix: treat placement as the crucial decision for a large house. Shaun's 14-person house was working well because of the people chosen for it, and he credits the team leader for that.
