## Description

The author's three channels for finding landlords: online ads, direct-to-vendor letters using the council's HMO register, and letting agents. Each has its own approach, covered in its own card.

## Details

1. Online ads: simple, clear on features and benefits, showing how you solve the landlord's problem
2. Direct-to-vendor letters or emails: download the HMO public register from the council website for names and addresses
3. Letting agents: build face-to-face relationships; one agent gave the author 15 properties
