## Description

The model Shaun has repeated over 90 times. You take a property from a landlord, guarantee their rent, and hand the property to a council for temporary or emergency accommodation. The council pays you either a nightly rate or a lease.

## Details

1. Find the deal: build relationships with letting agents and landlords.
2. Take the property on and guarantee the rent to the landlord.
3. Give the property to the council for temporary or emergency accommodation.
4. Get paid one of two ways, depending on the council's scheme: a negotiated nightly rate per tenant, or a lease of the property to the council.
- Councils run different temporary accommodation schemes, so the deal depends on which council you work with.
