## Description

Shaun tells young people that money does not change a person, it amplifies them. An evil, greedy, broke person becomes an evil, greedy, rich one. So stay the same person, and do not take money for granted, because what is given can be taken away.

## Details

- Money fuels more of who you already are; it does not change you
- Example he gives: an evil, greedy, broke person who gets money becomes evil, greedy and rich
- His own approach: he has always stayed the same person
- Reason: God can give it to you and God can take it away, so do not take it for granted

## Notes

Said on stage to a young audience at a Rich Young Minds event.
