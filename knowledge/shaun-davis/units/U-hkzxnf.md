## Description

Landlords, agents and councils know when you do not know your subject. Learn the process end to end, ideally by watching someone do it, and your confidence shows. That confidence is what closes them.

## Details

- Know the answer to every question and how to tailor it to the scenario in front of you
- Councils and landlords can sense when you do not know, and it costs you the deal
- Seeing the process done in front of you turns a vague idea into exact steps
- Once you know it A to Z you can walk into any agent in the country and explain what you do without insecurity
