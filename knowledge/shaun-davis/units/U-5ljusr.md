## Description

An interactive game with the kids that makes the flow of money visible. One £10 note is passed around three people, each buying something the other has to sell. It shows that the same money keeps moving because everyone brought something of value to trade.

## Details

How the game ran:
1. Aaliyah starts with £10. She wants the sweets Tyrese has, so she pays him £10 for them.
2. Tyrese wants football coaching from his dad, who charges £10 for it, so he passes the £10 on. The coaching is a service of value.
3. Dad wants the Lucozade Aaliyah has, so he pays her the same £10.
4. Stop and ask what happened: the same £10 moved through three people.
5. Ask why: because each person had something of value to sell, a product (sweets, a drink) or a service (coaching), and each item cost £10.

The point: if you have a product or a service, you become part of the money's journey instead of watching it.

## Notes

The transcript spells Lucozade as "Lucas aid", "Lucasade" and "Lucas head". The game can be replayed with any three people and any three items at one price.
