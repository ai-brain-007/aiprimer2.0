## Description

Everyone wants passive income and the freedom that comes with it. Shaun says people go wrong by not being willing to put the work in first. Have the goal of passive income, but build an active income first, and keep working even after the freedom arrives.

## Details

- The goal of time and location freedom is fine; Shaun says definitely have it.
- The mistake: wanting the freedom without being willing to do the work first.
- The order: build an active income first, put the work in, then aim for passive income.
- Shaun still puts the work in now that he has time and location freedom.
- It takes resilience to keep going through this stage.
