## Description

Back-to-back leasing works with any provider, but choose the ones that need many properties fast. Temporary and emergency accommodation providers have high turnover and constant demand, so one relationship can scale. A children's home may give you one property and take a year to get ready.

## Details

- Temporary and emergency accommodation: high-level demand, high turnover, they usually need a lot of properties, and it is fast. This is where scaling makes sense.
- Children's home or care home: you might get one property, and it takes ages to get ready, possibly a year. All the work of finding the provider and the property, with no end goal beyond that one deal.
- Rule: pick providers that are willing to scale and need volume.
