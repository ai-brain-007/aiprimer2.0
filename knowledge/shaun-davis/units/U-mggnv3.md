## Description

Sean's order of play when he sits down with a landlord. Build rapport until they like and trust you, make them believe that you believe in what you sell, get them to lay out their pain points, then present your service as the solution and spell out the benefits: government backed, guaranteed rent, no hassle.

## Details

1. Build rapport before anything else. Doing business is about them understanding you, liking you, trusting you and having the confidence that you are the right person for the job. Sean cannot teach you how to find common ground because every person has different interests: find out theirs and relate them back to you so you are relatable.
2. Get them to believe in you. People do not need to believe what you are saying; they need to believe that you believe it. That shows up as conviction, passion and enthusiasm, and enthusiasm sells.
3. Help the landlord see your service as the solution to their problem. Start by extracting their pain points: what has their experience in property been, why are they now interested in social housing, why is it a viable option for them now. Once they have laid out their pain points, sell your service as the solution to those points.
4. Tell them the viable benefits: what social housing is, how it works, that it is government backed and that they get guaranteed rent. Most landlords want a profitable, zero-hassle solution, so show how your service provides exactly that.

Sean points to a filmed challenge where he lived with a stranger, Sandu, for a week and got him his first deal from start to finish, as the in-depth version of this process.
