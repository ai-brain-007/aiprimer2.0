## Description

If you have no properties yet, do not hide it. Tell the landlord they would be your first and show that you will fight for this property because everything depends on it. Landlords who see that tenacity are more likely to back you.

## Details

- Be open and honest: this is my first property
- Do not be afraid to say the landlord would be your first
- Turn it into a strength: this is a property you will have to die for, and they can see that
- Landlords who see that tenacity are more likely to buy into you
