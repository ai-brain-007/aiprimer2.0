## Description

Sean built his property business while working in the motor trade with a single day off a week, Tuesday. That day was for massive action on the right things. It shows how to treat limited time as the constraint it is.

## Details

- Sean was working in the motor trade with one day off a week: Tuesdays.
- On Tuesdays he went all out taking massive action, but on the right things, the things that move the needle, not on being busy.
- Because his time was so limited, he had a VA do the groundwork during the week so that Tuesday was packed with high-value tasks.
- His point for you: if you have a job or other commitments, you do not have infinite time. Time is a limited resource, so plan for it.

## Notes

Transcript says 'multi trade'; Sean says elsewhere in the video that he was in the motor trade.
