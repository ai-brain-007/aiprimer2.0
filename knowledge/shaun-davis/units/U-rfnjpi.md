## Description

A hot provider is one that has just won a government-backed contract and now has to fill it. They have the funding and a deadline, so they are far more likely to want your properties than a random name from Google. Finding hot providers is the whole point of the Contracts Finder method.

## Details

- Definition: a provider that is actively looking to scale, typically because it has just closed a government-backed contract
- Why they are hot: the contract has an end date, so they want to fulfil it quickly; they need a lot of properties; the funding is already there
- Where to find them: awarded contracts on Contracts Finder (gov.uk/contractsfinder)
- What to do with them: contact them, find out whether they are looking to scale, and offer your properties as the solution
