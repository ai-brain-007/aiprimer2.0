## Description

Shaun tells parents the best thing they can do for their children is to go after their own dreams in front of them. Kids forget what you say but never forget what you did. Seeing you fail and get back up teaches them the character they will need.

## Details

- Chase your own dreams; that is the best thing you can do for your kids
- Kids forget what you say but not what you did; actions speak louder than words
- It is okay to fail in front of them, because failure builds character
- What they remember: dad or mum failing, getting back up and trying again
- His own childhood memory: his dad failing, getting back up and saying this is my dream
