## Description

Six years ago Shaun would have refused to speak in front of ten people: too nervous, sweaty palms, nothing to say. Now he speaks all over the UK every month. He did not train for it as a goal; he grew into it through the process of building his business. It is his proof that who you become matters more than what you get.

## Details

- Then: asked to do a public speaking engagement for about 10 people, he would have said absolutely not. Too nervous, would not know what to say, sweaty palms.
- Now, six years later: public speaking engagements all around the UK, literally every month.
- How: growth through the process of building the business, not a separate target.
- Lesson he draws: if you want to have more, you need to become more.
