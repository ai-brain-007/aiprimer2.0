## Description

You do not need trading history to become a provider. The author says councils do not care whether you have turned over millions or just started; they look at the value you bring and whether you pass the fraud checks.

## Details

- Common worry: I have a new business with no turnover, will the council take me seriously?
- The author's answer: yes
- Turnover does not matter; what matters is the value you can bring to the council
- The checks are about fraud on you and the directors, not about company size
