## Description

Ask the council for the surveyor's checklist before the inspection and walk the property ticking every item. It is the only way the author knows to pass first time and get paid without delay. Failing the survey wastes time while the landlord waits.

## Details

- Request the surveyor checklist before the survey happens
- Walk the property with paper and pen and tick the items off one by one
- Aim: pass straight away, onboard the property and get it into payout
- Why: the landlord or agent is waiting to close; you want to look competent and not waste time
