## Description

Before he started, Shaun told himself the game would take at least five years to understand. Because he expected a long, bumpy road, every early failure felt like part of the plan instead of a reason to quit. Setting that expectation up front is what made it easy to get back up.

## Details

1. Before you get into the game, decide that it will take at least five years, and probably more.
2. Use those five years to understand the game, how it moves and the marketplace, to move correctly and to build your skillset.
3. When you fail and stumble at the start, tell yourself: this is what is meant to happen. Shaun failed a lot and calls it an emotional roller coaster.
4. Because you expected it, it becomes much easier to pick yourself back up and carry on.
