## Description

When something goes wrong, swap one question for another. Instead of asking why this is happening to you, ask what this is teaching you. The first question keeps you stuck as a victim; the second makes you look for the lesson that is right in front of you.

## Details

- Do not say: why is this happening to me?
- Say instead: what is this teaching me?
- Then try to learn from it and take accountability for your part in it.
- The speaker says this is what he says to himself, and gives it as his final point on handling the dark moments.
