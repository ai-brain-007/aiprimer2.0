## Description

When a provider or landlord calls and nobody picks up, they assume that is the service they will get when something goes wrong. A staffed phone is part of your professional image. Use a VA or a call answering service.

## Details

- Unanswered calls make a provider or landlord think the worst about how you will respond to future issues
- Make sure someone is there whenever the phone rings
- Options: a full-time VA; if the VA is busy, a call answering service
