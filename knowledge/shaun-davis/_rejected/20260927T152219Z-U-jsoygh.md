## Description

This is the frame of the whole video: seven habits Shaun Davis credits for becoming a property millionaire. They are all about mindset rather than deals or numbers. Use the list as a checklist of what to work on before you chase your first property.

## Details

- Habit 1: working on yourself
- Habit 2: staying a student to the game
- Habit 3: building relationships
- Habit 4: taking action before you are ready
- Habit 5: staying consistent when results are slow
- Habit 6: protecting your environment and your energy
- Habit 7: thinking long-term

He calls them habits, tips and principles in turn, and once calls habit five a step. His claim is that when the habits and the mindset are right, the rest falls into place.

## Notes

The author says he kept the video short on purpose; each habit is a few minutes of talk, not a full method.
