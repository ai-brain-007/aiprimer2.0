## Description

Luigi's rule, which Shaun called a gem: find out what recalibrates your mind, and when stress hits, stop. Working stressed is neither productive nor healthy. Do something else, or simply switch to a different task and return to the stressful one later.

## Details

1. Learn what recalibrates you. For Shaun it is the gym; for Luigi it is the gym, the drums or a conversation.
2. When you feel stressed, stop working. Do not push through, because work done under stress is not productive and is not healthy.
3. Do the thing that resets you, then come back.
4. Lighter version: you may not need to stop working altogether. Stop the specific task that is stressing you, do a different task, and come back to the first one later. Luigi says this always works for business owners.

## Notes

Told by Luigi; Shaun said he would adopt it.
